    Events

    Winter 2026 Events

    Connect, learn, and have fun with fellow students who share your international experience. Attend our events and workshops to stay informed, acquire practical skills, and build lasting friendships. 

    Follow us on Eventbrite to receive notifications about our upcoming events!

    • Waterloo WAT Click this tab to view content pertaining to Waterloo
    • Brantford BRANT Click this tab to view content pertaining to Brantford

    Winter 2026

     

    Snow Globe Making

    Tuesday, January 20 | 1 - 2 p.m. |  P111 | Drop-in while supplies last

    We're bringing a little winter magic to campus! Join us to make your own snow globe, where you can design a mini snow globe to take home. All supplies will be provided. 

    This event is in partnership with Student Experience. 

    International Snacks Night

    Wednesday, January 21 | 4 - 6 p.m. |  P111 | Registration encouraged

    Craving something tasty and unique? Join us for International Snacks Night, where you can sample delicious treats from around the world! Test your knowledge with our “Guess the Country by Emojis” game and see how well you know global snacks and cultures. 

     

    Make a Snowball 

    Wednesday, January 28 | 4 - 5:30p.m. |  Departure from Laurier International, 2A01 | Registration encouraged

    Get ready to channel your inner snow artist! Whether you want to craft snow ducks, bricks, or classic snowballs, we’ve got the tools for you. Plus, hand warmers and hot beverages to keep your fingers from freezing mid-masterpiece. 

    Camp Kawartha 

    Friday, January 30 - Sunday, February 1 | Departure at 1p.m. |  Camp Kawartha | Registration required

    Experience cross-country skiing, snowshoeing, hiking, a campfire and other activities in the great Canadian outdoors! 

    Make and Take Mason Jar Meals

    Tuesday, February 3 | 3 - 4p.m. |  P119 | Registration required

    Join us to make a mason jar meal and collect a free $25 grocery gift card! We will connect and learn about food, nutrition, food security, meal preparation, and resources on campus. You will take away a meal with you to help you stay nourished! There will be vegan, gluten-free, and halal options available.  

    This event is in partnership with Wellness Education. 

    Embrace Your Culture Night

    Saturday, February 7 | 5 - 10p.m. |  Athletic Complex | Registration required

    Join us for a night of celebrating what makes each of us unique at the next home Golden Hawks Basketball Game! 

    Lunar New Year Celebration

    Thursday, February 12 | 2 - 4p.m. |  Concourse 

    Submit your recipe here by Jan. 31st and get a chance to win 2 free meal vouchers to the dining hall! 

    Food & Grocery Tour to the Kitchener Market

    Saturday, February 28| 10a.m. - 1p.m. |  Departure from Laurier International, 2A01 | Registration encouraged

    Explore the local Kitchener Market on a food and grocery tour that highlights local vendors, fresh produce, and global flavours. Don’t forget to bring your OneCard and Cash!
