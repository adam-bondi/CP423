    2023 Dean's Honour Roll

    Undergraduate students registered in a degree-seeking program will be eligible for Dean’s Honour Roll if they have achieved a minimum overall GPA of 10 (A-) in all courses taken from Sept. 1 to Aug. 31.

    Assessment for the dean’s honour roll will be completed in the following fall term by the faculty in which the student was most recently registered.
