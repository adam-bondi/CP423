    Application Process Now Open for 2026

    March 4, 2026
    Print | PDF

    The application deadline for fall 2026 admission is Friday, September 4, 2026. We do not accept applications after the deadline.

    You must be enrolled full-time in second year as of September to be eligible to apply. (If you are seeking admission to UXD Co-op or to PEP, you are eligible if you will be enrolled full-time in third year in September.) Double degree students at Laurier and Waterloo will be enrolled automatically; double degree students at Laurier only should also apply by the deadline to indicate your intention to participate in co-op.

    Application Process

    We are now accepting applications for fall 2026 admission.

    If you are applying to a program with a competitive admission process, you need to achieve a minimum GPA in first year to be offered an admission interview. For information about grades and course credit requirements, go to admission requirements and scroll down to your program.

    Go to application review for information about scheduling an admission interview, submitting a resume, and preparing for your interview.

    Application Webinars

    View a webinar to learn more about your program, and about the application process.

    Faculty of Arts

    If you are a first-year student in Honours Arts (in one of these majors), you may view a webinar about applying in 2026 to Faculty of Arts Co-op. 

    Faculty of Science

    If you are a first-year student in Honours Science (in one of these majors), you may view a webinar about applying in 2026 to Science Co-op. 

    Business and Economics

    If you are a first-year student in Honours Business or Economics, you may view three webinars about applying in 2026 to Business and Economics Co-op. 

    1. How to Apply to Business and Economics Co-op  

    2. About Business and Economics Co-op 

    3. Co-op Admission Interview  

    Business Technology Management

    If you are a first-year student in Business Technology Management, you may view a webinar about enrolling in 2026 in BTM Co-op. 

    Computer Science, Data Science, PEP

    If you are in student in Computer Science or Data Science, please view this webinar about applying in 2026 to Computer Science Co-op or Data Science Co-op or PEP. 

    Geography and Environmental Studies

    If you are a student in Geography or Environmental Studies, please view this webinar about applying in 2026 to Geography Co-op or Environmental Studies Co-op. 

    Double Degree at Laurier: Business and Computer Science, Business and Financial Mathematics

    If you are a first-year student in a double degree program at Laurier, you may view a webinar about enrolling in co-op in 2026 for BBA/Computer Science or BBA/Financial Mathematics. 

    Double Degree at Laurier and Waterloo: Business and Computer Science, Business and Mathematics

    If you are a first-year student in a double degree program at Laurier and Waterloo, you may view a webinar about enrolling in co-op in 2026 for BBA/Computer Science or BBA/Mathematics. 

    User Experience Design

    If you are a second-year student in UXD, you may view a webinar about applying to Applying to User Experience Design Co-op in 2026.
