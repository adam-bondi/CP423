    Services

    We offer distinct service areas to meet your needs (for self-serve printing use PrintMe): 

    • For printing, copies, small posters, coil binding, buttons, custom mugs ... go to Hawk Hub
    • For large posters, order online at Hawk Print. Note: if your poster project or conference poster is funded order here and include the course name, professor and department index for payment. All other posters need to be pre-paid by credit card.
    • For other requests, view our catalog and order online at Hawk Print
    • Don't see something you're looking for? email hawkprint@wlu.ca and we can provide an estimate and add it to our catalog.

    Poster Printing

    Hawk Print (formerly Laurier Printing Services) is the main print centre at the university at 230 Regina St.,Waterloo, and offers wide format printing services as well as complex printing and bindery services.

    This location is open Monday to Friday from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., and closed for weekends and university holidays. 

    Poster Printing

    We request a minimum of 5 business days during peak times.  

    1. Submit your files and pre-pay online 
    2. Select pick-up location: 
      • Waterloo: Hawk Print – 230 Regina St. N.
      • Brantford 
      • Hawk Express Locker pick-up is not recommended for large posters - put a note in special request if needed and we will make special arrangements
