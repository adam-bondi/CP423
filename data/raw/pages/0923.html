    Campus Safety

    Your safety is our highest priority.

    It's important to familiarize yourself with our emergency procedures and opt-in to receiving alters from Laurier's Special Constable Service (SCS).

    Sign Up to Receive Alerts

    SCS will send safety alerts using a number of communication channels, including: email, SAFEHawk app, text messages and desktop notifications to Laurier computers. Here are some ways that you can make sure you receive these time-sensitive alerts: 

    • Opt-in to receive SMS alert messages. 
    • Download the SAFEHawk app and enable notifications.

    Residence and Off-Campus Housing

    Whether you're living in residence, off-campus, or arriving from abroad, Laurier offers support to help you feel at home.

    • Residence Life: First-year students can apply for residence to access academic support and build lasting friendships. Learn about move-in, residence guidelines, and how to learn where you live.
    • Off-Campus Living: Stay connected with campus life while living off-campus. Find housing resources, connect with roommates, and get involved through LOCUS.

    Mail Services

    Amazon Lockers

    Amazon Lockers are available for the Waterloo campus 24 hours a day, Monday to Sunday, providing you a method of picking up your Amazon parcels in a secured and convenient location.

    To place an order to be delivered to the lockers:

    1. Within your order, under "Shipping Address," select "or pick up near this address."
    2. Select "Pick-up here" at the following location:
      Amazon Hub Locker - Clovis
      Wilfrid Laurier University, 75 University Avenue West, Waterloo, ON, N2L 3C5

    North-west corner of the Dr. Alvin Woods Building (DAWB), across from the Schlegel Centre. Take the path along the side of the building where the lockers are located under an overhang.
