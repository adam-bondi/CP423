    The Library is Here to Help

    The Library is much more than just a place to study. It is an academic hub for research and learning and a space for community building and creative expression. Using the Library improves student learning and success!

    Highlights


    Locations - The Library has four locations:
    • Waterloo
    • Brantford (Grand River Hall)
    • Kitchener (Lyle S. Hallman Faculty of Social Work).
    • Milton (Laurier Milton Academic Centre)

    Study - If you need to study, set up in one of the individual carrels or work with friends in a group study room or presentation practice room.

    Course Readings – Find your assigned readings for your course online using Ares

    Resources - The Library provides access to nearly two million electronic and print books as well as thousands of electronic resources, including databases, journals and streaming media. The Library is home to the University’s archives, music and film studies collections. Through Omni, you can search and access print resources of Laurier plus 18 other Ontario universities.

    Do Better Research - Don’t know where to start with your research? Every faculty, program and campus at Laurier has a designated Librarian to help students. Find your Librarian here on the Library’s website and contact them for assistance. 

    Print/Scan/Copy - You can print, scan or copy anything you need at the Library.

    Work with Us! - The Library is one of the largest student employers on campus, offering both paid and volunteer positions. Students will gain valuable work experience and income. More information on student employment.
