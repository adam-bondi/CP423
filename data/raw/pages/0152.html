    You can find all the essential details regarding our upcoming convocation, including the dates and times of the ceremonies and guest information. 

    You can verify your ceremony time and date under 'Upcoming Ceremonies'  on the Student Profile of LORIS.

    Ceremony Exceptions

    Students with Combined Majors

    Students with combined majors will be assigned a ceremony based on their first alphabetical major.

    • Faculty of Arts: Women and Gender Studies is a combined major that will be awarded at each of the Faculty of Arts ceremonies under the first major.
    • Graduate Studies students will be aligned with the respective undergraduate programs.
    • Double degree students (BBA/BA and BBA/BSc) have the option to choose which faculty ceremony they wish to attend. Both degree parchments will be presented at their chosen ceremony.

    Exception Requests

    Students must graduate with members of their faculty. For unique exceptions, written requests must be submitted to convocation@wlu.ca prior to September 1 (Fall Convocation) and April 1 (Spring Convocation). Venue constraints and other variables will be taken into consideration in reviewing such requests.

    Guest Information

    Tickets

    All ceremonies are ticketed events. Guest tickets can only be claimed by the graduating student.

    For more information about tickets and accommodation requests, see the Tickets page. 

    Day of Ceremony 

    Find all the guest information you need about attending your student's ceremony such as when you should arrive, where you should park, where you can ask questions, and more. 

    Livestream the Ceremony

    For guests who are unable to attend the ceremony in person, a livestream on Laurier Live will be available approximately 15 minutes prior to the ceremony start time.
