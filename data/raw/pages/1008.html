    Get Involved with Sustainability

    To truly make Laurier a thriving sustainable campus we need students like you to help raise awareness, take charge, and create a culture of sustainability.

    We offer a variety of ways to get involved, stay informed, and help guide the direction of sustainability initiatives on our campuses. Whether you are passionate about sustainability or just starting to take interest, we welcome all our undergraduate and graduate students.

    Add Sustainability to Your Laurier Experience

    We offer a combination of short- and long-term opportunities, some of which are paid positions, others are funding opportunities, some are clubs and groups, and some are volunteer-/participation-based and can be used to add to your Laurier Experience Record (LER).

    By providing a range of ways to get involved with varying time commitments, we can better accommodate your busy schedule and make this a positive, informative experience for you.

    Stay Informed

    The best way to find out about new opportunities is to stay connected. All of our events, new programs, council positions, partnership opportunities, and job postings will be announced in the Sustainability Office newsletter and in our social media.

    Sign up for our monthly newsletter.

    Follow us on Instagram, Facebook, and X.

    We also welcome any inquiries you may have about existing programs or new ideas. You can connect with us directly at sustainability@wlu.ca.

    Join Our Team! 

    Each semester, the Sustainability Office hires a co-op student to support the work of the sustainability officers and part time students to support individual projects or initiatives. Students from all academic backgrounds are encouraged to apply.

    Positions

    These positions can provide experience in communications, project planning and implementation, feasibility, student engagement, and data analysis.

    Our student roles include: 

    • Sustainability Projects Assistant (co-op) 
    • Sustainability Programs Assistant  
    • Sustainability Advisor (Waterloo campus) 
    • Sustainability and Social Change Advisor (Brantford campus) 
    • Urban Agriculture Assistant 

    Our Partnerships

    We have also partnered with other internal departments to support sustainability focused student roles: 

    • Dean of Students Office 
    • Department of Residence 
    • Graduate Students’ Association 
    • Grounds Services 
    • Indigenous Student Centre 

    Create Your Own Project

    Interested in working on a sustainability project of your own? The Sustainable Hawk Fund awards up to $30,000 each year to students, staff, and faculty (up to $5,000 per project) to help bring your ideas to fruition on campus. Since its inception in 2015, the Sustainability Office has awarded over $180,000 to over 55 projects, some of which have since been adopted as programs by Laurier.

    Laurier also offers a variety of programs designed to provide research, logistical, and technical support for students looking to create projects on campus. Whether it’s providing guidance and training, access to equipment, or connecting you to faculty or community groups, Laurier is dedicated to providing experiential learning opportunities to enhance your academic experience.

    Check out the following resources to see how we can support you:

    • Laurier Hub for Community Solutions
    • LaunchPad Brantford
    • Library Makerspace
    • Science Maker Lab
    • StartUp Lab

    Engage with Likeminded Students

    Connecting with likeminded students to promote sustainability and create initiatives on campus is a great way to demonstrate your school spirit and contribute to culture change on campus. Student-run clubs, groups, and councils help create dialogue on issues, advocate for change, and build networks. They provide peer-to-peer support, and they are always looking for new members! These groups offer positions for those who are looking to lead and those who are looking to participate.

    • EcoHawks (Brantford)
    • EcoHawks (Waterloo)
    • Enactus (Waterloo)
    • ESG Laurier (Waterloo)
    • Laurier for the SDGs (Waterloo)
    • Laurier Social Justice Club (Waterloo)
    • Sustainability in Business (SIB) (Waterloo)
    • Sustainability Council (Waterloo and Brantford)
