    Residence Life

    The Residence Life team is committed to engaging students as valued members of the Laurier community, and is focused on creating opportunities for students to positively contribute to their own growth. Residence Life at Laurier has always been characterized by mutual respect, and works diligently to promote healthy and caring communities.

    Residence Life Area Coordinators (RLACs) facilitate the day-to-day management of the buildings, work to maintain Laurier’s standards of community living, provide individual advising and train and supervise the Residence Life Dons. There is one RLAC on call to respond to emergencies at all times.

    The Residence Life Managers and the Associate Director oversee the RLACs and coordinate the overall administrative, supervisory, developmental and student conduct related functions in the Residence Life program.

    The Manager, Residence Student Conduct oversees the student conduct team responsible for the development of the Residence Conduct Curriculum and day-to-day administration of all conduct processes.

    Residence Education

    The Residence Education team is responsible for developing and coordinating initiatives that promote student learning and academic success for 3,500 students living in residence buildings on our Brantford and Waterloo campuses. Examples include Residence Learning Communities, Learning Clusters, a residential curriculum and service integration. Such services include academic advising and career coaching, the First Year Leadership Program, community-based leadership education and residence-wide experiential learning opportunities that connect residents to the broader community and support Laurier’s educational mandate.

    Facility Operations

    Residence Facility Operations is responsible for the upkeep and safety of the university residence buildings. This includes building repairs, renovations, capital projects and ensuring that work is completed in a timely fashion.

    Contact: residence_facilities@wlu.ca 

    Administrative Services

    Residence Administrative Services is responsible for all administrative processes related to residence applications and room assignments. The staff is also available to assist you with the replacement of lost/broken keys, and mail delivery within the residences.

    • Waterloo: housing@wlu.ca 
    • Brantford: brantfordresidence@wlu.ca
