    Welcome Days for incoming students living off campus

    Print | PDF

    Save the dates! You won't want to miss it! 

    Waterloo Campus: Saturday, September 5th, 2026

    Brantford Campus: Sunday, September 6th, 2026

     

    As part of Laurier Orientation, LOCUS is excited to welcome first-year off-campus students attending our Brantford and Waterloo campuses to our Off-Campus Welcome Day! 

    We want to make sure your first day on campus as a Golden Hawk is filled with opportunities to meet other first-year students and learn about life at Laurier. At the event, students will be able to connect with their upper-year mentors (Off-Campus Advisors and Icebreakers) for Orientation and the year ahead. 

    Early bird registration is now open and will close on August 21st. The last day to register for the event is September 2nd. Click here to register today.

    *Early bird registration will enter you for a chance to win a Laurier prize pack!

    Email locus@wlu.ca if you have any questions

    Attending the Milton campus? Since all our Milton students are off-campus, the Off-Campus Welcome Day programs are included throughout the Laurier Orientation schedule. Be sure to check out all the events!

    An overview of the Off-Campus Welcome Day schedules for the Brantford and Waterloo campuses can be viewed below. Students should refer to the LOCUS section of the Laurier Orientation website for full event details.
