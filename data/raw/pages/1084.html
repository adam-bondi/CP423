    Classrooms

    If you’re looking to book a classroom, Laurier has a space to suit your needs.

    All rooms and space on campus must be booked using the Room Booking Form, with the exception of some rooms.

    Learn how to operate classroom technology or report problems in classrooms as you're using our spaces as well.

    Technology in Centrally Bookable Classrooms

    Standard Hardware

    • projector or display
    • AV control panel
    • computer with Windows 11
    • guest laptop connections (HDMI, USB-C)

    Standard Software

    • Windows 11
    • Microsoft Office 365 (Word, Powerpoint, Excel)
    • Internet Explorer / Google Chrome / Firefox
    • Windows Media Player, RealPlayer, QuickTime, VideoLAN, Cyberlink PowerDVD

    For information about operating classroom technology, please consult the Classroom Orientation Guides.

    Please contact the ICT ServiceDesk at support@mylaurier.ca for technology support in classrooms.

    The Laurier community is encourage to help us maintain our spaces by returning classrooms to their original configuration, leaving technology working and ensuring spaces are left tidy.

    Web Conferencing Classrooms

The room's cameras and microphones connect into the classroom computer and instructors can start a Zoom session from the console computer at any time.
