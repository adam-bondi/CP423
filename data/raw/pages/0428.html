    Registration Webinars

    Course registration for first-year students starting Fall 2026 opened on June 29 at 10 a.m. 

    Course registration can be a confusing first step in your preparation for Laurier. Between required courses, electives, labs and tutorials, there are a lot of options to consider when creating your first Laurier class schedule.

    Our Laurier 101 course registration webinar will guide you through the process of reviewing your program requirements, evaluating your elective options, and developing a class schedule that works for you.

    After watching the webinar recording, if you have additional questions, please contact us at laurier101@wlu.ca

    Incoming Student Course Registration Webinars

    Course Registration Webinars are meant to be general, meaning students can attend any one of the three options. The webinars will cover material such as:

    • An introduction to the Course Registration Guide.
    • A demonstration of how to use the Visual Schedule Builder.
    • A demonstration of how to complete your registration on LORIS.
    • An overview of common LORIS errors.

    The below Demonstration Videos are quick step by step registration support for incoming undergraduate students that complement the full Course Registration Playlist.

    Need Extra Help?

    For any registration troubleshooting questions, email us (laurier101@wlu.ca) from your MyLaurier student email and include:

    • your full name,
    • nine-digit student ID, and
    • screenshots if applicable.
