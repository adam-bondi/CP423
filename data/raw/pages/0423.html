    Connect With Science Academic Advising

    Print | PDF

    Find Your Academic Advisor

    The Science Academic Advising Team (N1054) provides general academic support to all Faculty of Science students.   

    Department Undergraduate Officers are experts in their discipline and program / course content. 

    Select your program of registration for information on your assigned Academic Advisor and Department Undergraduate Officer.

    Ways to Connect Virtually

    Webform (replaced direct email)

    For any general academic advising-related inquiries, complete the  Faculty of Science Academic Advising Inquiry form to initiate a request for information and support. For 2026/27 registration-related inquiries, the Faculty of Science Registration Inquiry form may be used to initiate a request for advising support, as needed.

    Completed forms are forwarded to the Faculty of Science Academic Advising office (Science Building, N1054). The received forms are reviewed during university office hours, Monday to Friday from 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.  You will receive a response to your myLaurier email account from your Academic Advisor, Department Undergraduate Officer or advising support staff within 1-5 business days (depending on the type of inquiry and volume of requests).

    Virtual Booked Appointment

    Virtual appointments are available with your assigned Faculty of Science academic advisor for more complex circumstances. Complete the Faculty of Science Academic Advising Inquiry form to initiate an appointment request.

    Note that:

    • Booked appointments will not be available during the busy registration period, June 29 - July 17, 2026
    • Each advisor has virtual appointments, in either the morning or afternoon, three days/week.
    • Appointments are 15 minutes maximum virtually through Teams.

    Ways to Connect In Person

    In-Person Reception Desk Inquiry

    Our administrative assistants can assist you with brief general inquiries, forms and procedural information. 

    Connect with us Monday to Friday from 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. in the Science Academic Advising Office, N1054, noting the office is closed for lunch from 12 noon to 1:00 p.m.

    In Person Booked Appointment

    In person appointments are available with your assigned Faculty of Science academic advisor. Complete the Faculty of Science Academic Advising Inquiry form to initiate an appointment request.

    Note that:

    • Booked appointments will not be available during the busy registration period, June 29 - July 17, 2026
    • Each advisor has in person appointments, once/week (Monday through Wednesday).
    • Appointments are 15 minutes maximum.

    Drop-in appointments

    These appointments with a Faculty of Science Academic Advisor are offered on specific dates during busy advising periods (usually the beginning of the term).
