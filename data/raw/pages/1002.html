    Using Your OneCard

    Meal Plans and Meal Plan Changes

    • Waterloo WAT Click this tab to view content pertaining to Waterloo
    • Brantford BRANT Click this tab to view content pertaining to Brantford

    Waterloo residence students will receive a mandatory meal plan of either Dorm or Apartment style. These plans are non-refundable and non-transferrable.

    Plan Changes and Deadlines

    Meal plan change requests can be emailed to mealplanrequest@wlu.ca after you receive your invoice for tuition etc. until Sept. 30. On Oct 1, all residence meal plans lock in for the academic year and cannot be changed. The plan locked in will be received again for the winter semester.

    Requests for change must come from the student account holder only, using their Mylaurier email account. Included, we require your full name and student id number. It is strongly advised that you try your initial plan for a couple weeks before requesting a change.

    Dorm vs. Apartment Plans, and Upgrading

    If you are assigned dorm residence you will have a choice between a 7-day or 5-day dorm plan by default. If you are assigned an apartment residence, you will have a choice to select the 7 or 5-day dorm plan or the apartment plan. Dorm students cannot request an apartment plan. Your selection must be indicated on your residence application. Please note that meal plan upgrade requests are accepted throughout the academic year.

    Meal plan balances of flex dollars will Carry Forward and remain available for use at all the same participating food and beverage providers for as long as you are an active registered student. Dorm access to the Dining Hall-Fresh Food Company expires at the end of the semester purchased.

    Meal plan balances cannot be used for Bookstore purchases or printing. For more information, see the Deposits, Balances and Refunds page.

    Campus Convenience Dollar Venues

    You can use your convenience dollars anywhere OneCard is accepted as long as you are an enrolled Laurier student. Accounts with no activity for 24 months will render the account to be deactivated and not refundable. Convenience is the ONLY account accepted at the following locations.

    • The Bookstore
    • International News
    • The Wellness Centre
    • Snack vending machines on campus
    • Printing with PaperCut
    • The Hub-printing services

    Convenience Plans

    As a Brantford student in residence, Convenience plans are optional. You have three options. All three plan costs are per term (fall/winter). The following costs are based on the 2025/26 academic year.

    • Welcome plan: $480.00
    • Study plan: $565.00
    • Comfort plan: $680.00

    You can add more money to your OneCard at anytime.

    Bus Pass

    • Brantford Transit website

    • Brantford BRANT Click this tab to view content pertaining to Brantford

    The bus pass is automatic for full-time students only (2.0 or more credits per term). The bus pass can now be opted into for part time students. It is not administered by the OneCard office, but is online at: web.wlu.ca/feeoptouts This is available during the same add/drop period each semester. Any questions should be directed to Service Laurier.

    You will see the bus pass fee on your LORIS account for the fall and winter terms. 

    If you have further questions, consult with the Students' Union.

    For information on bus pass options available for purchase, visit the GRT website.

    Students registered at the Brantford campus have access to the Brantford Transit services during the academic year with their OneCard.
