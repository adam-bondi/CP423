    Interested in Being an LSIF Mentor?

    Many of our alumni serve as mentors for our current student analysts in LSIF. Mentors are matched with students on one-to-one basis. Mentors recommend a stock for a student to investigate and provide feedback to student analysts in addition to the review provided by the faculty supervisors.

    If you are interested in being an LSIF mentor, please email bsmith@wlu.ca or ntang@wlu.ca.
