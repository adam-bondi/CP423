    Student Affairs

    Student Affairs is here to support you in every part of your student experience, both inside and outside the classroom. 

    From getting involved on campus, to finding support when you need it, we're your go-to resource for finding your community, succeeding in your academics, prioritizing your wellness and growing as an individual.

    Getting Extra Care

    If you're ever faced with extenuating circumstances impacting your academic or personal success during your time at Laurier, the Dean of Students: Care and Support team can provide coordinated support to help you navigate through it.

    Resources to Help You Thrive

    Laurier has many resources to support your academic and personal well-being. Each department offers specialized supports to meet your individual needs.

    Wellness and Support

    Explore the supports available to help you prioritize your well-being at Laurier.

    • Childcare Services: Supporting student-parents with childcare options.
    • International Student Support: Personalized support from immigration advising to academic assistance.
    • Student Wellness Centre: Take care of your mental and physical wellness.

    Academic Supports

    Helping you navigate your coursework and enhance your learning. 

    • Accessible Learning: Academic accommodations to support students with disabilities.
    • Student Success: Numeracy, writing and study skills support.

    Campus Services

    Get the most out of your time at Laurier with services that support your growth, career and community life. 

    • Athletics and Recreation: Stay active and connected through sports and fitness.
    • Career Centre: Plan your future with career advising, job search help, and more.
    • Residence and Off-Campus Housing: Supports to help make Laurier your home away from home.

    Our Commitment to Make Your Student Experience a Priority

    Welcome, Golden Hawks!

    We’re so glad you’re here! From the moment you accept your offer all the way to crossing the stage at convocation, our Student Affairs experts are here to support you every step of your journey.

    At Laurier, we believe your student experience is more than just your academics – it’s about getting involved, finding your community then helping to build it up for others, and discovering who you are. That’s why we offer a wide variety of resources, connections, and experiences to help make your time here both meaningful and rewarding.

    Within the Student Affairs division, we have numerous teams here to support you: Accessible Learning, Athletics and Recreation, Career Centre, Dean of Students: Care and Support, International Student Support, Off-Campus Initiatives, Residence Life, Student Experience, Student Success, and Student Wellness Centre. I encourage you to take advantage of all the resources designed with you in mind, say “yes” to new opportunities that energize you, and challenge yourself both in and outside of the classroom.

    We can’t wait to see you learn, lead, and love your time at Laurier.

    Stay golden,

    Kate McCrae Bristol
    Vice President, Student Affairs

    Student Organizations

    Get involved. Find your people. Make your mark.

    Wilfrid Laurier University Students' Union

    As an undergraduate student at Laurier, you're automatically a member of the Wilfrid Laurier University Students' Union. Funded by your student fees, they exist to support your university experience through clubs you can join, services, events and advocacy.

    It’s your space to get involved, grow, and make the most of your time at Laurier.

    Graduate Students' Association

    The Graduate Students’ Association (GSA) is the official voice of all master's and PhD students at Laurier. With a focus on student leadership, the GSA proudly represents its diverse membership and seeks to enhance the vibrancy of the graduate student community. They support the graduate student experience through programming, services, grants and bursaries, and representation on committees.

    You Are Not Alone. Reach Out.

    You’re not alone on your journey at Laurier. There is a whole network of people and resources here to support you, whenever you need it.
