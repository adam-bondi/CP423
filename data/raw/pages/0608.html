    Evaluation Criteria and Introductory Statement for applicants to UX Design Co-op

    Please read the following statement carefully before your co-op admission interview

    You will receive a link to the Introductory Statement and Form via email. Complete the statement and form only once. 

    Before you begin

    We are providing the contents of the form so you know what to expect because the form will time out after one hour of inactivity. To help you prepare, see the sample introductory form.

    Ensure you are ready to complete the entire form, provide employment history details, and upload your resume. Please do not submit the form more than once. 

    During the interview

    The interview will last about 15 minutes. You will have an opportunity to verify the information you have entered in the form during your admission interview.  Although we require a resume, we consider only what you tell us during the interview when evaluating your suitability for admission to co-op. We urge you to answer each question fully, and to provide examples from your own experience. 

    Interview location:

    Your Admission interview will take place as a video call over Microsoft Teams. Please arrange to have access to the internet and a device with a webcam and microphone. We will provide a link to information on using Microsoft Teams. 

    Evaluation Categories

    • Paid work experience in the last three years
    • Volunteer service and extracurricular activities in the last three years
    • Academic record in your two years at Laurier
    • Performance during the interview
    • Your portfolio will also be assessed by UX Design faculty, and their feedback will be integrated into this category.

    Your interview is your only opportunity to tell us about your experience in each category. We will give equal weight to each category.

    Your interview is your only opportunity to tell us about your work experience, as we use your resumé for reference only.

    We will evaluate the work you performed during the last three years. (For most UX Design students, this will be from the beginning of grade 12 to the end of Orientation Week in third year.) We want to hear about your duties and assignments in each position. We especially value jobs in offices or professional environments, and positions with leadership or responsibility.

    When comparing candidates, we give higher standing to students who have worked full-time for the past three summers than to those who have worked less consistently. Be prepared to tell us how many hours you worked each week for each job, what months and years you worked, and how it would be of interest to a co-op employer. 

    If you volunteered in a non-profit agency during the last three years, please tell us about that experience when we ask about volunteer service and extracurricular activities during the admission interview.

    If you were not employed at some point in the last three years, please be prepared to tell us how you used your time. 

    When comparing you with other candidates, we consider both the quality and quantity of your work experience.

    Quality of Work Experience

    During your admission interview, tell us about your duties and assignments in each position. We especially value jobs in offices or professional environments, and supervisory roles or positions with significant responsibility.

    Quantity of Work Experience

    We give higher standing to students who have worked in paid jobs than to those who have worked in unpaid jobs. We anticipate that all students to whom we offer admission will have had paid work experience.

    We will consider your volunteer service and extracurricular activities at school and in the community over the past three years. For most students, this will be from the beginning of grade 12 to the end of Orientation Week in third year at Laurier.

    What We Expect

    Please list your roles and tell us about any significant contributions you made. We will consider all activities, but we tend to value leadership roles more highly than general membership. Also, we tend to value varsity sports more highly than intramurals.

    Your interview is your only opportunity to tell us about your volunteer service and extracurricular activities.

    When evaluating your interview performance, we will consider the quality of the content and delivery of your answers. Your ability to communicate your skills and accomplishments is a key component of the interview.

    When telling us how you have developed specific skills, please use examples from all experiences including academic case studies with external clients.

    We will also consider your ability to be concise and stay within the 15 minutes allotted.

    At the end of the admission interview, we will ask you to tell us anything you wish to add in support of your application. Be sure to tell us about any accomplishments or experience you have not had an opportunity to mention earlier, including how you used your time if you were unable to secure employment during the last three summers.

    Once you have been approved to schedule an admission interview, you will be required to submit a portfolio by September 8, 2026.  Your portfolio will be assessed by UX faculty, and they will provide feedback, which will be integrated into the interview performance category of the evaluation criteria.

    Verifying Information

    We expect you to present your skills and experience truthfully and factually. We reserve the right to verify information you provided.  Any students who have misrepresented themselves will be ineligible for enrolment in the co-op program. We reserve the right to review your academic status and history with the Faculty of Liberal Arts.

    Admission Decisions

    We notify all candidates of our admission decision by email. If you receive an offer of admission, you will also receive instructions on accepting the offer by the deadline. 

    User Experience Design Co-op

    • Late afternoon on Friday, September 25, 2026 we will send a message to your Laurier email address to let you know whether we are offering you admission to co-op.
    • The deadline for accepting an offer of admission to UX Design Co-op is 10 a.m. on Monday, September 28, 2026.

    1. Please review the introductory statement below. (It includes an explanation of your admission interview.)
    2. Confirm that you have read the introductory statement and verify that the information on your resumé is truthful; see “Confirm and Verify” below.
    3. If you are an international student, please let us know of any period when you were not eligible to work in Canada; see “Eligibility” below.
    4. Upload your resumé.
