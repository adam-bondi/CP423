    Tax Season is here!

    Feb. 19, 2026
    Print | PDF

    Dollars and Sense: Money Management for Students annually hosts Free Tax Clinics on campus to help with students who are seeking additional help to file their taxes. This is open to Domestic and International Students and registered through Community Volunteer Income Tax Program (CVITP) through CRA. If you are interested in volunteering for this event you can still sign up! 

    Need Help Filing Taxes? 

    Waterloo Campus Free Tax Clinic

    Get your 2025 personal taxes prepared by trained Community Volunteer Income Tax Program (CVITP) volunteers for FREE! Free Tax Clinics are for Simple Tax Returns and you can find out if this is the right space for you. 

    *Please check our required form and bring your documents on the day to file your taxes. 

    • Wednesday March 18, Thursday March 19 and Friday March 20
      • Time: 12 p.m. to 4:00 p.m.
      • Location: Senate and Board Room, Paul Martin Centre and DAWB 2-104
      • Sign up for your spot here!
    • New! Friday March 27 
      • Time : 12 p.m to 6:00 p.m
      • Location: BA110 and BA111
      • Sign up here or walk in during our open hours.  

    Brantford Campus Free Tax Clinic

    Please check our required form and bring your documents on the day to file your taxes. 

    • Saturday April 11 and Saturday April 18 - Dates are subject to change.
      • Time: 10 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.
      • Location: One Market 2nd floor Student Commons Kitchenette 
      • Sign up for your spot here!

    Want to know more on how to file your Taxes? 

    Tax 101 session with WealthSimple Foundation 

    If you want to learn more about filing your income tax, join and learn to file one. Our current partner WealthSimple Foundation will help guide you to file your student tax returns. Join in with your laptop and learn. Please note that this is not a tax filing session but focuses on what to know for your tax season.

    • Virtual Session Wednesday, March 25
      • Time: 6 p.m. to 7:00 p.m.
      • Register here and also win prizes from the session!
