    Dining on Campus

    The Food Services team at Laurier is here to keep you fueled and nourished with a variety of dining options across our campuses. The food options in and around campus meet diverse tastes with flexible hours and convenient payment options. 

    Whether you're grabbing a coffee between classes, enjoying a meal with friends, or catering an event, Laurier Food Services is here to support your campus experience.

    Explore our hours of operation, meal plan options and don’t hesitate to reach out to us at with any questions.

    Hours and Locations 

    From early morning coffee runs to late-night bites, there is a wide range of dining options to suit your schedule and cravings.

    Waterloo students: Find menu, locations and hours on the Campus Dish website. 

    Brantford students: Find food vendor menus and hours on their respective websites. 

    Meal Plans

    We offer flexible meal plans designed to meet the diverse needs of Laurier students. Whether you're living on campus or commuting, any student can purchase a meal plan.

    All students living in residence are required to have either a mandatory meal plan or a convenience plan as part of their housing agreement.

    Meal Plan Changes and Balances

    You may request a change to your meal plan until Sept. 30 by emailing mealplanrequest@wlu.ca. After this date, plans are locked in for the academic year.

    Your meal plan is valid at the start of the fall term and available to use until 24 hours after residence move-out day at the end of the winter term.

    Meal plans are non-refundable and non-transferable. Remaining OneCard Flex Dollar balances as of April 30 will roll over to the next academic year.

    Guests

    Your friends and family are welcome at the Fresh Food Company. You can purchase guest meal passes or pay as you go. 

    About the Meal Plans

    Residence meal plans are subject to HST exemptions as outlined by Revenue Canada. Students in residence with mandatory meal plans will not be charged tax on on-campus food purchases.

    In partnership with the Wilfrid Laurier University Students’ Union, all residence students contribute a levy through their meal plan. These funds support capital upgrades to enhance campus dining experiences.

    Fresh Food Company

    The Fresh Food Company provides flexibility to have a quick snack or a full meal to suit any schedule:

    • Led by a team of chefs and features an all-you-care-to-enjoy rotating menu with a focus on quality, convenience and ultimate flexibility.
    • Provides a variety of options such as stone-fired pizza, stirfry, salad bar, deli, grill, sushi and more.
    • Accommodates special dietary needs such as allergies or food intolerances as a variety of alternative options are available.

    We ask that large bags such as backpacks and gym bags be placed in a locker at the entrance.

    • You may use a personal lock to secure your locker.
    • We are not responsible for lost or stolen items. 
    • All personal property found at the end of each day within the dining hall will be handed to Special Constable Services.

    Order Membership

    Your membership will automatically be applied to your OneCard convenience account. To order a membership: 

    1. Go to dining.wlu.ca. 
    2. Create a new account. 
    3. Select Meal Plans.
    4. Select your plan and add to card. Follow the payment prompts. 
    5. Print your receipt and visit the OneCard office to obtain your membership benefits.

    Using Your Membership

    The Dining Club Membership is a separate account from a meal plan account. 

    • Convenience dollars are accepted everywhere the OneCard is accepted and they never expire.
    • Can be used at any designated food locations in Waterloo, in addition to International News, Hawk Print and the Hawk Shop.
    • To request a refund, contact the OneCard office.

    Brantford Meal Plans

    Students living in residence may choose to purchase a convenience plan (optional). 

    The following three plans are available. 

    • Comfort plan: $680 per term. 
    • Study plan: $565 per term. 
    • Welcome plan: $480 per term.

    Convenience Dollar Deposit and Balances

    To purchase a convenience plance, you can deposit funds onto your OneCard.

    Convenience dollars do not expire at the end of the term and will continue to be available beyond first year.
