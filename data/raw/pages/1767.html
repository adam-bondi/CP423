    Undergraduate Student Orientation and Transition

    Incoming Undergraduate Student Supports

    Laurier 101 is a transition program designed to help new undergraduate Golden Hawks feel prepared, supported, and excited for their Laurier journey. It offers exclusive content for students and their supporters to get ready for life inside and outside the classroom.

    Before classes begin, students will:

    • Receive guidance on course registration.
    • Learn strategies to help navigate university-level learning.
    • Have opportunities to connect with peers and campus.
    • Learn about campus resources and supports.
    • Discover what it means to be part of the Laurier community.

    Explore Laurier 101 Fall-Start Supports and Programs

    Fall Undergraduate Student Orientation

    Orientation welcomes new Golden Hawks with engaging events that introduce them to Laurier’s community, supports and traditions.

    New Golden Hawks will have opportunities to:

    • Connect with fellow Golden Hawks.
    • Explore campus.
    • Interact with key campus resources and supports.
    • Participate in can’t miss activities.

    Graduate Student Orientation and Transition

    Incoming Graduate Student Support

    ASPIRE for incoming graduate students is a transition program designed to help new graduate students feel prepared, supported, and confident as they begin their Laurier journey. Through webinars, workshops, checklists, and events, new graduate students will:

    • Learn strategies to help navigate graduate-level learning.
    • Have opportunities to connect with peers, staff, and faculty.
    • Learn about campus resources and supports.
    • Discover what it means to be part of the Laurier community.

    Explore ASPIRE Incoming Graduate Student Supports

    Graduate Student Orientation

    Graduate Student Orientation (GSO) welcomes new Laurier graduate students into the vibrant and supportive Laurier community.

    New Golden Hawks will have opportunities to:

    • Connect with fellow Golden Hawks.
    • Meet staff and faculty.
    • Connect with campus resources and supports.
    • Learn how to navigate campus and campus systems.

    Finding Your Way Around Our Campuses

    Getting oriented is one of the first steps to feeling at home at Laurier! Our interactive campus maps make it easy to explore the Brantford and Waterloo campuses, whether you’re heading to your first lecture, tracking down student supports and services, or finding new study spots. Use these maps to navigate key buildings, accessible routes, and campus landmarks, so you can spend less time searching and more time settling into life as a Golden Hawk!
