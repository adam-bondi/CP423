    Convocation and Graduation

    Your #forevergolden Moment

    Graduation is the golden moment you've been working towards during your time at Laurier.

    Graduation refers to successfully completing the degree/diploma requirements of your program.  Convocation is a ceremony at which graduates are celebrated. It is a ceremony to celebrate the completion of your hard work and an opportunity to celebrate your success with your friends and family. 

    You are required to apply to graduate regardless of whether you intend to participate in the scheduled ceremony events. 

    Spring 2026 Ceremonies

    Laurier will host spring 2026 convocation ceremonies
    June 9 in Brantford and June 15-19 in Waterloo.

    Find Your Ceremony Details

    Your Guide to Graduation

    You must apply to graduate regardless of whether you intend to participate in the scheduled ceremony event.

    Step One:
    Apply to Graduate

    Once you apply to graduate, you'll be sent further information about the scheduled ceremony event.

    Step Two:
    Stay Connected

    Frequently check your mylaurier inbox for important information from the Graduation and Convocation Planning Team for your next steps. You'll receive an invite to your ceremony and instructions on how to claim tickets. You can also consult our Convocation ceremony checklist.

    Step Three:
    Receive your Degree

    Degrees are presented at your convocation ceremony or available after convocation.

    Be Our Guest

    Find all you need to know about being a guest at convocation, such as ceremony locations, where to park and how to obtain tickets on our guest information page.

    If you cannot attend the ceremony in person, you can stream the ceremony on Laurier Live.

    Photography & Videography

    Yearbook and Class Composite photos are managed by WLU Student Publications. Please contact them directly with inquiries.

    Candid ceremony photography will be posted on Flickr in the days following the ceremony.

    Not all ceremonies will have a photographer present for candid photos. Graduates and guests are encouraged to attend the post ceremony reception and utilize one of the many photo displays provided to capture the special day.

    Following convocation, each graduate will receive an email that will include their personalized stage crossing moment!

    Contact Us

    Have any questions about convocation and graduation? 

    Connect with us by email at convocation@wlu.ca. 

    Looking Ahead

    Our Spring 2027 ceremonies will take place June 8 in Brantford and June 14-18 in Waterloo. Students who have applied to graduate can expect a ceremony invitation to their @mylaurier.ca email mid-spring for spring ceremonies, or late summer for fall. 

    Please note that these dates are subject to change.
