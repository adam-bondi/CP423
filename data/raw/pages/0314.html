    Academic Skills (ASk)

    The Academic Skills (ASk) Certificate is open to all students and is designed to support your academic success. It includes a mix of workshops and one-on-one study skills appointments.

    To earn your certificate, you need to complete five ASk activities in one semester:

    • At least four different workshops
    • Study skills appointments

    By completing the ASk Certificate, you’ll build strong academic habits and add a valuable achievement to your Laurier Experience Record.

    If you have any questions, please contact studentsuccess@wlu.ca


    Workshop Format

    ASk workshops are offered both virtually and in person.

    For virtual workshops:
    Click the Zoom link to join using your Laurier Zoom account.

    Important:
    To qualify for the ASk Certificate and receive Laurier Experience Record credit, you must attend and stay engaged for the entire session—whether it’s online or in person.

    Workshop topics include:

    • Mastering Time Management
    • Goal Setting and Habit Forming
    • Regular Review: Study Cycle
    • Writing 101
    • Notetaking 101
    • Ace Your Online Course
    • Study Smarter, Not Longer
    • Multiple Choice Mastery
    • Silencing Digital Distractions
    • Stop Procrastinating Now!
    • I Failed, Now What?
    • Optimize Your Brain Power
    • Final Countdown: Four Weeks Left
    • Preparing for Final Exams
