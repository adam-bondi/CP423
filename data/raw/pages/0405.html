    Online Public Safety Programs Advising

    Online Public Safety program academic advising is available to help you navigate your academic journey from acceptance of your program offer through to graduation. This may include, but is not limited to:

    • Developing your academic plan
    • Exploring the optimal program for you, including minors and options
    • Verifying that you are meeting program requirements in terms of course completion and grades
    • Clarifying academic regulations, policies, and procedures
    • Discussing academic challenges
    • The petition process for exceptions to academic regulations
    • Suggesting additional resources for enhanced academic and personal well-being

    We are committed to providing high-quality, inclusive, and confidential academic support to undergraduate and graduate students. Regular contact with an academic advisor is recommended and encouraged.

    Virtual and Phone Appointment Guidelines

    These guidelines help us make sure appointments run smoothly and that all students have fair access to advising.

    1. Appointment Scheduling:  Appointments can be booked up to 7 days in advance and are designed to be 20 minutes long so we can support as many students as possible.
    2. Registration Questions: Registration inquiries are best handled by email. If an appointment is booked for registration help, we’ll cancel it and direct you to the appropriate email support.
    3. Cancellations: If you can’t attend, please cancel using the link in your confirmation email or email publicsafetyadvising@wlu.ca.
    4. Grace Period: We allow a 5‑minute grace period. If you join later than that, the appointment will need to be marked as a no‑show.
    5. No‑Show Policy: Missing three appointments without notice, or having an appointment ended due to policy concerns, will result in a one‑year pause on virtual and phone appointments. 
    6. Seasonal Adjustments: During our busiest times (January and July), virtual and phone appointments may be paused so we can focus on essential administrative tasks.

    What We Ask of You as a Student

    These expectations help ensure your appointment is productive.

    1. Preparation: Arrive ready to engage, with an understanding of your program requirements and any questions you’d like to discuss.
    2. Punctuality: Your advisor will call you at the scheduled time. Please be logged into Microsoft Teams or near your phone and ready to begin.
    3. Professional Attire: Dress as you would for an in‑person meeting on campus.
    4. Technology & Environment: Use a reliable desktop or laptop in a quiet, private indoor space. If you’re in a shared area, headphones help maintain privacy.
    5. Mobile Device Limitations: Teams works on mobile, but some features (like screen‑sharing) may not function fully, which can limit the support we can provide.
    6. Professional Conduct: To ensure a focused conversation, advisors may end appointments if you’re driving, walking, on transit, or otherwise unable to participate fully. Ended appointments count as no‑shows.

    What You Can Expect from Your Advisor

    We’re committed to offering clear, supportive, and accurate guidance.

    1. Timeliness: Your advisor will begin the meeting or call right at the scheduled time.
    2. Focused Support: You’ll receive personalized guidance on your academic record, program requirements, and progress toward graduation.
    3. Accurate Information: Advisors will share up‑to‑date information about prerequisites, policies, and university regulations.
    4. Follow‑Up Notes: A summary of your appointment will be emailed to your mylaurier.ca account within 24 hours.

    Complete the Graduation Check Form once you have fully registered for both your fall and winter terms to receive confirmation that you have the correct courses to meet your graduation requirements. If you make changes to your schedule after submitting the form, please resubmit the form or email us to alert us to your changes.
