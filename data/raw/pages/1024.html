    • Fee Including Tax: $28.67/week; $57.46/month

    Carpool Parking 

    • Reserved carpool space only
    • Fee Including Tax: $233.60 /8 months incl. tax per person (Sept. 1 - April 30) 
    • Carpool parking restrictions; minimum two people registered at all times

    Motorcycle/Scooter/Moped Parking 

    • Valid May 1 – Oct. 31 only
    • Fee Including Tax: $80.23 for six months
    • Parking permitted in designated spaces only

    Residence Parking for Spring/Summer Term (24h Parking)

     Permit Colour: Blue (King St. Garage)

    • Fee Including Tax: $333.86 / 4 months
    • Effective Date: May 1 to August 31, 2025

     Permit Colour: Red/Green

    • Fee Including Tax: $206.35 for four months
    • Effective Date: May 1 to August 31, 2025

     Temporary Parking Permits

    Note: Temporary residence parking permit prices are listed without tax (tax exempt); if not living in residence, HST is applicable.

    Temporary permits are available at the Parking and Transportation Office based on availability. License plate information must be provided at time of purchase. The cost for a lost/stolen permit is $50.

    Temporary parking permits are not available in white lots. If you require parking due to a temporary disability, see accessible parking for information.

    The costs below are in effect as of Sept. 1st, 2026

    Red or Green Surface parking Lots;

    • One Day (24 hours): $16.00
    • One Week: $30.00
    • One Month: $70.00

    Blue and Green garage parking lots

    • One day: $20.20
    • One Week: $38.00
    • One Month: $86.00
