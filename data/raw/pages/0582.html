    Start Your Journey

    We believe in #everyoneachangemaker. There are many ways you can get involved – from attending an event, to volunteering on an initiative, to even starting your own world-changing venture. Ready to leap in?

    Learn Try Do

    Track your changemaking journey on Laurier’s Experience Record to document your changemaking skills and experience for employers and graduate schools.

    Learn

    To start on your changemaking journey, the first step is to build your changemaking toolkit.  

    Take the Ashoka Changemaker Index assessment to reveal your strengths and areas for growth as a changemaker. Remember everyone can be a changemaker, you just have to want to make a difference in the world. 

    Then join our hands-on workshops to learn how to think, build, and lead like a changemaker - turning your ideas into tangible solutions to real-world challenges. 

    Try

    To truly become a changemaker, you’ll need to get your feet wet and try out your changemaking skills. Through the Changemakers Hub you’ll find countless opportunities to do just that while connecting with other innovators and entrepreneurial thinkers who are passionate about building a better world.  

    Throughout the year, you’ll find a range of activities and events designed for you to experience what changemaking looks like in action – to experiment, test new ideas, and get creative while making impact. Check out the Changemakers Events calendar to see what’s coming up and get involved today! 

    Do 

     

    Once you’ve explored your changemaking skills, it’s time to put them into action and build something real. The Changemakers Hub supports you in taking the next step - whether that’s joining an existing social enterprise or launching your own.

    You’ll have access to mentorship, tools, and a community of peers who are committed to turning bold ideas into meaningful solutions. From early-stage concepts to ready-to-grow ventures, the Hub helps you shape your impact and bring your vision to life.

    Build a Better World

    Check out the UN Sustainable Development Goals below to find your passion and what you can do today to get involved and start making a difference. Meet and connect with Laurier changemakers who are making an impact and who share your vision for a better world.
