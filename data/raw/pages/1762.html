    Music Programs at Laurier

    Your success is our passion. Our Faculty of Music programs prepare you for a lifetime in music, with skills to open doors as your career unfolds.

    Our staff and faculty are always available to help with questions about your academics or career planning.

    Supports and Resources

    Find some areas of support that you may find useful throughout your studies:

    • Music advising: Connect with your advisor to help you set and achieve your academic goals. 
    • Accessible learning: Register with Accessible Learning to access academic accommodations for students with disabilities (or suspected disabilities).
    • Ways for graduate students to fund music conferences: We recognize the importance of showcasing your methodologies and innovative performance practices, so we’ve rounded up some ways that you can apply for funding to offset the costs of attending conferences.
    • Scholarships and bursaries: Apply for scholarships and bursaries to help with the costs of postsecondary education.

    Your Music Student Experience

    We've rounded up some resources that can enhance your studies in the Faculty of Music. 

    • Concert calendar: Attend our concerts throughout the year to take a break from your studies and become inspired by and learn from peers and top international performers. 
    • Studio instructor request form: Request the studio instructor you'd like to study with. 
    • Join a QuartetFest workshop: This intensive chamber music program is hosted in partnership with the Penderecki String Quartet and the Kitchener-Waterloo Chamber Music Society.
    • Piano Lab: The purpose of this intensive Spring term Piano Lab is to master weaker areas in students’ playing by working on them exclusively and intensively for the duration of the tutorial
    • Voice Lab: This unique workshop, tailored for undergraduate and graduate-level singers, offers an invaluable opportunity to delve deeper into vocal studies beyond the standard fall and winter terms.
