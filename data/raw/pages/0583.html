    Lisa Jarvis

    Associate Director, Community and Workplace Partnerships
    E: ljarvis@wlu.ca
    P: 548.889.3962
    Office: CWP202, 12 Lodge St., Waterloo

    I work with, students, faculty, staff, employers and community members to develop and expand opportunities for experiential learning (EL) within the curriculum at Laurier. Our team ensures that EL placements and projects, and various work experiences in all faculties and campuses build community capacity, support student learning through linking in-class learning with experiential learning, facilitate the ability for students to meet learning outcomes and support faculty in using pedagogies shown to be effective for work-integrated and community-engaged learning within their courses.

    I provide support, oversight, and leadership in areas such as risk management, program evaluation, departmental communication and marketing, and financial reporting for our unit.

    I am a proud grad of the MSW program at Laurier (’06) and came to Laurier as a Toronto co-op coordinator in 2009. I have been in the role of Manager, Community and Workplace Partnerships since the unit was created in 2017. My background includes a combination of professional social work experience, community development and community based research, teaching, practicum coordination and supervision, international service learning and co-operative education.

    I have stayed close to my roots in social work over the past number of years teaching in the Social Service Worker program at Seneca College, doing community-based research for EAFWR, and as a member of the L’Arche Daybreak community. I am a mother of four and we are FamilyHome Providers through EAFWR sharing our home with an individual who has been a part of our family since 2014.

    Kwame Sarpong

    Coordinator, Workplace Partnerships
    E: ksarpong@wlu.ca
    P: 548.889.3603
    Office: CWP203, 12 Lodge St., Waterloo

    I collaborate with faculty, community organizations, and businesses to facilitate experiential learning opportunities. This may involve students working on projects within the classroom for partner organizations or engaging in fieldwork to interact with others. My role is to strike a balance between the content in courses, the interests of our partner organizations, and the learning outcomes of our students. My portfolio primarily comprises business marketing and kinesiology courses.

    I have been employed at Laurier since 2020, initially working in Accessible Learning. In 2023, I transitioned to the Community and Workplace Partnerships team. I earned a Bachelor of Kinesiology and a Master of Science from the University of Toronto before starting a career in post-secondary education. My experience includes working with students as a learning advisor, accommodations consultant, and currently, as a Workplace Partnerships Coordinator.

    My favourite part of this role is seeing students positively impact our industry partners. The students produce amazing work that provides significant value and insights to our partner organizations. Additionally, these experiences contribute to the confidence necessary for our students as they transition into their professional careers.

    E: tvanherzele@wlu.ca

    I work directly with faculty, community organizations, and students to ensure all are supported throughout an experiential model of engagement.

    • Support with curricular development related to experiential learning, including model implementation, reflection practices, and professional development sessions.
    • Assistance with partner acquisition and relationship management.

    Community Support

    • Collaboration to explore engagement opportunities that align with organizational needs and timelines.

    Student Support

    • Guidance on course-specific requirements, hour tracking, self-initiated placements, and support with the Navigator platform.

While completing my master’s degree, I was elected as the Graduate Students’ Association representative on the Board of Governors for a two-year term. As a coordinator with the CWP team, I’ve been able to apply my passion for education and the student experience in new and exciting ways each term.

    Sara Darling

    Coordinator, Community Service-Learning
    E: sdarling@wlu.ca
    P: 548.889.3974
    Office: OM-206-49, Brantford

    As Community Service-Learning Coordinator at Wilfrid Laurier University’s Brantford campus, I support the integration of community-engaged learning into academic courses. My role includes three parts- collaborating with faculty to embed community experiences into their courses, developing partnerships with external community organizations, and facilitating student participation through placements and projects with our partners.  These course-based experiential learning opportunities connect classroom learning with real-world experience.  Students apply course concepts in community settings through meaningful engagement while also learning from community partners, creating mutual benefit for both students and community partners.

    I joined Wilfrid Laurier University in 2015 as the Community Service-Learning Coordinator for the Brantford campus. I hold a Master of Education (2025) from Wilfrid Laurier University, a Bachelor of Arts in Human Services (2012) from Athabasca University, and a diploma in Early Childhood Education (1997) from Mohawk College.  My career began in the field of early learning, both as an Early Childhood Educator and then ten years at the Ontario Early Years Centre: Brant, serving as Early Literacy Specialist and Volunteer Coordinator. During this time, I built partnerships and developed deep knowledge of the local community.  It was here that I discovered my passion for community partnerships and working together to meet common goals, which I have carried with me to my work at Laurier.

    With my keen interest in community-building, I can often be found at events on campus that bring people together, whether that is in formal or informal settings. I have had the pleasure of meeting people from all over campus. In the words of Helen Keller, "Alone we can do so little, together we can do so much."

    Shannon Pennington

    Coordinator, Community Service-Learning
    E: spennington@wlu.ca
    P: 548.889.3979
    Office: CWP206, 12 Lodge St., Waterloo

    As a community service-learning (CSL) coordinator, I strive to provide personal and responsive service to students, community partners and faculty and facilitate meaningful connections between them. To do so, I consult with faculty about their course curriculum and outcomes and integrate those with experiential learning opportunities for their students. I work with external community partners to explore their needs and generate appropriate and meaningful projects and placements that provide real-world connections to curricular content in academic courses for students. I coordinate placements/project opportunities for students in CSL courses, prepare them for the experiences, monitor their performance, and facilitate critical reflection components throughout the experience.

    I have been a CSL coordinator for more than 10 years. Prior to this I was a biology lab instructor at Laurier and an outdoor educator with the local public school board and conservation authority. My undergraduate degree is in biology and geography, from Laurier and my Master's degree is in Environmental Studies, from UW.
