    Career and Employment Support

    Get Started

    Rely on the Career Centre for career and employment support. We provide you with the programs and resources to help you be engaged in career development and experiential learning as an integral part of your student experience.

    Our Career Centre Team

    We are qualified career and further education consultants, employer relations specialists and student staff who are committed to your career development and success.

    Engage Now

    Navigator is your portal to access Career Centre resources, job postings, events and appointment bookings. It is updated daily with new opportunities and offerings. Connect with the Career Centre today and make the most of your time at Laurier!
