    Studying and Academics

    • Virtual Course Fee: $37.00/half-credit course

    The Virtual Course fee is charged for each course that is offered through a virtual asynchronous (VA), virtual synchronous (VS) or virtual hybrid (VH) model of delivery. This fee is assessed to recover a portion of the overall costs of services and supports. This includes live support for students and instructors during virtual assessments, support resources for students to navigate virtual learning environments, licensing and fees associated with specialized virtual learning technologies, and design processes to create and maintain dynamic and engaging learning experiences.

    If a student drops a virtual course, the university will adjust all or a portion of the fee on a pro-rated basis, per the academic calendar.

    Contact: VAlearning@wlu.ca
