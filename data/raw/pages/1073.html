    Apply for Residence

    Online applications to live in residence on our Brantford and Waterloo campuses for the 2026/27 academic year (September to April) have launched!

    Medical and Disability-Related Accommodations

    Complete and submit the Residence Disability/Medical Accommodation Request Application 2026-2027, together with supporting documentation, by June 8 at 4:00 p.m. if: 

    • You require accommodations in residence based upon a documented disability or medical condition. 
    • You have a nut allergy and would like to request placement in a nut-free environment.

    This application is available in the online residence portal.

    Within this application is a Disability/Medical Accommodation Supporting Documentation Form that you will need to download and have completed by your health care professional. Once completed, you will upload this form back into the Residence Disability/Medical Accommodation Request Application prior to submitting the application.

    The Department of Residence will use this information to determine what accommodations need to be provided when finalizing your residence placement.

    First-Year Undergraduate Students

    Rooms are assigned through a lottery based on preferences indicated on your residence application, and room availability. We do everything we can to ensure that students sharing rooms are compatible – that’s why the application form asks about your personal habits and preferences.

    If you have questions about the room assignment process, contact us at housing@wlu.ca before submitting your residence application.

    If you'd like to share a room with a friend, we'll try to accommodate your request. 

    When filling out your residence applications, form a roommate group with your friend and be sure that you follow all the roommate group rules outlined in the application.

    Your residence contract is for the eight-month academic year, meaning you're required to stay in residence from September 2026 to April 2027, as outlined in the Residence Agreement.

    In completing the residence application, you will be agreeing to the terms and conditions.

    Cancel Residence Application

    lf you wish to cancel your residence application: 

    1. Send an email to housing@wlu.ca.
    2. Your message must include your full name and nine-digit Laurier ID.

    The $600 residence deposit will be refunded to students who submit a cancellation request by 11:59 p.m. on June 1, 2026. Students who choose to cancel after the June 1 deadline are not eligible to receive a refund of the deposit.

    Leaving Residence

    If you choose to leave residence before the end of the agreed-to occupancy period while still attending Laurier, you are responsible for the full fees for your assigned residence room for the entire occupancy period.

    Moving In

    Your residence room assignment and important move-in information will be emailed to your mylaurier.ca email in late July.

    Moving Out

    You must vacate your residence room 24 hours after your last scheduled exam in the winter term.

    Senior, Transfer and Graduate Students

    Typically, students who have previously attended another postsecondary institution are considered senior students for residence purposes. Contact us with questions about your eligibility to apply for residence.

    At the Brantford campus, the Department of Residence is excited to announce we are offering two upper year residence communities!

    If you are interested in applying for a space in one of these communities, email brantfordresidence@wlu.ca.

    At the Waterloo campus, all of our residence spaces are reserved for first-year undergraduate students.

    Residence will be full managing the residence guarantee for first-year students. As such, we will not have sufficient vacancy to consider applications from those students not eligible for Laurier's first-year student residence guarantee.

    Only first-year students eligible for the guarantee will be accommodated in residence, and any waiting lists will be available to first-year students only.

    If you are a senior or graduate student with a significant documented disability and can access the learning environment at Laurier only by living in residence, you may be eligible for a residence placement. If this applies to you, email housing@wlu.ca as soon as possible, and prior to June 1, to discuss your eligibility for residence.

    Exchange Students

    Accommodations for exchange students are single rooms within an apartment-style residence. A limited number of beds are reserved.

    Application 

    Submit your completed residence application by: 

    • Fall term: June 1, 2026 at 11:59 p.m.
    • Winter term: TBD
    • Full year: June 1, 2026 at 11:59 p.m.

    Deposit

    The residence deposit is waived for exchange students. You are not required to submit the $600 deposit with your application.

    Your residence contract is for either four or eight months, depending on the application you complete, as outlined within the Residence Agreement.

    • Fall term: September to December 2026.
    • Winter term: January to April 2027.
    • Full year: September 2026 to April 2027.

    In completing the residence application, you agree to these terms and conditions.

    Cancel Residence Application

    lf you wish to cancel your residence application: 

    1. Send an email to housing@wlu.ca.
    2. Your message must include your full name and nine-digit Laurier ID.
    3. Your residence cancellation request must be received by 11:59 p.m. on June 1, 2026.

    Leaving Residence

    If you are successful in obtaining a space in residence, you must stay in residence for the entire period of time for which you have applied.

    If you choose to leave residence before the end of the agreed-to occupancy period, you are still responsible for the full fees for your assigned residence room for the entire occupancy period.

    You are strongly advised to consider the financial implications of this decision before accepting the terms of the Residence Agreement and committing to live in residence. Some exchange students arranged private accommodations in the community, and prefer to live there.

    Moving In

    The Department of Residence will email you to advise if you were successful in receiving a space in residence (mid-July for fall term and full year students; late-November for winter term students).

    Your residence room assignment and important move-in information will be emailed to your mylaurier.ca email (early August for fall term and full year students; early December for winter term students), including details for key pick-up.

    Moving Out

    You must vacate your residence room 24 hours after your last scheduled exam in December for fall term students and in April for full year and winter term students.

    Residence Guarantee Disclaimer

    While Laurier University endeavours to honour its residence guarantee, the university will not be liable for any failure or delay in providing residential accommodation when such failure arises out of any cause or causes beyond its reasonable control.

    Such causes may include but are not limited to fire, strike, lock-out, inability to procure materials or trades, war, mass-casualty event, flood, local, regional or global outbreak of disease or other public health emergency, social distancing or quarantine restriction, legislative or regulatory requirements, severe weather event, failure of public utility or common carrier, or attacks or other malicious act, including but not limited to cyber attacks on or through the internet, or any internet service, telecommunications provider or hosting facility (an “Emergency” or the “Emergencies”).

    The university will use reasonable efforts to provide advance notice to students if an Emergency prevents it from following through with the residence guarantee. There shall be no compensation in any form whatsoever as a result of Emergencies or other circumstances which are beyond the university’s control.
