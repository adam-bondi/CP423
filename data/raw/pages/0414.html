    Science Academic Advising

    We are Here to Support You! 

    We are committed to providing high-quality, inclusive, comfortable, and confidential academic support to undergraduate students. Regular contact with an academic advisor is recommended and encouraged! You can connect with Science Academic Advising through a variety of methods. 

    Academic Advising Question?

    For any academic advising-related inquiries, complete the Faculty of Science Academic Advising Inquiry form to initiate a request for information and support. This form replaces direct email to the Faculty of Science Academic Advising office or any Department Undergraduate Officer.

    Completed forms are forwarded to the Faculty of Science Academic Advising office (Science Building, N1054). The received forms are reviewed during university office hours, Monday to Friday from 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. 

    Who are My Academic Advisors?

    The Science Academic Advising Team (N1054) provides general academic support to all Faculty of Science students.

    Department Undergraduate Officers are experts in their discipline and program / course content. 

    Faculty of Science undergraduate academic advising is available to help you navigate your academic journey from acceptance of your program offer through to graduation.  This may include, but is not limited to:

    • clarifying academic goals
    • degree program requirements
    • course options
    • GPA and progression requirements
    • explaining academic procedures, policies, and regulations.

    Select your program of registration below for information on your assigned Academic Advisor and Department Undergraduate Officer.

    2026/27 Registration Question?

    Many registration questions/concerns can be resolved by students themselves, using the available registration resources. Refer to the course registration guide for general information about how to register and select your courses. You can also refer to Faculty of Science-specific course registration resources:  

    • “Why Can’t I Register In…”: Registration Restrictions
    • Registration tips for first-year Mathematics courses
    • Selecting Electives: First-year Faculty of Science students
    • Electives, Minors and Options: Senior Faculty of Science Students
    • Pre-requisite information for first-year Chemistry courses

    For any 2026/27 registration questions, complete the Faculty of Science Registration Inquiry form to initiate a request for advising support. This form replaces direct email to the Faculty of Science Academic Advising office or any Department Undergraduate Officer. Completed forms are forwarded to the Faculty of Science Academic Advising office. You will receive a response to your myLaurier email account from your Academic Advisor, Department Undergraduate Officer or advising support staff, where appropriate.

    Whom Do I Contact?

    The academic advisor that you should contact depends on:

    • your year of study;
    • your program of registration
    • the type of inquiry; and
    • the complexity of your academic circumstances.

    Other Resources in the Science Academic Advising Office

    Appointments with the Academic Integrity and Accommodations Coordinator, Denise Cooney, are available to undergraduate students with questions or concerns regarding:

    • academic misconduct - warnings, formal notifications, findings and penalties
    • academic integrity - resources and information, understanding university policies related to academic integrity
    • accommodations - temporary requests

    Appointments are up to 30 minutes in length, take place virtually using Microsoft Teams and can be booked using the online booking calendar .  If you prefer to connect via email, please reach out to dcooney@wlu.ca 

    Undergraduate students may appeal, to their home faculty, any decision made by an individual or body acting in the name of the university if the decision affects your academic standing and you believe it was made unfairly or you believe you should receive an exception to the rules due to etenuating circumstances. 

    Academic Petitions and Appeals are for extenuating circumstances only.

    To contact the Petitions and Regulations Coordinator for the Faculty of Science complete the Faculty of Science Academic Advising Inquiry form to initiate a request.
