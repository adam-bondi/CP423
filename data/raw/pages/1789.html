    Locations and Contacts

    The Dean of Students Office is available to students across all locations and is dedicated to delivering comprehensive service and support to all students who reach out.

    If you require any accessibility accommodations during your appointment, let us know in advance.

    • Waterloo WAT Click this tab to view content pertaining to Waterloo
    • Brantford BRANT Click this tab to view content pertaining to Brantford
    • Milton MLTN Click this tab to view content pertaining to Milton
    • Kitchener KIT Click this tab to view content pertaining to Kitchener

    Waterloo Campus

    Office: Fred Nichols Campus Centre Rm. 2-209

    * From Apr. 28 to mid-August, the Office of Student Affairs and Dean of Students Office will be operating out of the lower-level Co-op and Career Centre (192 King St. N.).

    Email: deanofstudents@wlu.ca

    Hours of Operation: Monday - Friday: 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. (closed noon-1 p.m. daily)

    Elevated Student Support

    Waterloo students requiring elevated student support can book an in-person or virtual appointment online or by reaching out by email at deanofstudents@wlu.ca.

    From September to April, the Dean of Students Office hosts weekly drop-in hours for students seeking support and guidance. Appointments are in-person, 10-15 minutes in length, and first come, first served every Wednesday from 1:30-3:30 p.m.

    Student Conduct

    Waterloo students with concerns related to student conduct or who wish to request an appointment can email studentconduct@wlu.ca.

    Brantford Campus

    Office: One Market Building OM 207-40

    Email: deanofstudents@wlu.ca

    Hours of Operation: Monday - Friday: 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. (closed noon-1 p.m. daily)

    Elevated Student Support

    Brantford students requiring elevated student support can book an in-person or virtual appointment online or by reaching out by email to deanofstudents@wlu.ca.

    Student Conduct

    Brantford students with concerns related to student conduct or who wish to request an appointment can email studentconduct@wlu.ca. 

    Milton Campus

    Office: MAC Reception Desk

    Email: deanofstudents@wlu.ca

    Hours of Operation: Monday - Friday: 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. (closed noon-1 p.m. daily)

    In-Person Support

    Students who have initial questions about support available to Milton students can connect directly with Alicia Clarke, Student Affairs Coordinator, for more information.

    Elevated Student Support

    Milton students requiring elevated student support can book a virtual appointment online with a Brantford-based staff member or by emailing deanofstudents@wlu.ca.

    Student Conduct

    Milton students with concerns related to student conduct or who wish to request a virtual appointment can email studentconduct@wlu.ca. 

    Kitchener Location

    Students attending the Kitchener location are supported by Waterloo campus-based staff.

    Elevated Student Support

    Students can book virtual appointments with Waterloo campus-based staff or attend in-person appointments on the Waterloo campus.

    Appointments can be booked online or by emailing deanofstudents@wlu.ca.

    Student Conduct

    Kitchener students with concerns related to student conduct or who wish to request a virtual appointment can email studentconduct@wlu.ca. 

    Online Students

    Elevated Student Support

    Online students requesting elevated student support are supported through our Brantford campus staff.

    Virtual appointments can be booked online or by emailing deanofstudents@wlu.ca.

    Student Conduct

    Online students with concerns related to student conduct or who wish to request a virtual appointment can email studentconduct@wlu.ca.
