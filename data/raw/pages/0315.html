    Athlete Academic Success Program

    The Athlete Academic Success Program provides Laurier’s varsity athletes with academic assistance regarding learning strategies and study skills in a manner that accommodates their training and competition schedule so that they can achieve their full academic potential.

    This program includes study halls to assist student athletes with their studying, and workshops to assist student athletes with their learning strategies and study skills development.

    Athlete Study Halls

    There is always something productive you can work on as a student athlete during study hall. If you’re unsure where to begin, try one of the following:

    • Check MyLS or your course syllabi for upcoming deadlines
    • Review lecture slides
    • Rewrite or organize your class notes
    • Start an assignment early
    • Read one article or chapter for an upcoming class
    • Create a to-do list for the week
    • Email a professor or TA with a question
    • Work on group project planning
    • Prepare questions for your next class

    Your Athlete Peer Academic Coaches can help you during study hall with creating a plan, breaking down assignments, improving your time management, and staying accountable. We look forward to seeing you there!

    Arrange Peer Academic Coaching

    Athlete peer academic coaches are your fellow varsity athletes who achieve success both in the classroom and on the field of play.

    To arrange academic coaching, you can:

    • Schedule an appointment with your peer academic coach during study halls.
    • Schedule appointments with a learning consultant through the Student Success Portal.

    Note: You can track your attendance through the Student Success Portal.
