    Brantford Study Spaces

    Check out the study spaces on the Brantford campus below. The Brantford interactive map can help you find your way around campus to each of the buildings.

    Take note of the amount of traffic (people moving through) and noise common for each to find the best fit. Some of the spaces require your OneCard to access.

    Brantford Public Library

    • Location: Third floor
    • Hours:
      • Weekdays 5:30 a.m. – 9:30 p.m.
      • Weekends 8 a.m. – 6 p.m.
    • Restrictions: Quiet study zone

    This space is public use and has many digital resources available. It has medium-high traffic with low noise levels.

    Carnegie Building

    Commuter Lounge

    • Location: Basement
    • Hours:
      • Weekdays 8 a.m. – midnight
      • Weekends 7:30 a.m. – 10 p.m.
    • Restrictions: None
    • Microwave available

    This space is a small lounge area with access to a small kitchenette and lockers. It has low traffic and medium-low noise levels.

     

    Lobby

    • Location: Main floor
    • Hours:
      • Weekdays 8 a.m. – midnight
      • Weekends 7:30 a.m. – 10 p.m.
    • Restrictions: None

    This space is a small, open lounge surrounded by classroom spaces. It has low-medium traffic and low noise levels.

    Grand River Hall

    Digital Library and Learning Commons

    • Location: Basement
    • Hours: Always open
    • Restrictions: Quiet study zone

    This space is a large open area with a variety of seating options and side rooms. It has medium traffic and medium noise levels.

    Laurier Brantford YMCA

    Hawks Nest

    • Location: Fourth floor
    • Hours:
      • Weekdays 5:30 a.m. – 10 p.m.
      • Weekends 8 a.m. – 6 p.m.
    • Restrictions: Requires OneCard for elevator
    • Microwave available

    This space is a large rectangle with lots of natural light. It has low traffic and low noise levels.

    Market Darling Centre

    • Location: Basement lounge
    • Hours:
      • Weekdays 7:30 a.m. – midnight
      • Weekends 7:30 a.m. – 10 p.m.
    • Restrictions: None
    • Microwave available

    This space is a medium lounge area with various seating options and a large kitchen. It has medium traffic and medium noise levels.

    Odeon Building

    Lobby Lounge

    • Location: First floor
    • Hours: Always open
    • Restrictions: None

    This space is a medium lounge area surrounded by classrooms. It has medium-high traffic and medium noise levels.

     

    Basement Lounge

    • Location: Basement
    • Hours: Always open
    • Restrictions: None

    This space is a small, quiet area with vending machines. It has low traffic and low noise levels.

    One Market

    Atrium

    • Location: Basement
    • Hours:
      • Weekdays 8 a.m. – 11 p.m.
      • Weekends 7:30 a.m. – 5 p.m.
    • Restrictions: None
    • Microwaves available in the kitchenette, by the podcast studio and the Laurier International office

    This space is large and open with skylights, three-story ceilings and various seating options. It has medium traffic with low-medium noise levels.

    Level 1 Lounge

    • Location: OM116
    • Hours:
      • Weekdays 9 a.m. – 10 p.m.
      • Summer Weekdays 9 a.m. – 4:30 p.m.
    • Restrictions: This lounge is only open with staff working the desk. Hours may vary based on staff availability.
    • Microwave availability

    This space is a social lounge with many booths and tables. It has medium-high traffic with high noise levels and music.

    Student Learning Commons

    • Location: OM207
    • Hours:
      • Weekdays 8 a.m. – 11 p.m.
      • Weekends 7:30 a.m. – 5 p.m.
    • Restrictions: None
    • Microwaves available in the kitchenette, by the podcast studio and the Laurier International office

    This space is a large open area with a variety of seating options for individuals or groups. It has high traffic and medium-high noise levels.

     

    Research and Academic Centre

    Courtyard

    • Location: Research Academic Centre East
    • Hours: Always open
    • Restrictions: None

    This outdoor campus space has lots of shaded options and is located right behind the RAC. It had medium traffic and medium noise levels.

    Main Lobby

    • Restrictions: None
