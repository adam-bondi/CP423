    Locations and Contacts

    Connect with the Career Centre

    The Career Centre is available to students and alumni through online and in-person services in Waterloo, Brantford, Milton, Kitchener and Toronto. Our vision is for all students to engage in experiential learning and their career development as an integral part of the Laurier experience. View the Experiential Learning and Career Development Strategic Plan for more details.

    Waterloo Campus

    • 192 King Street North (across from the Science Building)
    • 548.889.3254
    • careercentre@wlu.ca

    Hours of Operation

    • Monday - Friday: 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., Wednesday: 8:30 a.m. - 7 p.m. (September-April only)
    • The Career Centre offers in-person and online appointments and workshops which can be booked in Navigator.
    • You can pre-book an appointment or book a same-day brief appointment. Brief appointments are available Monday - Friday from 1 p.m. to 4 p.m. for assistance with resumé/cover letter writing, interview skills, job search strategies and further education questions.

    Lazaridis School Graduate Students

    Booking Appointments

    Individual appointments may be booked in Navigator to discuss your career planning and job search needs.

    Milton Campus

    Milton students can access Career Centre services online or in person at the Waterloo campus. In-person services on the Milton campus will be listed on the Career Centre event calendar.

    You can pre-book an appointment or book a same-day brief appointment. Brief appointments are available Tuesday - Thursday from 1 p.m. to 3 p.m. for assistance with resumé/cover letter writing, interview skills, job search strategies and further education questions.
