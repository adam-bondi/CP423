    LOCUS Off-Campus Advisors

    Off-campus advisors (OCAs) are upper year Laurier students on Laurier’s Brantford, Milton and Waterloo campuses here to support all incoming first-year undergraduate students living off-campus. OCAs host regular programming opportunities and are available to answer any questions you may have such as those related to:

    • the Laurier student experience
    • academics and campus resources
    • the city and broader community
    • other general inquiries or specific experiences

    OCA’s can also help you navigate life at Laurier, find more opportunities for volunteering, leadership, and fun. There's also lots of programming for you to engage with – be sure to check out our schedule of upcoming events!

    Connect with an OCA today on the Brantford team, Milton team or Waterloo team today! Looking to get involved with LOCUS? We have a number of work and volunteer positions each year.

    Work and Volunteer for LOCUS

    LOCUS could not provide exceptional experiences without our dedicated student volunteer team! It's a great way to get involved with the Laurier campus community.

     

    Volunteer hiring for the 2026-2027 academic year has ended. Interested in applying in the future? Take a look at the listings below to see what opportunities are available on your campus.

    An off-campus advisor works with the advisor team to plan regular programming for first-years and is there to answer any questions students may have on school, academics, the city, or just life in general. 

    Position Description 

    Reports to: Coordinator, Off-Campus Initiatives 
    Time Commitment: Five to seven hours per week and Orientation (including Off-Campus Welcome Day)

    Off-Campus Advisor (Core Role) Position Details

    • Host social events with the intent of building positive social relationships and integrating students into campus including those themed around campus services/community, virtual programming, and living in Brantford/Milton/Waterloo. 
    • Support and assist with the hosting of events themed around academic success and student well-being for LOCUS students. 
    • Actively engage in the brainstorming, planning/creation, facilitation, and evaluation of all LOCUS events and initiatives, completing all proper documents and following all steps by the provided deadlines. 
    • Be a consistent resource to the first-year students by answering questions, addressing concerns, sharing resources, and making referrals  
    • Hold a position as an ice-breaker on the Orientation team and fulfill all the duties as outlined in that job description (hours determined by the Students’ Union, typically 30+ hours for orientation programming plus trainings)  
    • Work with the Orientation team to actively promote the LOCUS program, as well as ensure that the first-year student population experiences an exceptional orientation week (including staffing LOCUS-specific orientation events and activities). 
    • Actively engage and support the promotion and facilitation of LOCUS Links and the LOCUS House Council during summer months, orientation and the academic year. 
    • Create & maintain a friendly relationship with first year off-campus students 
    • Attend all training, included mandatory summer training  
    • Support other LOCUS staff by promoting & attending their events, including LOCUS House Council, the First Year Leadership Program events, etc.  
    • Attend LOCUS staff meetings, events, & one-on-ones 
    • Actively participate in the planning, facilitation and evaluation of larger scale programming from LOCUS in campus wide events such as Thrive Week, Keep Calm & Study On, Off-Campus Welcome Day, Fall/Winter Orientation etc.   
    • Attend FYLP events including those planned by campus councils and Hawk Weekend/Golden Hawk Challenge.   
    • Maintain open and honest communication with everyone involved in the success of LOCUS 
    • Promote, present, and recruit students to the LOCUS program at various events (Open Houses, Orientation, etc.) 
    • Act as an ambassador of the LOCUS program and Wilfrid Laurier University 
    • If assigned, fulfill the role as a Link Leader, providing direct guidance and programming to a specific group of students with the expectation of regular outreach and a minimum of 1 active program per month. 
    • Optional: Successful candidates will have the opportunity to apply to be a council advisor within the First Year Leadership Program 

    Link Leader (Specialized-OCA Focus) Details: 

    For the 2026/2027 academic year, we will have one OCA lead each Link. To make sure we have Links up and running, it is possible that a student leader's flexibility to lead a Link is considered in the hiring process. To view all available Links and additional information, please click here for Brantford and here for Waterloo.

    Note: Being a Link Leader is not meant to increase the workload of Off-Campus Advisors. All non-Link Leader OCAs will have to lead a minimum of 2 social events each month. All OCAs that are leading a Link will have to lead a minimum of 1 Link event and 1 social event per month. Both totalling a minimum of 2 events per month. 

    • Additional things to consider with communication: while Link Leaders have assigned a group of students to check in/connect with, the remaining OCAs are also expected to be connecting with students, but they do not have 1 specific target audience. 

    Meeting Expectations

    • Weekly LOCUS Staff Team Meetings 
    • Monthly one-on-ones with the Coordinator, Off-Campus Initiatives   
    • Other meetings as assigned 

    Important Dates and Time Commitment

    • August Training: LOCUS Program Training (3 days over the final 2 weeks of August)
      • This training must be attended in person/on campus 
    • Fall Orientation: 35-40 hours 
    • Spring/Summer Term: 5-7 hours per month  
      • All spring/summer hours (except August training) can be completed remotely and mainly consist of online asynchronous training modules  
    • Fall and Winter term: 5-7 hours per week

    Benefits of the Role

    • Opportunity to mentor first-year off-campus students and make their first-year experience positive and memorable 
    • Opportunity to develop and improve organization, leadership, presentation, and time management skills 
    • Opportunity to plan programs and events designed for first-year students 
    • Opportunity to effectively and creatively communicate with off-campus students and campus partners about the LOCUS program 
    • Opportunity to collaborate with various departments on campus, ie: Transition and Learning Services, Dean of Students Office, Your Students’ Union, the Centre for Student Equity, Diversity and Inclusion, Learning Skills and Development, Athletics and Recreation, etc. 

    Successful Candidates will: 

    • Be enrolled as a full-time Laurier undergraduate student for the 2024/2025 school year 
    • Have experienced one full academic year at Wilfrid Laurier University 
    • Have a minimum GPA of 6.0, or the minimum required GPA to progress within their academic program, whichever is higher 
    • Have a passion for student leadership, engagement, and providing an exceptional first-year student experience 
    • Have demonstrated excellent interpersonal and communication skills 
    • Have demonstrated ability to work independently and as part of a team 
    • Have proven creativity, innovation, and initiative, particularly in regard to event planning and communicating with students 

    An off-campus advisor works with the advisor team to plan regular programming for first-years and is there to answer any questions students may have on school, academics, the city, or just life in general. 

    Position Description 

    Reports to: Coordinator, Off-Campus Initiatives 
    Time Commitment: Approximately five hours per week, and Orientation Programming

    Off-Campus Advisor Position Details

    • Host a minimum of one (1) social event per month, alongside other volunteers, with the intent of building positive social relationships and integrating students into campus including those themed around campus services/community, virtual programming, and living in Milton and surrounding areas 
    • Host a minimum of one (1) drop-in style passive event per month for students to connect with you and each other, focusing on community connection and faculty/program-based work 
    • Support and assist with the hosting of events themed around academic success and student well-being for LOCUS students 
    • Actively engage in the brainstorming, planning/creation, facilitation, and evaluation of all LOCUS events and initiatives, completing all proper documents and following all steps by the provided deadlines 
    • Create engaging and informative content to share upcoming event details and resources  
    • Be a consistent resource to the first-year students by answering questions, addressing concerns, sharing resources, and making referrals  
    • Actively engage and support the promotion and facilitation of the LOCUS House Council during summer months, orientation, and the academic year 
    • Create and maintain a friendly relationship with first year off-campus students 
    • Attend all training, including mandatory summer training  
    • Support other LOCUS staff by promoting & attending their events, including LOCUS House Council, and individually led OCA events etc.  
    • Attend LOCUS staff meetings, events, and one-on-ones 
    • Assist with the promotion of resources from Laurier’s Off-Campus Housing Support program as needed 
    • Actively participate in the planning, facilitation and evaluation of larger scale programming from LOCUS in campus wide events such as Thrive Week, Keep Calm & Study On, Fall/Winter Orientation etc.   
    • Maintain open and honest communication with everyone involved in the success of LOCUS 
    • Promote, present, and recruit students to the LOCUS program at various events (Open Houses, Orientation, etc.) 
    • Act as an ambassador of the LOCUS program and Wilfrid Laurier University 

    Meeting Expectations

    • Weekly LOCUS staff team meetings 
    • Monthly one-on-ones with the Coordinator, Off-Campus Initiatives   
    • Regular meetings as needed with peers (outside of weekly staff meetings) to plan and prepare for LOCUS events as needed. 
    • Other meetings as assigned 

    Important Dates and Time Commitment

    • August Training: LOCUS Program Training will take place over 3 days within the last 2 weeks of August. Applicants should hold August 25th to 28th until more details are available 
      • These training sessions must be attended in-person 
    • Fall Orientation: 16 hours 
    • Spring/Summer Term: five to seven hours per month  
      • The majority of spring/summer hours (except August training) can be completed remotely, mainly consisting of online asynchronous training modules and virtual programming. There may be some in-person on-campus opportunities in which we would love to have you present if able. 
    • Fall and Winter term: approximately five hours per week 

    Benefits of the Role

    • Opportunity to mentor first-year off-campus students and make their first-year experience positive and memorable 
    • Opportunity to develop and improve organization, leadership, presentation and time management skills 
    • Opportunity to plan programs and events designed for first-year students 
    • Opportunity to effectively and creatively communicate with off-campus students and campus partners about the LOCUS program 
    • Opportunity to be a part of the first LOCUS Milton volunteer team 
    • Opportunity to collaborate with various departments , ie: Academic Advising, Career and Co-Op, Student Success, and VP: Academics Office. 

    Successful Candidates will: 

    • Be enrolled as a full-time Laurier undergraduate student at the Milton campus for the 2026/2027 school year 
    • Have experienced one full academic year at Wilfrid Laurier University 
    • Have a minimum GPA of 6.0, or the minimum required GPA to progress within their academic program, whichever is higher 
    • Have a passion for student leadership, engagement and providing an exceptional first year student experience 
    • Have demonstrated excellent interpersonal and communication skills 
    • Have demonstrated ability to work independently and as part of a team 
    • Have proven creativity, innovation and initiative, particularly in regards to event planning and communicating with students 

    Application Information & Deadlines 

    During the application window, applicants should prepare to answer the following questions on the application form: 

    1. What interests you in volunteering with LOCUS? 
    1. If successful in this application process, what ideas do you have to support first year students as an Off-Campus Advisor (or House Council Advisor)? 
    1. Can you share an event idea that you'd run for LOCUS programming? 

    Applications are due by Wednesday March 11th, 2026, at 11:59pm 

    Interview Details: 

    Successful applicants will be tasked with creating a presentation to present to the hiring panel during their interview. The presentation will outline why they are the ideal candidate for the position they are applying for. Presentations should be 5-7 minutes in length. Candidates can present in any format that they would like. Slides/visual components are not required, but may be used for this presentation; a prepared verbal response is satisfactory. 

    Your presentation should also address the following question: 

    What is the biggest challenge off-campus first-year students face? Please describe 2-3 ways in which you would try to minimize this challenge.  

    After the presentation, the interview panel will ask a series of questions relating to the off-campus advisor role.  

    Interviews will take place mainly on March 17th with few timeslots available on March 18th. All interviews will be in person on the Milton campus.

    Interview Details: 

    Successful applicants will be tasked with creating a presentation to present to the hiring panel during their interview. The presentation will outline why they are the ideal candidate for the position they are applying for. Presentations should be 5-7 minutes in length. Candidates can present in any format that they would like. Slides/visual components are not required, but may be used for this presentation; a prepared verbal response is satisfactory. 

    Your presentation should also address the following question: 

    What is the biggest challenge off-campus first-year students face? Please describe 2-3 ways in which you would try to minimize this challenge.  

    After the presentation, the interview panel will ask a series of questions relating to the off-campus advisor role.  

    Interviews will take place mainly on March 17th with few timeslots available on March 18th. All interviews will be in person on the Milton campus.

    The marketing and engagement executive works directly with the Off-Campus Advisor (OCA) team to amplify and promote all programming offered through the LOCUS program. Special focus and support will be given to increasing the social media presence of the LOCUS program across various platforms. 

    Position Description 

    Reports to: Coordinator, Off-Campus Initiatives 
    Time Commitment: 5-7 hours per week + Orientation (including Off-Campus Welcome Day)

    Position Details

    • Work with the LOCUS student leader team and assist in promoting LOCUS-led programs and events each month (including LOCUS programming, Links, House Council/FYLP, and more) 
    • Create and share a social media strategy for all LOCUS accounts with the team, receiving approval from the supervisor for the upcoming month 
    • Populate consistent and engaging content on LOCUS social media accounts, including the use of stories, reels, and posts 
    • Maintain consistent records of LOCUS events, FYLP events, key university dates/deadlines, and campus partners’ programs to ensure frequent and timely promotions 
    • Create innovative content that educates off-campus students about the Laurier community and broader Waterloo, Brantford & Milton communities 
    • Host engaging contests to push higher followers and engagement on social media accounts 
    • Analyze and record social media analytics and share findings with LOCUS staff during meetings and as needed. 
    • Be a consistent resource to the first-year students by hosting drop-in hours/booths, answering questions, addressing concerns, making referrals, and promoting engagement within the LOCUS program 
    • Attend all training, including mandatory August training  
    • Support LOCUS staff by attending their events and capturing content 
    • Coordinate the event reflection process and any event wrap-up details. With information from the Coordinator, prepare mid and end of term recaps, exploring how we can better utilize unique marketing tactics to enhance engagement 
    • Provide staff and administrative support throughout Orientation, including working the LOCUS registration booth, staffing during OCA breaks/downtime, and assisting in facilitating LOCUS-specific programming 
    • Attend LOCUS staff meetings, events, and one-on-ones 
    • Actively participate in the planning, facilitation and evaluation of larger scale programming from LOCUS on campus wide events such as Thrive Week, Keep Calm & Study On, Off-Campus Welcome Day and Fall/Winter Orientation etc.   
    • Promote, present, and recruit students to the LOCUS program at various events (Open Houses, Off-Campus Welcome Day, Orientation Week, etc) 
    • Maintain open and honest communication with everyone involved in the success of LOCUS 
    • Create & maintain a friendly relationship with first-year off-campus students 
    • Act as an ambassador of the LOCUS program and Wilfrid Laurier University 

    Meeting Expectations

    • Weekly LOCUS Staff Team Meetings 
    • Weekly drop-in hours for student staff to connect about marketing projects 
    • Biweekly multi-campus marketing check-ins 
    • Monthly one-on-ones with the Coordinator, Off-Campus Initiatives   
    • Other meetings as assigned 

    Important Dates and Time Commitment

    • August Training: LOCUS Program Training (3 days over the final 2 weeks of August)
      • This training must be attended in person/on campus 
    • Fall Orientation: 35-40 hours 
    • Spring/Summer Term: 5-7 hours per month  
      • All spring/summer hours (except August training) can be completed remotely and mainly consist of online asynchronous training modules  
    • Fall and Winter term: 5-7 hours per week

    Benefits of the Role

    • Opportunity to mentor first-year off-campus students and make their first-year experience positive and memorable 
    • Opportunity to develop and improve organization, leadership, presentation, and time management skills 
    • Opportunity to plan programs and events designed for first-year students 
    • Opportunity to effectively and creatively communicate with off-campus students and campus partners about the LOCUS program 
    • Opportunity to collaborate with various departments on campus, ie: Transition and Learning Services, Dean of Students Office, Your Students’ Union, the Centre for Student Equity, Diversity and Inclusion, Learning Skills and Development, Athletics and Recreation, etc. 

    Successful Candidates will: 

    • Be enrolled as a full-time Laurier undergraduate student for the 2026/2027 school year 
    • Have experienced one full academic year at Wilfrid Laurier University 
    • Have a minimum GPA of 6.0, or the minimum required GPA to progress within their academic program, whichever is higher 
    • Have a passion for student leadership, engagement, and providing an exceptional first-year student experience 
    • Have demonstrated excellent interpersonal and communication skills 
    • Have demonstrated ability to work independently and as part of a team 
    • Have proven creativity, innovation, and initiative, particularly in regard to event planning and communicating with students 

    Please note, that this role is exclusively on the Waterloo campus. All other positions are available on the Brantford and Waterloo campuses.
