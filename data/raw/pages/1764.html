    Support and Wellness

    Your well-being is essential to your success, both inside and outside the classroom. Support and wellness bring together the services, resources and communities that support your personal and social wellness throughout your time at Laurier.

    Whether you're seeking guidance, advocacy, connection or care, you'll find what you need to thrive.

    Student Wellness Centre

    The Student Wellness Centre supports your health and wellness journey with confidential and comprehensive care, both physical and mental, throughout your time as a student. 

    Access Student Wellness Centre Services

    Inclusive and Respectful Campuses

    Centre for Equity, Diversity and Inclusion

    The Centre for Equity, Diversity and Inclusion (CSEDI) helps foster a campus culture where everyone feels valued and respected. 

    • Explore EDI initiatives and programs.
    • Attend events and EDI education opportunities.

    Gendered Violence Prevention and Support

    The Gendered Violence Prevention and Support team provides a safe and supportive environment for students who have experienced gendered and sexual violence. 

    • Find confidential support services, reporting and response options.
    • Participate in prevention education and training. 

    Human Rights and Conflict Management

    The Human Rights and Conflict Management Office plays a role in creating a supportive environment for your personal wellbeing and where you can be treated with respect. 

    • Connect if you have experienced harassment or discrimination. 
    • Navigate conflict resolution and mediation services. 

    Student Life at Laurier

    Through Student Affairs, you can expect to find your people, get the support you need and make the most of your time at Laurier, in and out of the classroom.

    • Childcare Services
    • Governance at Laurier

    Community Support

    Indigenous Student Services

    The Indigenous Student Centres are dedicated to providing self-identified Indigenous students seeking connections with the Indigenous student community and engaging with cultural teachings. 

    • Connect with Indigenous student support and advising.
    • Attend cultural programming and events.

    International Student Support

    The International Student Support team supports international students at Laurier, ensuring that you feel at home. 

    • Consult with advisors for immigration and visa. 
    • Connect, learn and get to know fell students who share your international experience.

    You Are Not Alone. Reach Out.

    You’re not alone on your journey at Laurier. There is a whole network of people and resources here to support you, whenever you need it.
