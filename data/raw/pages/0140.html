    Service Laurier Live Chat 

    Live Chat is for general Enrolment Services inquiries, including admissions, financial aid and OSAP, scholarships and bursaries, and records and registration.

    Chat Availability

    Chat availability may vary, check the bottom right corner of the page for the following: 

    • Chat with an Expert means we're online.
    • Agent Offline means we're unavailable. Return to the Service Laurier page to connect with us another way. 

    • If your question is unique or confidential, connect with us via phone, virtual appointment, or webform. 
      • Do not include personal information unless our advisors ask you a series of questions to verify your identity.
      • We will never ask for your SIN, banking, including credit card information. 
    • Chat users should be aware that records of the chats are retained.  
    • Using inappropriate language may result in the termination of the chat and, in addition to supplying false or misleading information, is considered student misconduct under the Non-Academic Student Code of Conduct. 
    Service Laurier staff are available to answer your questions and provide support. We hold ourselves and each other accountable to our service values. We aim to provide a helpful, friendly, and positive experience. We will act with integrity, respect, and accountability in all interactions. Please engage with us in a similar manner.

    This platform functions best with Chrome.  

    Firefox users: Enhanced tracking protection is usually turned on by default for Firefox (Firefox-specific feature). To turn it off:

    1. Select the lock icon in the address bar.
    2. Tap the Enhanced Tracking Protection toggle off.
    Additional support can be found on the Enhanced Tracking Protection in Firefox for Android page.
