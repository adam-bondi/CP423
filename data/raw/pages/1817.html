    Student Wellness Centre

    Laurier's Student Wellness Centre, on the Brantford, Waterloo, and Milton campus, works to promote health and wellness by: 

    • Creating a safe, welcoming, non-judgemental environment for all students.
    • Providing confidential, comprehensive, and collaborative physical, emotional, and mental health services that support your needs. 
    • Facilitating referrals to ensure optimal care. 
    • Preparing and empowering you to be successful in your health and wellness journeys during your time at Laurier.

    If you require a doctor's note or documentation of illness for academic reasons, complete the Illness Self-Declaration form.  Medical documentation is not required when you miss a final exam due to illness. Please follow the faculty-specific petition guidelines on the Deferred Exams page that include making a personal attestation of your illness.

    Urgent and After-Hours Care

    If you are suicidal or do not think you can keep yourself safe right now: 

    • Call or text 988, the Suicide Crisis Helpline;
    • Call Here 24/7 at 1.844.Here.24.7 (1.844.437.3247);
    • Call Laurier's Special Constable Service at 519.885.3333.
    • Call 911.

    Appointments and Fees

    To schedule an appointment:

    • Drop in or call us when we are open to schedule an appointment.
    • Book online.
    • We will need your:
      • Health card (OHIP, provincial health card or valid UHIP coverage) and OneCard
      • The address where you are living while you are at school
      • Phone number

    We do not schedule or reschedule appointments over email, but you can email us to cancel your appointment.  If you cancel your appointment on short notice or don't show up for a scheduled appointment, you will be charged a no-show fee.  You will also be charged if your appointment is cancelled because you arrived late.

    Medical Doctor Appointments

    A limited number of same-day appointments are available each day for students with acute illness and unpredictable concerns.

    Students are asked to call as early as possible to schedule an appointment with the doctor later that day.

    Counsellor Appointments

    A limited number of same-day "WIT" appointments are available with counsellors each day for students who are not in crisis but feel they need to speak with someone that day.

    These appointments are scheduled on a first come, first serve basis, so please call early.

    If you are in crisis, let us know when you call, so we can schedule you promptly.

    We require 24 hours notice of cancellation. 

    To cancel your appointment, 

    • call us or leave a message, if we are closed,
    • email us at wellness@wlu.ca, or
    • reply to your reminder email to let us know you would like to cancel. 

    If you cancel your appointment on short notice or don't show up for a scheduled appointment, you will be charged a no-show fee.  You will also be charged if your appointment is cancelled because you arrived late.

    No-show fees are based on your appointment type:

    • counselling session: $20
    • routine medical (short): $30
    • routine medical (long): $60  

    Visits to the Student Wellness Centre for medical appointments are generally covered by provincial health insurance (OHIP) or UHIP. The cost of counselling sessions is covered by your student services fees. Certain types of appointments, certain forms requested by third parties, and certain types of documentation are subject to additional fees.
