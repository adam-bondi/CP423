    iClicker

    iClicker

    Overview

    iClicker is software your instructor can use to collect attendance, take a poll, or run a quiz. The polling may be anonymous or graded, depending on your instructor's goals. iClicker access codes can be purchased from the Laurier Bookstore. Courses that are only taking attendance do not require a subscription.

    1. You can create your account by either downloading the mobile app from the App Store or Google Play, or by visiting the iClicker student web app and selecting Sign Up!
    2. In the Find Your Institution textbox, find and select Wilfrid Laurier University
    3. Enter your information (use your @mylaurier.ca email address) and select "Create Account"

     

    Courses that are only taking attendance through iClicker do not require an iClicker subscription. You can download the iClicker student app onto your mobile device or check in using a web browser.

    1. Create an account by either downloading the mobile app from the App Store or Google Play, or by visiting the iClicker student web app and selecting Sign Up!
    2. In the Find Your Institution textbox, find and select Wilfrid Laurier University
    3. Enter your information and select "Create Account"
    4. Remotes cannot be used to check in for Attendance Only courses, so you can Skip This Step
    5. Add your instructor's course to your Courses list in the iClicker student app

    iClicker access codes can be purchased from the Laurier Bookstore.
