    Virtual Orientation: Start Strong in September

    Kick off your Laurier journey with our Virtual Orientation: Start Strong in September session, designed to help you feel confident, connected, and prepared for virtual learning from day one.

    Join us for an interactive evening where you’ll be welcomed to the Laurier community, meet fellow virtual students, and start building connections. You’ll explore what to expect in your first week of classes, learn how to navigate MyLearningSpace, and discover practical strategies for succeeding in a virtual learning environment. We’ll also introduce you to key student services and supports available remotely, so you know where to turn throughout your studies. The session wraps up with a live Q&A, giving you the chance to get your questions answered and feel fully prepared to begin your Golden Hawk experience!

    Virtual Learning: Academic Supports

    Our Academic Skills Workshops are designed to help you start strong and stay on track throughout your first year at Laurier. These sessions give you practical strategies you can use right away, like managing your time, taking effective notes, and building solid study habits that set you up for success.

    With flexible workshop options, you can learn how to stay organized, boost your focus, and feel more confident as you begin your university journey. It’s a great way to build the skills you need to thrive in your courses from day one.

    Register for workshops using the Student Success portal.

    Set yourself up for a strong start at Laurier! As you begin your studies this Fall, we strongly recommend you book an appointment with one of our professional Learning Consultants to build the skills you need for university life. We’ll work with you to strengthen your focus, manage your time effectively, and develop study strategies that help you understand your course material with confidence.

    Whether you're adjusting to your first term or looking to establish great habits from day one, we’re here to support your academic success and help you create a healthy school–life balance.

    You can book a one-on-one appointment on the Student Success portal. Let’s work together to make your Laurier journey a successful one.

    Peer Academic Coaches offer peer-to-peer support to help you navigate the transition to university. Our coaching team is made up of successful senior students who want to help you get off to a good start. Connect with a coach to help you build a study schedule, discuss note-taking methods, learn effective study strategies, and more.

    Go to the Student Success portal to book a 25-minute, one-on-one, First-Year Peer Academic Coaching appointment.

    Take your learning to the next level with our Study Skills team!

    We offer workshops, one-on-one coaching, and resources to help you succeed in university and beyond. Learn how to manage your time, take effective notes, stay organized, and prepare for exams with confidence. Whether you're aiming for better grades, sharpening your focus, or boosting productivity, our tools and techniques are tailored to your learning.

    We encourage you to take some time to explore our full complement of Study Skills programs, workshops, and appointment options.

     

    Struggling with solving equations, using formulas, or applying key concepts covered in your course?

    Our Math and Course Support team of successful senior students and professional staff will work with you to provide guidance, study techniques, and strategies to enhance your understanding. Let us help you succeed in your math, statistics, and problem-solving based courses.

    We encourage you to take some time to explore our full complement of Math and Course Support programs and workshops.

    Need help crafting your essays, refining your writing skills, or organizing your thoughts?

    Our Writing Support team can support you through any step of the writing process. From understanding the assignment to polishing final drafts, we’ll help you hone your writing skills and build a sustainable writing practice for university and beyond.

    We encourage you to take some time to explore our full complement of Writing Support programs, workshops, and appointment options..
