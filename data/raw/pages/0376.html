    Technology for Exams and Quizzes

    Your exam accommodations may include using reading and/or writing software. While using this software, some features like spell check, may not be available.  

    If your exam accommodations includes using technology, make sure that you:

    • Book your exams by posted deadlines.
    • Familiarize yourself with the technology well before your exams, and speak with an Assistive Technologist for support.
    • Bring headphones without Bluetooth for listening or dictation.
    • Alert the proctor immediately if you encounter any difficulties. They will contact an Assistive Technologist if necessary.

    Exam Types and Software Options

    Depending on whether your exam is in-person and paper-based or online, you may be permitted to use various types software and/or features. The table below lists possible software options for various types of exams.
