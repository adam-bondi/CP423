    January Jumpstart: Events and Activities

    January Jumpstart is your chance to join the Laurier 101, ASPIRE, Student Experience, Students' Union, Indigenous Student Services, and International Student Support teams throughout the month of January as we welcome you for the winter term.

    Whether you joined Laurier in the fall or are starting this winter, January is full of opportunities to meet new people, explore campus life, and get involved. We have events designed to help you build connections, discover resources, and make the most of your Laurier experience. Check out what’s happening on your campus!

    Jan. 5 to 9

      Monday, Jan. 5
      12:30 to 1:20 p.m.
      One Market Atrium (OM100) | Registration Required: Undergraduate Student Registration; Graduate Student Registration
      Open to Undergraduate and Graduate students starting at Laurier in Winter 2026

    Starting your Laurier journey this January? Join us for the New Student Welcome Lunch, a warm and friendly way to kick off your first day!     Notion 101

      Wednesday, Jan. 7
      1 to 2 p.m.
      MacDonald House 24 Lounge
      Open to first-year undergraduate Laurier Off-Campus University Students (LOCUS)

    Kick off the new year feeling organized and in control!

    Join us for Notion 101, a beginner-friendly workshop where you’ll learn how to use Notion to plan your semester, manage assignments, organize readings, and keep track of your academic schedule all in one place.

    We’ll walk through the basics of building pages, using templates, and creating tools that actually work for you. Whether you’re brand new to Notion or looking to level up your setup, this session will help you start the semester strong.

    Come by, bring your laptop, and leave with a Notion system that sets you up for success!

    This event is hosted by the Life & Health Sciences Link. Check out our Join a LOCUS Link! section to learn more.
