    Global Engagement and Exchanges

    • Volunteer in China July 2026!

      Looking for a meaningful way to spend Summer 2026 while gaining international experience and leadership skills? Check out Laurier's Summer Leadership Experience in China. Apply by March 15th! 

    • Late Exchange Application Deadline: May 25, 2026 at 4p.m.

      Thinking about going on exchange in Winter 2027? Don’t miss the late application round, opening March 27 and closing May 25, 2026 at 4:00 PM EDT. This is the final deadline for Winter 2027, with only a few spots remaining. Please note that Fall 2026 applications are now closed. Learn more here!

    • Student Exchange Program Information Sessions

      Thinking about applying to go on exchange during the 2026-2027 academic year? Attend an information session to learn more. Attending an information session (or watching a recording) is mandatory if you want to apply!

    • HerStart International Fellowship - Applications due February 2!

      HerStart International Fellows complete 3-month placements that meaningfully contribute to the goals of HerStart, while gaining hands-on professional international experience, expanding their global networks, and collaborating with our impact-driven teams in Ghana, Tanzania, and Uganda! HerStart fellows received a holistic benefits package, funded by the Government of Canada. Learn more and apply here!

    • Summer 2026 Exchanges

      Always thought about doing exchange but don't want to do a full semester? Consider participating in the Summer Exchange Program! It's just like our regular exchange program, except you'll be going abroad for a shorter period (one to eight weeks). Applications for summer 2026 are due at various times based on our partners schedules. Find more information here!

    • Study Abroad: Next Steps

      Going on exchange next year and wondering about next steps? Make sure you check out the Pre-Departure Checklist for more details!

    Broaden your horizons with global engagement programming and outbound mobility opportunities! Whether you travel abroad or participate from Laurier (or do both!), our programming provides many ways for you to be globally engaged.

    Opportunities Around the World

    Explore new places, experience new cultures and expand your intercultural knowledge with international mobility opportunities, including:

    • Studying abroad on exchange for a term or academic year.
    • Studying abroad in the summer with a summer semester exchange.
    • Traveling and learning with your classmates on a faculty-led program abroad.
    • International internships and volunteer opportunities.

    Learn About the World at Laurier

    A global education can start at home. Whether it's sharing another culture's food with new friends, watching international films, taking our intercultural certificate program, or enjoying the events of the annual International Education Week, our International at Home global engagement programming can take you around the world – without the carbon emissions.

    Financial Assistance

    There are a variety of internal and external scholarships and awards available to help you offset the cost of going abroad, including scholarships offered by host institutions. Be sure to review them to see if you're eligible!

    Career Guidance and Resources

    At the Laurier Career Centre you can learn how to make the most of your international experience to help build your career in Canada or abroad. A career consultant can provide you with one-on-one guidance to help you with your resumé and job search, interview preparation, and connecting your international experience with your career goals. Workshops and events are offered throughout the academic year and cover topics such as how to market your international experience and how to build a professional network.
