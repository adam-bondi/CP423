    Canadian Literature Reading List

    Print | PDF

    The following reading list for the English and Film Studies PhD program is provided by the department and is updated yearly to reflect changes in the discipline.

    For more information about reading lists, see the Comprehensive Area Exam page.

    Section I: Pre-confederation to 1867

    • Brooke, Frances. The History of Emily Montague.
    • Haliburton, Thomas. The Clockmaker.
    • Jameson, Anna Brownell. Winter Studies and Summer Rambles in Canada.
    • Moodie, Susanna. Roughing It in the Bush.
    • Richardson, John. Wacousta.
    • Traill, Catherine Parr. The Backwoods of Canada.

    Section II: Confederation to 1918

    *When the notation beside a poet’s name reads “selected poems,” students should read a wide selection of that poet’s work.

    • Carman, Bliss. Selected poems.
    • Connor, Ralph. The Man From Glengarry.
    • Crawford, Isabelle Valancy. Selected poems.
    • Duncan, Sara Jeanette. The Imperialist and “A Mother in India.”
    • Johnson, Pauline. Selected poems.
    • Lampman, Archibald. Selected poems.
    • Leacock, Stephen. Sunshine Sketches of a Little Town.
    • McClung, Nellie. Sowing Seeds in Danny.
    • Montgomery, Lucy Maud. Anne of Green Gables.
    • Roberts, Charles G.D. Selected poems.
    • Scott, Duncan Campbell. Selected poems.

    Section III: Between the Wars and After: 1918-1959

    • Birney, Earle. Selected poems.
    • Carr, Emily. Klee Wyck.
    • Glassco, John. Memoirs of Montparnasse.
    • Grove, Frederick Philip. Settlers of the Marsh.
    • Hiebert, Paul. Sarah Binks.

    • Layton, Irving. Selected poems.
    • Livesay, Dorothy. Selected poems.

    • Mitchell, W.O. Who Has Seen the Wind?
    • Ostenso, Martha. Wild Geese.
    • Page, P.K. Selected poems.
    • Pratt, E.J. Selected poems.
    • Ross, Sinclair. As For Me and My House.
    • Roy, Gabrielle. The Tin Flute.
    • Scott, F.R. Selected poems.
    • Smart, Elizabeth. By Grand Central Station I Sat Down and Wept.
    • Smith, A.J.M. Selected poems.
    • Watson, Sheila. The Double Hook.
    • Webb, Phyllis. Selected poems.
    • Wilson, Ethel. Swamp Angel.

    • Atwood, Margaret. Surfacing.

    • French, David. Leaving Home and Of the Fields, Lately.
    • Gallant, Mavis. Home Truths.

    • Kogawa, Joy. Obasan.
    • Kroetsch, Robert. Badlands.
      -----. Selected poems.
    • Laurence, Margaret. The Diviners.

    • Nichol, bp. Selected poems.

      ----. Selected poems.

    • Wiseman, Adele. Crackpot.

    • Armstrong, Jeanette. Slash.

    • Atwood. Survival.

      -----. Post-National Arguments. D

    • Grant, George. Lament for a Nation.

    • The Massey Report: A Royal Commission on the National Development and Arts, Letters, and Sciences, 1949-1951.

    • Scobie, Stephen. Signature, Event, Cantext.
