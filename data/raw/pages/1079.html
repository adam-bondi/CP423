    Study Spaces

    Grab a coffee or tea, crack open your laptop or textbook, settle in and get your study on. Whether you're looking for a quiet space for individual study or rooms to study with classmates, we have an abundance of study spaces to choose from across Laurier's campuses and locations.

    Campus Lounge Policies

    Conduct

    Laurier is committed to equity, diversity and inclusion (EDI) as part of its core values. Reflecting this, Laurier students on campus are expected to follow and uphold the Non-Academic Student Code of Conduct at all times. This includes respectful treatment of all persons and property within and around student lounges.

    Access to Space

    All Laurier buildings have regulation outlining their hours and access, which change to accommodate ongoing circumstance. When trying to access student lounges, it is recommended to always keep your OneCard on you.

    Find Your Study Space

    Brantford

    The study spaces on the Brantford campus are found throughout our revamped historical buildings within the city’s downtown core. You can enjoy a variety of different modern learning environments, each with that small community feel.

    Explore Brantford Spaces

    Kitchener

    Library

    • Location: Lyle S. Hallman Faculty of Social Work, lower level
    • Hours:
      • Monday and Friday 8 a.m. – 5 p.m.
      • Tuesday to Thursday 8 a.m. – 6 p.m.
      • Closed Saturday and Sunday
    • Restrictions: Quiet study space; OneCard required to access

    This space is an open area with seating options for individuals or groups. It has medium traffic and low noise levels.

    Milton

    The study spaces on the Milton campus are integrated across the entire first floor of our building with a variety of study spaces to choose from: in an open area, semi-private or private. With ample seating to choose from, you're steps away from your classes, and campus amenities as you study and relax on campus.

    Explore Milton Spaces

    Waterloo

    The study spaces on the Waterloo campus are integrated in the various buildings nestled into one large city block. With ample seating to choose from, you're steps away from your classes, residence and campus amenities as you study on campus.
