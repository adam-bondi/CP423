    International Student Leaders

    Our International Student Leaders (ISLs) are upper-year students who can give you a student's perspective an insight into life at Laurier. Every summer, the ISLs connect with incoming international students to:

    • Welcome you to Laurier.
    • Answer your questions.
    • Provide you with more information about being a Golden Hawk.

    Throughout the school year, ISLs plan events, host student gatherings and help engage you with all that Laurier has to offer. 

    ISLs are a great resource if you have questions about the student experience or if you are looking for ways to get involved on campus.

    Meet this year's Undergraduate and Graduate ISLs.

    Become an ISL

    We are always looking for students to join our ISL team. The job posting for ISLs in Waterloo and Brantford opens each year in February. Upper-year international and domestic students from all programs are welcome to apply on your campus of study. 

    This is a one-year position that begins each year in May. We will provide training webinars over the summer. To be an ISL for more than one year, you will need to reapply each year.
