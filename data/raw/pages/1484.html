    Course Offerings

    Please use the LORIS Browse Classes to check course offerings in Kinesiology for 2026/2027. Schedules are subject to change and should be checked on LORIS, where location information can also be found. Full, official academic information can be found on the academic calendars. It is your responsibility to check the appropriate calendar.

    Unless otherwise specified, classes take place on Laurier's Waterloo campus. 

    If you would like to take more than 2.5 credits in one term, you will have to fill out the Overload Request form: Faculty of Science.

    Courses for Non-KIN Students

    Kinesiology and Physical Education is offering two courses for non-Kinesiology students: KP161: Fundamentals of Motor Learning and Control and KP484: Group Dynamics in Team Environments in Fall 2026. Please contact the department for more information.

    Experiential Learning with SYNDAVERS

    Our anatomy and physiology labs are equipped with the very first full-sized synthetic cadavers in Canada.  The two syndavers are complete with every bone, joint, muscle, organ, and tendon found in the human body, as well as major parts of the nervous and vascular system, which can be manipulated, disassembled, and reassembled so students can learn how parts work together. 

    In our program, students don't just memorize anatomy, here you learn it by doing, and by getting involved.
