    Summer Exchanges

    If you're interested in a shorter study abroad opportunity, consider going abroad during the summer. 

    Study at any of Laurier's partner institutions that offer summer programs, where you can earn credits towards your Laurier degree while still paying your Laurier tuition. A summer opportunity: 

    • Can average from one to eight weeks long. 
    • Allows you to earn the equivalent of 0.5 to 1.0 credits towards your Laurier degree. 

    The summer opportunities available to you include: 

    • Summer exchange program
    • Summer cultural excursions

    Summer Exchange Program

    The summer exchange program is like our exchange program, except you'll be going abroad for a shorter period (one to eight weeks). 

    Applications are due at variious times based on our partners schedule, so please check Journey program postings to go abroad in summer 2026. 

    Before you apply for summer exchange, you must watch a recorded summer exchanges information session (Passcode: Summer2026*). 

    You must have already completed 10 Laurier credits by the end of Winter 2026 to apply for Summer 2026.

    You can participate in both a summer semester abroad and full-term exchange only with specific permission from your department.

    If you are graduating in the fall, you can study abroad in the summer; however, there is no guarantee that the credit transfer process will be completed before the deadline to apply to graduate.

    Apply Through Journey

    1. Go to Journey.
    2. Select the purple Laurier Student/Faculty/Staff - Sign-In With Your Laurier Account button. 
    3. Enter your Laurier SSO credentials. 
    4. Select Search Programs. 
    5. Select Summer from the "Term(s)" field.
    6. Click Apply Search. 
    7. Select the host institution you wish to apply for, then click Apply. 
    8. Follow the prompts and complete the fields. 
    9. Check back frequently as new opportunities will be posted between November and Mid-April as we receive news from our partners.

     

    Documentation and Offer Letters

    You must submit your required documents to Laurier International by our posted deadline, which is different for each partner school. If your application is complete and you are accepted, we will contact you with an offer letter and next steps. 

    After you submit your completed forms and waivers, we will nominate you to the host institution.

    Nomination to Host Institution

    Once you are nominated to the host institution: 

    1. Complete the application for your host institution. 
    2. Submit your documents directly to the host institution by their deadline (will vary depending on which school you choose). 

    Note: Your acceptance to the program does not guarantee your acceptance to the host institution.

    Acceptance Letter from Host Institution

    If you are accepted, the host institution will send you an acceptance letter, you must: 

    • Submit any additional required documents or information by the institution's deadline, if requested. 
    • Email a copy of your acceptance letter to exchange@wlu.ca. 
    • Complete mandatory pre-departure sessions as outlined in your offer letter.

    Course Credit Transfer Agreement (CCTA) Form

    Meet with your academic advisor to complete the CCTA form, which matches your courses taken abroad with the credits on your Laurier transcript. 

    The number of credits you can earn will depend on the program and opportunity.

    For summer travel, your CCTA Form is submitted once you are registered in your courses abroad.

     

    You will pay Laurier tuition and fees for the total number of credits you will be earning. If you decide to take fewer credits once you are abroad, you will not receive a refund from Laurier. 

    Financial awards and/or supplements may be offered on a case-by-case basis from the host institution.

    Do not make any travel arrangements until you have received your official acceptance from the host institution.

    Before your departure: 

    1. You must complete Laurier International's mandatory pre-departure sessions as outlined in your offer letter. 
    2. Complete the additional SHERM health and safety forms in Journey. 

    You are responsible for your travel preparations, which can include: 

    • booking your flight
    • arranging accommodations
    • purchasing travel insurance
    • arranging for a visa or study permit, if required
    • getting or renewing your passport

    Summer Cultural Excursions

    Make the most of your summer and develop international experience and skills. With summer cultural excursion opportunities, you pay tuition directly to the host institution and with certain programs, you may even earn Laurier credits. 

    Our summer cultural excursion program is currently on pause, but check back for future opportunities.
