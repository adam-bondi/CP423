    Confirm Your Registration Date and Time Ticket

    Print | PDF

    Course registration dates and times are based on the number of credits you've completed or are in the process of completing, including transfer credits, to determine your year level. 

    Follow the guide below or view the "Determining Your Time Ticket, Program and Year Level" video: 

    Use LORIS to Determine Your Time Ticket

    To find your own personal time ticket: 

    1. Log in to LORIS. 
    2. From the main menu, select the Student Services tab. 
    3. Click Registration Dashboard on the Registration and Class Schedule card.
    4. Choose Prepare for Registration. 
    5. Select your first term. 
    6. Select Continue to view your time ticket and see information about your Registration Status.

     

    Note: The time ticket that is generated by LORIS cannot be adjusted. Your time is considered firm/final.

    Determine Your Year Level

    At Laurier, your year level is based on the number of credits you’ve completed, not the number of years you’ve been enrolled. This impacts your registration date, the courses you can register for, and eligibility for certain course levels.

    • Year 1 (Less than 4.0 credits)  
    • Year 2 (4.0 to 9.0 credits)  
    • Year 3 (9.25 to 14.0 credits)  
    • Year 4 (Honours students only with a minimum of 14.25 credits)  
    • Year 5 (Double degree students with 20.25+ credits)  

    The year level can be viewed through LORIS under Student Services > Registration Dashboard (under the Registration and Class Schedule card) > Prepare for Registration > Select a Term.

    It’s possible to have a split year level. For example, being considered Year 1 for the Fall term and Year 2 for the Winter term, depending on how many credits you’re completing in the current term.

    The system will automatically update your year level multiple times per day as you register in more courses. If you're encountering restrictions for Winter term courses that require a higher year level, it may be because your current registration hasn’t yet triggered the system update.

    Tip: Continue checking LORIS regularly. Once your registration meets the credit threshold for the next year level, your access to additional courses should open.
