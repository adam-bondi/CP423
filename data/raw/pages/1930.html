    Dollars and Sense: Money Management

    Dollars and Sense is Laurier's money management program designed to help decrease your financial stress and increase your financial wellness. We are dedicated to providing the necessary information, resources and tools so that you can make the financial decisions that are right for you.

    Get Financial Coaching

    Improve your financial behaviour and habits with the help of a financial coach. A financial coach can help you with budgeting, setting and achieving a financial goal, exploring financial resources, applying for scholarships, awards and bursaries, or addressing an immediate financial concern. 

    Appointments are generally 30 minutes on Microsoft Teams. 

    Book a Financial Coaching Appointment

    Earn the Dollars and Sense Money Management Certificate

    Through this certificate program, you'll learn about budgeting, using credit, how to invest and financial planning. 

    Once you complete the certificate, you can add it to your Laurier Experience Record. 

    Learn more about Dollars and Sense. 

    Boost Your Financial Literacy

    Learning how to budget and how to use credit wisely are some ways that you can boost your financial literacy. 

    We've gathered practical tips to help you manage your money and to help set you up for financial stability while you're at Laurier and for life after you're done here. 

    Explore our money management resources.

    Workshops and Presentations

    Our workshops and presentations are your ticket to a confident and savvy financial future. 

    We invite financial experts to speak to you about ways you can enhance your money management skills so that you can level up your financial literacy and take control of your financial future. 

    Register for our workshops and presentations.
