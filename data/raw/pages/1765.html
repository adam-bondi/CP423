    Laurier Orientation:
    Graduate Student

    Graduate Student Orientation (GSO) at Laurier is your opportunity to connect, explore, and prepare for success as you begin or continue your graduate journey. Join us to meet fellow students, faculty, and staff, take part in informative sessions and campus tours, and discover the key resources and academic supports available to you.

    Whether you're new to Laurier or building on your experience here, GSO will help you start your graduate studies with confidence and clarity while making connections to help build your community from day one.

    Stay up to date by checking your @mylaurier.ca email regularly and following @lauriergradstudents on Instagram for the latest information.
