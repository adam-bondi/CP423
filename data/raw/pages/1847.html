    Staff Listing

    The friendly and dedicated team at International Student Support is here to help you make the most of your time at Laurier. Our goal is to ensure you have a fulfilling and successful experience. Whether you're seeking advice, support, or just a friendly chat, we're here for you. Email us to set up an appointment, to get answers to your questions, receive support, or simply to say hi!

    Connect with our: 

    • International Student Advisors (ISAs)
    • Administrative Staff

    We look forward to connecting with you and being a part of your Laurier experience. Don't hesitate to reach out - we're here to help!

    International Student Advisors

    Our International Student Advisors (ISA) are available to assist all international students in Waterloo and Brantford. Advisors provide support, answer questions and help you find the resources you need. They can help you with:

    • academic planning
    • immigration information and support
    • adapting to life in Canada
    • health and wellness
    • career development
    • financial challenges
    • university procedures and policies

    If you would like to meet with an ISA, stop by their weekly drop-in hours at the Laurier International office or book an appointment. 

    Drop-in schedule for Spring 2026:

    • Mondays from 10a.m. - 12p.m. (virtual)
    • Tuesdays from 2-4p.m.(in-person, Waterloo campus, Arts A Wing, 2A01)
    • Thursdays from 10a.m. - 12p.m. (virtual)

    When you join virtual drop-in sessions, you may be in the waiting room for a while - this is normal! Our advisors let one student in at a time to ensure privacy and will speak with you as soon as possible.
