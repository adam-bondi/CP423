    Domestic Graduate Students

    Master's and doctoral students will be charged full- or part-time tuition based on their status at the time of admission. Students who successfully petition to change their registration status may have their tuition fees adjusted as a result. Incidental fees will be charged according to a student's registration status in a particular term.

    Notes:

    • Fees classified as non-funded: Programs for which Laurier as an institution does not receive government funding. As a result, these programs are not eligible for any exemption benefit programs (including tuition waiver and senior exemption benefit) or OSAP funding.
    • Graduate students can apply to the graduate student tuition payment plan each term.

    • All international undergraduate students are required to pay a tuition deposit. The tuition deposit is not an additional fee; the amount will be applied to your first-term tuition balance once you enrol in courses and your invoice is issued.

    Jay Treaty   

    Wilfrid Laurier University honours the Jay Treaty 1794 by offering Native American students from the continental United States domestic tuition rates.  To qualify, students must be pursuing an undergraduate or graduate program at Laurier and have validated their indigenous identity with Indigenous Initiatives.  

    Once confirmed, students will be eligible for the domestic tuition for the entirety of their studies at Laurier.  The application of domestic rates will begin as of the term when the validation by Indigenous Initiatives takes place. 

    For more information on this process, please connect with the Office of Indigenous Initiatives.
