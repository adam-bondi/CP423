    Prism Resources

    Bridging the gap between technology and students

    Welcome Prism members!

    Prism was established in 1991 as Providing Resources for Information Systems Management. We are a student-run program of Lazaridis School of Business and Economics, providing services and support to Laurier students under three pillars: Equipment rentals, Training Courses and optimal Study Spaces.

    Our mission is to ‘bridge the gap’ between technology and students by providing resources that enable our members to excel, both during their time at Laurier and beyond. 

     

    Exam Rental Policy  

    During the exam period (July 31 – August 14), only the following items will be available for rental:  

    1. Laptops (Dell and MacBook)  
    2. iPads  
    3. Chargers  
    4. Calculators 
    5. Portable Monitors  
    6. Keyboards 

    Members interested in renting any of the above equipment are required to email a screenshot of their exam schedule to support@prismresources.ca before proceeding with the rental.  

    All rented items must be returned within 24 hours after your last exam. Returns can be made either at the desk during our operating hours (10:00 AM to 2:00 PM) or through our return lockers. If using the lockers, please ensure the equipment is securely locked and take a photo of it being placed inside.  

    Important:  

    All other equipment must be returned by July 28, 2026, to avoid late fees. To accommodate this, the due date for any currently rented items will be updated to July 28.  

    For any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at support@prismresources.ca.  

     

    Rental Returns: 

    All equipment must be returned by the due date specified on your order. Overdue equipment will incur late fines of $5 per calendar day, as per the Prism Terms and Conditions. If you are unable to return equipment by the due date at 2:00 PM, please email support@prismresources.ca to coordinate your return. 

    Rental Service: 

    The rental service for the Spring 2026 semester will be available to members starting the week of May 18, 2026. The hours of operations will be Monday-Friday, from 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM. 

     

    Training Service:

    Prism training courses will begin on the week of May 25, 2026 and end for the exam period. Courses will continue to be offered in person and virtually. Please keep an eye out for our training schedule by visiting prismresources.ca, or following us on Instagram @prismresources

    If you have any questions about our services for next semester, please email support@prismresources.ca.
