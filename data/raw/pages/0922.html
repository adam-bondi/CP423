    Services

    At the Career Centre, we are dedicated to empowering and guiding you on your path to success. View the Experiential Learning and Career Development Strategic Plan for more information on our vision, mission, goals and values.

    Our comprehensive range of services are designed to equip you with the skills, resources and connections to gain the experience, confidence and knowledge needed to start your career after Laurier. 

    Explore our services: 

    • Book an appointment. 
    • Access individualized career resources. 
    • Attend workshops. 
    • Attend recruitment and networking events. 
    • View job postings for Laurier students and alumni. 

    Appointments

    Book an Appointment

    To book an appointment: 

    1. Log in to Navigator.
    2. Click the Career Centre Icon. 
    3. Select Appointments.
    4. Choose your appointment type. 

    Appointments are available in person or online and are generally 15 to 45 minutes in length. 

    Additional same-day brief appointments open at 9 a.m. each day.

    Types of Appointments

    Improve your resumé or cover letter with personalized feedback and tips. Ideal for students actively applying to roles as well as those just starting out.  

    Pro tip: Attend the Resumé Writing and Cover Letter workshops to get the most out of your appointment.

    Alternatively, for topical feedback, upload your resumé or cover letter to receive email feedback within two business days.

    Career consulting appointments are collaborative sessions designed to help you explore career possibilities. Our career consultants provide guidance and support on topics such as: 

    • Exploring your interests, values, skills and personality.
    • Selecting or changing a career direction.
    • Choosing a major or area of study aligned with your goals.
    • Gaining meaningful volunteer or work experience.
    • Identifying jobs and employers of potential interest to you.
    What to Expect 

    Career development is an ongoing process that may involve several meetings over time. During your appointments, you and the career consultant will collaborate to explore your goals and identify action items. You'll then work on these steps between meetings to continue your progress. Career assessments may be introduced as an option. 

    Your Commitment

    If you engage in career consulting, it will require your commitment to: 

    • Attend an initial appointment (45 minutes).
    • Follow through on identified action items and next steps in your research.
    • Complete optional career assessments (approximately 30 minutes).
    • Attend a follow-up session if you get stuck in your career research and decision-making, or need to debrief an assessment (45 minutes). 

    In Job Search appointments, we can discuss:

    • Job search and networking for summer, part-time or full-time positions.
    • How to access the hidden job market and leverage LinkedIn.
    • Job and salary negotiation.

    In Interview Coaching appointments, we can discuss:

    • How to highlight your strongest qualifications
    • How to craft effective responses to common interview questions
    • Managing nerves so you can perform your best

    In a Mock Interview appointment, you will:

    • Participate in a simulated interview with a career consultant 
    • Practice your answers to common interview questions
    • Receive feedback and tips for improving at the end of the appointment

    To practice for an upcoming job interview, include the job description of the position you are interviewing for in your appointment request. The consultant can then ask targeted questions based on the job posting. 

    In Further Education appointments, we can discuss:

    • Information on professional schools including faculties of education, medical school, law school and others.
    • Graduate school applications, interviews and tests.
    • How to complete statements of interest required for graduate schools.
    • Post-degree programs at college.

    Appointment Policy

    • Career Centre appointments are available for Laurier students, alumni, faculty and staff only.
    • You have a maximum number of three appointments per term, including same-day appointments and booked appointments.
    • Students who are no-show for appointments or cancel twice will no longer be able to book appointments in Navigator.

    Individualized Services

    Alongside services for all students and alumni, the Career Centre offers tailored programming and resources that address the unique career‑related needs of specific groups and communities, recognizing that identities often overlap.

    • 2SLGBTQQIA+ students and alumni
    • Black and racialized students and alumni
    • First-year students
    • Graduate Students
    • Indigenous students and alumni
    • International students
    • Online students
    • Students and alumni with disabilities

    Recruiting and Networking Events

    Workshops and Certificates

    The Career Centre offers a range of workshops such as career planning, job search preparation, further education and more. By attending workshops, you can work towards earning Career Development Certificates.

    Employer Recruitment

    These events provide you with the opportunity to connect with potential employers to learn more about their organization and job opportunities.

    Career Events

    Learn more about potential career options and paths that may be of interest to you. Our career events also provide you with the opportunity to network and open up future opportunities to connect with employers.
