    Drop-In Help

    Print | PDF

    Students can obtain assistance understanding concepts from a variety of quantitative-based and problem-solving subject areas – mathematical fundamentals, algebra, calculus, proofs, linear algebra, financial math, probability and statistics, economics & accounting, computer coding, and chemistry.

    Our drop-in sessions provide an informal peer-to-peer learning environment. No appointment is needed. 

    For spring term, we will be offering a combination of in-person help sessions on the Waterloo campus, plus virtual help sessions.

    The regular drop-in help session schedule is in effect from Monday May 11 until Thursday July 23; additional sessions will be scheduled during the spring term exam period.

    Get help with: 

    • general mathematics questions at an introductory level
    • any 100-level mathematics course, including SC101 and DATA100
    • selected 200-level mathematics and statistics courses: MA200, MA201, MA205, MA222, MA250, MA287, ST230, ST231, ST259/260
    • introductory applied statistics courses offered by any academic department: BU/EC255, EC245, EC285, PS295/296, GESC254/258, PO218, SY382, CC327, KP390, etc.
    • introductory economics and accounting concepts and methods for EC120, EC140 and BU127
    • coding support for CP103, CP104 and CP164
    • chemistry support for CH110/130, CH111/131, CH202/206, and CH203/205/207

    Math and Statistics Drop-in Help

    • On campus, room LH1018 Lazaridis Hall, Waterloo
      • Mondays, Tuesdays, Wednesdays - 12 p.m. to 4 p.m.
    • Virtual, via Zoom on MyLS
      • Sundays, Wednesdays - 7:30 p.m. to 9:30 p.m.

    Accounting, Economics, and Coding Support Drop-in Help

    • Many of our peer tutors providing math and statistics support are also able to assist with concepts covered by introductory accounting, economics and coding courses.  Check the peer tutor schedule posted on our sites in MyLearningSpace.

    Chemistry Drop-in Help

    • On campus, room BA104 Bricker Academic, Waterloo
      • Tuesdays, Thursdays - 2 p.m. to 5 p.m.
      • Wednesdays - 4 p.m. to 6 p.m.

    Connecting Virtually

    Refer to the our pages on MyLearningSpace for full details, including peer tutor schedules and links for virtual sessions held via Zoom:

    • Math & Stats Learning Support - Waterloo
    • Course Learning Support - Brantford
    • Course Learning Support - Milton

    If you do not see the page in your list of courses: 

    1. Use the Advanced Search link. 
    2. Change the Semester option box to None. 
    3. Select the search/microscope icon. 

    If you are still not seeing the page, you can self-register for it: 

    1. Go to the MyLearningSpace home page. 
    2. Select Self Registration. 
    3. Select the relevant "Math & Stats" or "Course Learning Support" course to enrol.
