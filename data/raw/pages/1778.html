    Let’s Kick Off Your Golden Hawk Journey!

    Get ready for a jam-packed week of activities to introduce you to life as a Golden Hawk on the Brantford campus.

    We have a ton of orientation events happening Sept. 7 to Sept. 12. On this page, you'll find detailed event information and be introduced to Shinerama!

    Looking to find your way around campus? You can do so by using our interactive campus map.
