    Martin Luther University College Academic Advising

    When contacting your advisor, be sure to attach a list of all completed courses from LORIS and your program requirements for undergraduate and graduate levels. Program requirements can be used from any year’s academic calendar during which you were enrolled in your program of study. 

    Before submitting any special requests to the Luther Academic Advisory Committee (AAC), please email the academic advisor regarding your request. Ensure that you email from your Laurier email address and that you include your student number. 

    Note: While advising is available at Luther, it is the responsibility of students to ensure that they are properly registered and meet the course requirements as well as the requirements of the program.
