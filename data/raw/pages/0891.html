    Job Search Websites

    In addition to the jobs listed for Laurier students and alumni in Navigator, the following websites may be of use in conducting your job search:

    Job Search Engines

    Canada Indeed
    Eluta Canada 
    LinkedIn’s Job Portal 
    Magnet
    HiringCafe
    Monster.ca
    Simply Hired 
    Talent.com
    Workopolis
    Headhunters Directory - Ontario recruitment firms, headhunters, recruiters, executive search firms, staffing and employment agencies

    Information for your Career Search

    Glassdoor - view employee reviews, salaries and interview questions 
    Service Canada Job Bank - search for jobs, explore careers and use the post-secondary career tool
    TalentEgg

    Industry-Specific Job Postings

    Agricultural/Food Industry

    AgCareers - providing global talent solutions in agriculture and food
    Careers in Food - jobs in the food industry

    Cultural/Heritage/Arts/Music

    WorkInCulture - non-profit arts service organization that supports the professional lives of Ontario’s artists, creatives and cultural workers
    Canadian Museums Association - jobs in heritage and Young Canada Works Program
    Culture Works - a job board for the cultural sector administered by The Cultural Human Resources Council
    Professional Association of Canadian Theatres - job board
    Orchestras Canada - jobs in music

    Consumer Packaged Goods

    CPG Connect - consumer packaged goods jobs

    Education

    Apply to Education - job search engine for teaching jobs
    EduJobsCanada - jobs in Canadian Colleges and Universities (faculty positions, postdocs, research, professional and management positions)
    University Affairs - job board for Canadian post-secondary positions
    AcademicWork.ca - job board hosted by the Canadian Association of University Teachers
    Times Higher Education - academic and university jobs across the world

    Environmental

    Climate-Related Job Boards - collection of job boards
    Canadian Forests - forestry jobs in Canada
    Conservation Ontario - jobs in Ontario’s 36 conservation authorities
    EcoCanada - resource for environmental jobs, certification and training
    GoodWork.ca - jobs in environment, nature, conservation, sustainability, local food, and green business across Canada
    WorkCabin - job search engine for green and environmental jobs

    Government and Policy

    Government of Canada Jobs - jobs in the federal government
    Ontario Public Service - jobs in the provincial government
    Association of Municipalities of Ontario - municipal jobs in Ontario
    Civic Jobs - Canada's largest municipal job board
    Municipal World - Canada's largest municipal job marketplace
    Spring Board - newsletter with policy jobs in Toronto

    Information Management

    UofT's Faculty of Information Job Site - the University of Toronto’s Faculty of Information information management job posting board accessible to the public.

    Insurance

    Insurance Career Connections - careers in the insurance industry
    InsuranceWorks - insurance job site

    International

    MyWorldAbroad – website of global career information including links to job boards: access through Navigator > Career Centre > Career Resources
    International Experience Canada – acquire a youth mobility visa to work and travel abroad
    Government of Canada – volunteering and internships abroad
    Ontario Council for International Cooperation – opportunities in international organizations locally and abroad
    OverseasJobs.com – overseas jobs and international employment opportunities
    AIESEC – Laurier club offers opportunities to teach, volunteer or intern abroad

    Internships

    Career Edge - Canadian internship opportunities

    Law

    Clear Legal Jobs - Canadian legal jobs, such as legal assistant, legal support, legal secretary, law clerk, paralegal, solicitor, articling student, lawyer, etc.

    Media/Communications

    Canadian Public Relations Society (CPRS) - jobs in PR and communications
    Gaulin Media - jobs in journalism, media and communications industry
    MediaCareers.ca     - marketing, arts and media jobs
    National Media Awards Foundation - journalism and media job postings

    Non-Profit Sector

    Bmeaningful - career site for social impact job postings and career information
    Charity Village - employment search engine for opportunities in the nonprofit sector
    Work in Non-Profits - paid and volunteer opportunities in the non-profit sector across Canada

    Natural Resources

    Career Mine - North American mining jobs

    Research

    Mitacs - resources for research opportunities

    Science and Health

    Bioinformatics Canada - bioinformatics jobs across Canada
    BioTalent Canada - jobs in Canada’s bio-economy
    Canadian Association of Community Health Centres - health-related jobs
    PharmaJob - pharma-related jobs
    Health Careers (Ontario Hospital Association)
    Caring Support - Canadian healthcare jobs

    Tech/User Experience

    Waterloo Tech Jobs - job portal for Waterloo Region’s tech companies
    UXR Hunt - Canadian and international UX Research jobs
