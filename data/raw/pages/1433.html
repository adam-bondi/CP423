    Core Values

    As a biology student at Laurier, the following core values will drive your study:

    • Academic excellence: Our commitment to high standards of academic achievement and integrity.
    • Creativity: The creation of new knowledge.
    • Collegiality: Fostering a learning community of students, staff and faculty.
    • Dedication: To creating the conditions that allow all of our students to articulate, refine, and achieve their goals.
    • Integration: Of form and function at every level of biological organization.

    Inclusion, Diversity, Equity and Accessibility (IDEA)

    The IDEA committee provides resources and educates members of the Biology department community about issues surrounding IDEA in STEM.

    Get Involved

    There are many ways you can get involved as a biology student. Check out some of the student organizations below. For master's student, visit the Graduate Student Representatives page.

    The purpose of the Laurier Biology Association (LBA) is to provide a source of unity to all biology students across the many branches of the subject and facilitate interactions between other science-based faculties. Joining the LBA is easy! Simply email a Clubs & Associations contact with your name and Laurier email so we may add you to our club mailing list to receive updates on events and meetings. Then come out to our events to begin enjoying the benefits of being part of a top Laurier club.

    The Faculty of Science Students’ Association (FOSSA) is a group of science students that hope to provide a diverse and enlightening experience to the Laurier science community. See our website or email us.
