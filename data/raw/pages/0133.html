    Service Laurier

    Service Laurier provides front-line service on behalf of Enrolment Services, including Undergraduate Admissions, Student Accounts, Financial Aid and OSAP, Student Awards, Records and Registration.

    We aim to provide equitable service across all campuses, allowing students to connect with us from where they are rather than where we are through our chatbot, virtual appointments, webform, and phone. In-person drop-in is the only campus-specific service modality available at the Waterloo and Brantford campuses. 

    We are focused on supporting current students through the entire student lifecycle from initial inquiry through to convocation.

    Notice About Financial Holds 

    Financial holds are the most common hold type and are applied when a student has failed to pay tuition and fees by the invoice deadline.

    If you have a financial hold, find more information and next steps on the Holds page.

    For our Statement of Financial Responsibility, visit the Tuition and Fees page.

    Connect With Us

    Under the Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act (FIPPA), university faculty and staff are prohibited from discussing, changing, or divulging a student or applicant’s personal information (including grades, class attendance, information about course progress, and application status) with anyone other than the student. Please have the student contact us directly.

    Search questions 24 hours a day, 7 days a week through our Chatbot!

    All virtual appointments are booked online and released one week in advance.

    Meet with a Service Laurier Advisor

    A Service Laurier Advisor is the best way for current students or alumni to connect with our Enrolment Services team, including student accounts, financial aid and OSAP, student awards, records and registration. 

    Service Hours
    • Monday to Thursday, 9:30 a.m. to 3 p.m. ET
    • Friday, 9:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. ET

    Meet with a Prospective Student Specialist

    A Prospective Student Specialist helps with complex undergraduate admission inquiries related to education from multiple curriculums, academic upgrading and impact on admissions or offers, transfer students, etc. General inquiries should be directed to chooselaurier@wlu.ca. 

    Service Hours
    • Mondays and Thursdays, 9:30 a.m. to 3:30p.m. ET

    Appointment Limitations

    We kindly ask that you limit appointments to once per week to ensure fair access to all students across our campuses. If you have scheduled multiple appointments within a week, Service Laurier reserves the right to cancel all subsequent bookings after your initial appointment.

    In-person drop-in is available on a first-case basis, for general or urgent inquiries. Please note that while we are happy to assist in person, some inquiries may require follow-up by a staff member and may not be resolved immediately.

     

    Service Hours

    • Monday to Thursday, 10:00 a.m. to 4 p.m. ET
    • Waterloo: Friday, 1:30 to 4 p.m. ET, Brantford: Friday, 1:30 to 4:00 p.m. ET
    • Closed daily for lunch, 12:30 to 1:30 p.m. ET

    To arrange document or transcript pick-up outside of these hours but within university operational hours, email documents@wlu.ca (24 hours' notice is required).

    Our Service Commitment

    Service Laurier staff are available to answer your questions and provide support.

    We hold ourselves and each other accountable to our service values to be compassionate, consistent, efficient, authentic and inclusive.

    We aim to provide a helpful, friendly, and positive experience. We will act with integrity, respect, and accountability in all interactions with stakeholders including applicants, students, alumni, parents, visitors, and colleagues within and outside the Laurier community.

    Please engage with us in a similar manner.
