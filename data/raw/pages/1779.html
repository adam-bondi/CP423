    Laurier Fall Orientation:
    Waterloo Undergraduate Schedule

    Get ready for a jam-packed week of activities to introduce you to life as a Golden Hawk on the Waterloo campus.

    We have a ton of orientation events happening Sept. 7 to Sept. 12. On this page, you'll find detailed event information and be introduced to Shinerama.

    Looking to find your way around campus? You can do so by using our interactive campus map.

    Where: The Quad
    When: 11 a.m. to 5:30 p.m.
    Host: Your Students' Union and Laurier 101

    Intended Audience: Laurier Residence Students

    One of the first things you'll do as a residence student is attend your Community Meeting on move-in day! This is your chance to meet your Residence Life Don, get to know the other students on your floor, and start making connections from day one. You'll learn about your new home, discover the supports and resources available to you, and get a preview of all the exciting events and programs happening throughout Orientation Week. Bring your questions, introduce yourself to your neighbours, and take the first step toward building a fun, welcoming, and supportive community together.

     Where: Your Residence Life Don will communicate this information to you upon your arrival, and it will be posted around your community with the meeting location.
     When: 5 to 6 p.m.
     Host: Department of Residence

    Intended Audience: Laurier Residence Students

    All residence students are required to attend the Building Meeting on move-in day. This is your opportunity to meet the Residence staff who will support you throughout the year, including your Dons, Residence Education Advisor (REA), Residence Impact Coach (RIC), and Residence Life Area Coordinator (RLAC).

    You'll also connect with students from across your building, learn about residence expectations, discover the many resources available to support your success, and hear about exciting opportunities to get involved throughout the year. Together, we'll set the foundation for a respectful, welcoming, and inclusive community where everyone can thrive.

    By the end of the meeting, you'll:
    Know who to contact when you have questions or need support.
    Understand the importance of respect, responsibility, and community living.
    Learn about opportunities to get involved, including the First Year Leadership Program.

     When: 6:30 to 7:30 p.m.
