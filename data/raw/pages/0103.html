    Incoming Graduate Student Support

    Starting graduate school is exciting, and there’s a lot to get ready for. From important deadlines to new systems, expectations, and opportunities, the weeks and months leading up to your first classes can feel like a lot to navigate. That’s where ASPIRE comes in. We’re here to help you feel prepared, confident, and supported every step of the way.

    This Incoming Graduate Student Support page is a great place to start. You’ll find key dates, essential resources, and step-by-step guidance to help you stay on track and make informed decisions before the term begins.

    We offer a variety of workshops (Laurier Crash Course) designed specifically for incoming graduate students. These sessions are a great opportunity to learn about campus resources, connect with support services and other new Golden Hawks, and start building your graduate community.  A centralized Graduate Student Orientation at the beginning of the semester provides an introduction to graduate student life, helping you navigate key services, understand expectations, and feel more confident and connected as you begin your graduate journey.

    Whether you’re looking to get organized, build your network, or feel more prepared, we’re here to support you every step of the way.

    You've Accepted Your Offer... Now What?

    You've accepted your offer to graduate studies at Laurier, here are some next steps you can take to continue on your graduate student journey. If you are an incoming international student, make sure you check out the International Student Support office for immigration assistance and travel information. 

    Register for Your Courses

    Graduate course registration is now open. 

    There are some things that you'll need to consider before course registration opens. Whether you're writing a thesis or doing the coursework options for your grad studies, preparation is key: 

    • For registration tips, see the Graduate Course Registration page.
    • For comprehensive video tutorials of the registration process, view the registration playlist on YouTube.

    Next step: Register for your courses on LORIS, Laurier's information system for all things registration and finances.

    Explore and Apply for Funding

    Whether you're pursuing a master's or PhD, navigating tuition payment can be overwhelming. That's why we've gathered some tips on how you can explore scholarships, bursaries and awards, and the steps you can take to apply for the awards.

    • Complete the Scholarships and Bursaries Student Profile form so that you'll be considered for funding opportunities. 
    • Check out the Graduate Funding and Awards page for eligibility, application requirements and deadlines. 
    • Ask and apply for scholarships available through your program.
    • Learn about the Graduate Tuition Payment Plan Program.
    • Get familiar with what's included in your health and dental plan. 

    For students with a funding offer, view and accept your funding on LORIS.

    Paying Your Tuition Fees

    Pay your upcoming term invoice (tuition fees) by the due date to avoid late fees. Invoices are issued and can be found on LORIS once tuition fees have been assessed. Fees are assessed one month before the start of each semester.

    • Estimate your tuition invoice with a breakdown of fees
    • Determine tuition due dates
    • Pay your term invoice by the first day of class

    Preparation Checklists

    Complete the tasks in each category to ensure you're fully prepared for day one.

    Set Up Your Tech

    • Activate your MyLaurier student email to participate in orientation and beyond.
    • Activate your Laurier Zoom account to participate in online orientation events and more.
    • Familiarize yourself with MyLearningSpace to access course content and more.
    • Check out Laurier's Tech Guide to set up Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), connect to your Wi-Fi, and download available software to your device.
    • Download Microsoft Teams.
    • Install Microsoft Office 365 for free.

    Campus Essentials

    • Download the SafeHawk app.
    • Complete the Student Self-Identification (ID). It’s confidential, voluntary, and supports our equity, diversity, and inclusion goals at Laurier.
    • Apply for a Parking Permit if you're planning to drive to campus.
    • Upload your Laurier OneCard photo, this is your identification card and more on campus.
    • Review textbook list and purchase course materials.
    • Register with Accessible Learning (if applicable)
    • Check out Indigenous Student Services supports (if applicable)
    • Find out more about the different support options available to you.

    Funding and Financial Aid

    • Complete the Scholarships and Bursaries Student Profile.
    • Check out the Graduate Funding and Awards page for eligibility, application requirements and deadlines.
    • Apply for the Graduate Tuition Payment Plan Program by the deadline and pay your tuition in three equal instalments.
    • Learn about your health and dental plan.
    • For those with a funding offer, view and accept funding on LORIS.
