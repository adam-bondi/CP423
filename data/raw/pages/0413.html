    Milton Advising

    You want to reach your academic goals and we are here to help. 

    Your Milton Campus academic advisors are committed to providing high-quality, inclusive, comfortable and confidential academic support to undergraduate students.

    We encourage you to connect with an academic advisor at least once a year to ensure that you remain on track with your degree requirements. 

    Who Are My Academic Advisors? 

    There are two faculties on the Milton campus. Select your faculty to start. 

    • Lazaridis School of Business and Economics
    • Faculty of Science

    Lazaridis School of Business and Economics

    There are three advisors for the Business Technology Management BTM) program: Kelly, Devyn and Samantha. You can connect with any advisor on the team for support. 

    For the quickest and most effective response, email us at btmadvising@wlu.ca

    Make sure you: 

    • Email us from your @mylaurier.ca account. 
    • Include your student number.

    *To support a higher volume of students during course registration, booked appointments will be paused from July 6 to July 17. Please email btmadvising@wlu.ca to connect with an advisor, or attend one of the virtual drop-in LORIS Support Sessions if you need extra help during senior student registration week.

    Virtual appointments can be booked via our online booking system. Appointments are posted every Monday morning for the entire following week.

    Please note that booking a second appointment does not cancel your original booking. If you need to cancel a booked appointment, please email us and we can cancel it for you.

    Virtual LORIS Support drop-in hours will be held daily, Monday -Friday from 2-3 p.m. An Advisor will be available to connect with you on a first-come-first serve basis. Be sure to activate your Laurier Zoom account before joining any of the sessions:

    • July 6 from 2 to 3 p.m.
    • July 7 from 2 to 3 p.m.
    • July 8 from 2 to 3 p.m.
    • July 9 from 2 to 3 p.m.
    • July 10 from 2 to 3 p.m.

    Faculty of Science

    For first-year students, Dan Taylor is your academic advisor and primary contact for academic information. 

    Senior students (year 2+) can connect with department undergraduate officers who are experts in their discipline and program/course content: 

    • Computer Science undergraduate officers: Lilatul Ferdouse and Abdul-Rahman Mawlood-Yunis
    • Psychology undergraduate officers: Justin Cavallo and Kim Roberts

    How to Contact Us

    Complete the Faculty of Science Academic Advising Inquiry form to initiate a request for information and support.

    You will receive a response to your @mylaurier.ca email from your academic advisor, department undergraduate officer or advising support staff within one to five business days (depending on the type of inquiry and volume of requests). 

    Make an Appointment

    Virtual and in-person appointments are available for more complex circumstances. 

    To make an appointment request complete the Faculty of Science Academic Advising Inquiry form.

    Appointments are scheduled for 10 minutes. 

    Appointment Availability

    • In-person appointments are available at the Milton Academic Centre one day a week (Tuesdays). 
    • Virtual appointments are available Monday through Thursday on Teams.
