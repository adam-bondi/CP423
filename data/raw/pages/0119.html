    First-Year Program Requirements: Lazaridis School of Business and Economics (Milton)

    The following information is part of Step 1: Program Requirements of the Course Registration Guide for first-year Lazaridis School of Business and Economics (Milton) students. To view the remaining steps, consult the Course Registration Guide.

    Program Requirements by Major

    Definitions

    • BBTM: Bachelor of Business Technology Management (honours)

    Business Technology Management (BBTM)

    Fall

    • BU121
    • BU127
    • EC120
    • MB105
    • SC101 (highly recommended to prepare for MA129)

    Winter

    • EC140
    • MA129
    • MB106
    • MB115
    • 1 elective (0.5 credit)

    Notes

    The BTM program highly recommends taking SC101 to best prepare you for success in MA129. In SC101, you will  review skills in algebra, trigonometry and functions, and learn strategies to transition to university-level expectations in mathematics. It is especially important to take SC101 if you did not take Calculus and Vectors in high school. You must complete the Calculus Preparation Evaluation (CPE) before you can register in SC101.

    BU121, MB115 and MA129 require a lab. Remember to add the lecture and the lab to properly register.

    You must take the MC sections of BU127, EC120 and EC140 (not VA).

    Students must complete all required first-year courses to progress to Year 2, with a minimum major GPA of 7.00 (B-) and a non-major GPA of 5.00 (C).

    Course Load

    To earn an Honours Degree, you will need to complete 20.0 credits:

    • 0.5 credit: Normally conducted over one term
    • 1.0 credit: Normally conducted over two terms

    If you are studying part-time, you can take up to 1.5 credits per term. Full-time status is given to students who take 2.0 or 2.5 credits per term.

    If you select 2.5 credits in the fall term and 2.5 credits in the winter term, you can complete an Honours Degree in four years.
