    Projects

    The Students’ Union is one of Laurier’s largest partners in student satisfaction. Through the Student Life Levy (SLL), the Students’ Union has provided millions in funding to enhance the undergraduate student experience on both the Waterloo and Brantford campuses. Laurier is proud of this partnership that has empowered students to create projects and programs that build leadership skills and enhance student life.
