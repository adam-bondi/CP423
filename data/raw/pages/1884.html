    • Jan. 5, 2026 (winter 2026)
    • May 4, 2026 (spring/intersession/summer 2026 terms)

    Service Laurier

    We provide front-line service on behalf of Enrolment Services for inquiries about financial aid and OSAP, and scholarships and bursaries.

    Our staff are available to answer your questions and provide support. We hold ourselves and each other accountable to our service values to be compassionate, consistent, efficient, authentic and inclusive.
