    Dean's Honour Roll 2021

    Nov. 4, 2021
    Print | PDF

    Undergraduate students registered in a degree seeking program will be eligible for Dean’s Honour Roll if they have achieved a minimum overall GPA of 10 (A-) in all courses taken from Sept. 1 – Aug. 31. Assessment for the dean’s honour roll will be completed in the following fall term by the Faculty in which the student was most recently registered.
