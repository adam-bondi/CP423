    Graduate Scholarships and Awards

    The university has numerous opportunities for graduate students looking to secure funding for future academic studies. Funding is available through academic achievement, employment or financial need. Graduate students who are interested in learning about these opportunities should review the information provided by the Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies.

    The Department of Kinesiology and Physical Education is proud to celebrate the notable achievements of its graduate students, which are listed below.
