    Startup Lab Entrepreneurs

    One of the most unique aspects of our program is that we provide a community for the entrepreneurs to help each other and build a network. This is done through our social events and providing access to our workspace, located at Lazaridis Hall. 

    The lab is a quiet and private space where startups can work on their business. We like to use the space as a point of convergence for all entrepreneurs that are part of the StartUp Lab. 

    StartUp Lab's mission is to empower students, alumni and the community by providing education, resources and expreiences that inspire innovation. We guide entrepreneurs through the Discovery, Idea, Build and Growth phases, promoting data-driven decisions and flexibility.
