    Laurier Orientation:
    LOCUS Schedule

    Laurier Off-Campus University Students (LOCUS) offers a variety of programs throughout Orientation designed to help off-campus students get connected, feel supported, and build community from the very start of their Laurier journey.

    Kick things off at our Off-Campus Welcome Day, where you’ll meet other new students living off-campus, learn about life at Laurier, and start building meaningful connections right away. You’ll also have the opportunity to connect with your Off-Campus Advisor (upper-year student mentors) who will support you throughout Orientation and the academic year.

    LOCUS also hosts additional events and activities throughout Orientation, creating more opportunities to engage, make friends, and feel part of campus life, no matter where you live.

    To learn more and explore everything planned for you, select your campus below to view the full schedule of LOCUS programs and events.

    • Brantford Campus
    • Waterloo Campus
    • Milton Campus

    Note: All Milton students are considered off-campus students and part of LOCUS. There is no separate Off-Campus Welcome Day for Milton students; instead, they participate fully in the Milton Undergraduate Orientation schedule.

    More Orientation Events

    In addition to LOCUS-specific programming, be sure to explore the full Fall Undergraduate Orientation schedule for your campus so you don't miss out on other exciting programs and activities available to you!

    Off-Campus Welcome Day (Sept. 6) Sept. 7 to 12 Events

    Off-Campus Welcome Day - Sunday, Sept. 6 (Brantford)

    As part of Laurier Orientation, LOCUS is excited to welcome first-year off-campus students to our Off-Campus Welcome Day to kickstart the year!

    We want to make sure your first day on campus as a Golden Hawk is filled with opportunities to meet other first-year students and learn about life at Laurier. At the event, students will be able to connect with their Off-Campus Advisor and Icebreakers (upper-year student mentors) for Orientation and the year ahead.

    Sunday, September 6th, 2026 | 12:30  to 5:15 p.m. | One Market Building

    • Early Bird Registration: Before August 21st
    • Registration Deadline: September 2nd

    Register Now!

    NOTES:
    Register before the early bird deadline (August 21st) to be entered for a chance to win a Laurier prize pack! The selected winner will receive their prize at check-in on Off-Campus Welcome Day.

    This event is also open to transfer students entering their first year at Laurier (but may be entering their second year overall). Transfer students can opt in to LOCUS by emailing locus@wlu.ca.

    Check-In
    When: 1 to 1:30 p.m.

    Event Details

    Our staff and volunteers will be waiting to welcome you to Laurier and check you into our Off-Campus Welcome Day. Students should come prepared by arriving early and knowing their Laurier student number and email address.

    Where: OM206-10 | Senate and Board Chambers | One Market
    When: 1 to 1:30 p.m.
    Host: LOCUS

    Join us for a quick and friendly Welcome session, where you’ll learn about the LOCUS program and all the supports/resources available to help you thrive as an off-campus student. You’ll also have the chance to meet other off-campus students through optional icebreakers. 

    Where: OM206-10 | Senate and Board Chambers | One Market
    When: 1:30 to 2 p.m.
    Host: LOCUS

    Find your fit with one of our many Mentoring Cafe options! During this event, students have the opportunity to learn more about our LOCUS Links by meeting the Off-Campus Advisor (OCA) and learning how to opt in to a Link! Alternatively, we also have space dedicated to asking an OCA your questions about Laurier and to learn more about leadership opportunities that are exclusive to first-year students in our LOCUS House Council Information Session.

    *Each session will run twice within the scheduled time block. Students can select up to two unique sessions to attend.     

    Where: Multiple Locations (see below) | One Market
    When: 2 to 2:55 p.m.
    Host: LOCUS


    AVAILABLE SESSIONS:

    Social Work Link Connection

    The Social Work LOCUS Link is a faculty-based community that all off-campus first-year students are eligible to opt into. This Link will provide social work students with more opportunities to connect with like-minded individuals, attend related events, connect with their classmates, and more.

    Where: OM207-1 | One Market

     

    Arts and Literature Link Connection

    The Arts and Literature LOCUS Link is an interest-based community that all off-campus first-year students are eligible to opt into. This Link will provide its students with more opportunities to connect with like-minded individuals, an assigned Off-Campus Advisor, and related events, such as those involving painting, reading, beading, and more. .

    Where: OM207-2 | One Market

     

    Gaming Link Connection

    The Gaming LOCUS Link is an interest-based community that all off-campus first-year students are eligible to opt into. This Link will provide its students with more opportunities to connect with like-minded individuals, an assigned Off-Campus Advisor, and related gaming events such as Minecraft, Mario Kart tournaments, board game cafes, and more.

    Where: OM207-3 | One Market

     

    LOCUS House Council Information Session

    Join us to learn all about getting involved in your first year at Laurier. In this session, students will learn about the First Year Leadership Program (FYLP) and the related interest-based councils available to join and engage with. Students can connect directly with the LOCUS House Council Advisor and receive all application and council hiring details

    Where: OM206-10 | Senate and Board Chambers | One Market

    Take some time to explore campus with our Off-Campus Advisors! The campus tours will show you some of the highlights of campus, as well as some important information that you’ll want to know as an off-campus student! Bring your class schedule; we would be happy to help you find your classes before they begin

    Where: Tours are departing from the Student Commons Lounge | One Market
    When: 3 to 3:45 p.m.
    Host: LOCUS

    First Year Game Zone

    Get familiar with the Level 1 Lounge on our Brantford campus, and join the LOCUS team for Levi’s LOCUS Games, a team challenge featuring a variety of mini-games. Additionally, you can explore our Gaming Tournaments with your new friends.

    Where: Level 1 Lounge | One Market
    When: 3:45 to 5 p.m.
    Host: LOCUS

     

    First Year Keepsakes

    Take some time to capture this memory by creating something you can take home and hold onto for years to come. In this event, students will have the opportunity to engage with their peers while choosing from a variety of crafts such as a letter to a future self, terracotta pot decorating, friendship bracelet, and keychain making.

    Where: Level 1 Lounge | One Market
    When: 3:45 to 5 p.m.
    Host: LOCUS

    To conclude Off-Campus Welcome Day, our Off-Campus Advisors (OCA) will walk you over to the Orientation kit pick-up location, where you can make sure you are fully registered for the rest of the week, receive your Laurier o-kit, and engage with fun photo booths and activities.

    Where: Research and Academic Centre (RAC) Courtyard
    When: 5 to 5:45 p.m.
    Host: LOCUS and Your Students' Union
