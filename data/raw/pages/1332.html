    Awards and Scholarships

    At Laurier, we understand that pursuing postsecondary education can come with financial burden. The Lazaridis Master of Business Administration (MBA) offers program-specific financial aid, alongside institution wide financial support, to ensure that you are in the best position to succeed.

    Note that select program-specific awards and scholarships are available to MBA students based on their program cohort.
