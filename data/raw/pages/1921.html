    Laurier Tuition Bursary

    The Laurier Tuition Bursary Program is designed to provide financial support to eligible domestic students by offering tuition assistance based on demonstrated financial need.

    Students are able to apply for the program each term. The amount awarded may vary term-by-term depending on the number of qualified applicants and funding available.

    Application Type

    We’ve streamlined the process! The Laurier Tuition Bursary is now available every term. Previously, full-time students would apply for multiple terms at a time, with categories like “Fall & Winter” or “Winter & Spring”. The full-time applications have been replaced by the following three applications:

    • Fall Term
    • Winter Term
    • Spring Term

    If you previously applied under different categories as a full-time student, such as Fall & Winter, Winter & Spring, you will now complete individual applications based on your current term and enrollment. 

    The part-time Laurier Tuition Bursary is available to undergraduate students enrolled in less than 1.5 credits and part-time graduate students in the fall and winter terms.

    Check the awards important dates and deadlines to give yourself enough time to gather your required documents and make sure you complete the tuition bursary application on time. 

    Fall Laurier Tuition Bursary Application

    The fall Laurier Tuition Bursary application opens Oct. 1, 2025, and closes on Oct. 22, 2025 (11:59 p.m.).

    1. Fill out the fall Laurier Tuition Bursary application on LORIS

    • If you meet the base criteria, you will automatically receive the application when it opens on LORIS each term
    • This application is fall-specific. You must apply for each term you want to be considered

    Learn more about how to apply on LORIS

    2. Verify that you have a complete financial aid application

    Ontario Students:

    To qualify for bursary assistance, your full-time OSAP confirmation of enrolment must be complete for the fall term by the bursary deadline.

    If you are officially restricted from OSAP funding, you are eligible to apply for bursary assistance. To be considered officially restricted by the program requirements/guidelines of the Ministry of Colleges, Universities, Research Excellence and Security (MCURES), you must:

    • Submit a complete OSAP application
    • Receive an OSAP assessment confirming your restricted status

    If you indicate that you are restricted in your Laurier Tuition Bursary application but have not applied for OSAP, your bursary will be declined.

    Out-of-Province Students:

    You are not required to apply for financial aid from your home province, territory, or state to qualify for bursary assistance.

    To verify your out-of-province status, we may look for, but are not limited to, a student aid application from your home province, territory, or state, or an out-of-province address.

    If you are restricted from your out-of-province student aid program, you are eligible to apply. You must indicate this within the bursary application.

    For more details, please refer to the FAQ section

    The fall Laurier Tuition Bursary is available to students who meet the following criteria (September to December):

    • Citizenship: Canadian citizen, permanent resident (landed immigrant), or a protected person
    • Academic Standing: Minimum cumulative GPA of 4.0 (C-)
    • Financial Aid:
      • Ontario students must have a complete full-time OSAP application and meet one of the following conditions:
        • Confirmation of enrolment for the fall term is complete by the bursary deadline or
        • officially restricted from receiving OSAP funding
      • Out-of-province students are not required to apply for financial aid from their home province, territory, or state
    • Fall Enrollment: 
      • Undergraduate students enrolled in a minimum of 1.5 credits 
      • Graduate students enrolled in a full-time course load
      • Students with an approved reduced course load registration outlined by your accommodation plan with Accessible Learning.

    • Recipients will be notified by email by late November
    • Bursary funding will be applied directly to the student account by late November.
    • Bursary money will not be applied to your account before tuition for the fall term is due; you are responsible for payment of any late fees.

    Receive Your Funding

    Bursary funding will be applied directly to the student account learn how your Laurier Tuition Bursary is applied to your student account.

    Conditions

    Applications cannot be assessed until the deadline date passes. All conditions and eligibility requirements are subject to change at the discretion of the Student Awards Office.

    You are considered ineligible for assistance through the Laurier Tuition Bursary program if:
    • You submitted an incomplete Laurier Tuition Bursary application.
    • You have received the Entrance Bursary in the same academic term.
    • You have received Student Access Guarantee (SAG) funding valued at more than the Laurier Tuition Bursary.
    • You are an Ontario student who has not applied for OSAP as of the Laurier Tuition Bursary deadline.
    • You are an Ontario student who has an incomplete OSAP application (e.g., pending required documentation) as of the Laurier Tuition Bursary deadline.
    • You are an Ontario student who indicated that you are restricted from OSAP, but have not submitted an application as of the Laurier Tuition Bursary deadline.
    • You have sufficient financial resources, as determined by the information submitted in your application.
