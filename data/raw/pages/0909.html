    Interviews

    The Career Centre is here to assist you in preparing for interviews, including developing an interview agenda and knowing what types of questions to expect. The following services and resources are available to you:

    • Attend an Interview Success workshop.
    • Earn a Career Development Certificate.
    • Pre-book an appointment to meet with a Career Centre staff member. Start with an interview coaching appointment and then have a mock interview.
    • Attend a brief appointment during our same-day service hours.
    • Read the Career Centre's Interview Skills guidebook on the Career Centre Resources page in Navigator.
    • Research organizations and interview questions on Glassdoor.

    Know Your Rights

    Laws prohibit discrimination in employment based on areas such as disability, marital status, sex, sexual orientation, age, religious affiliation, national origin and ethnic background. Know your rights in the workplace and the hiring process.

    Interview Questions by Field

    In preparing for an interview, consider what questions you might be asked. Review the below questions by field to help prepare relevant answers. The following sample questions are meant to assist you with interviews in specific fields and are meant as a guide only.

    • What do you believe is your biggest challenge pertaining to this job?
    • Talk about a time you had a challenging customer/colleague to work with. Describe a situation and how you approached it.
    • What does work ethic mean to you?
    • Tell us about a time when you had to make a decision without all the information you needed. How did you handle it? Why? What was the outcome?
    • What kind of management style do you thrive best working under?
    • Where do you see yourself in five years? 10 years?
    • What drives you?
    • What is something I would be surprised to learn about you, based on your resume?
    • Tell us what you know about our company/organization and its services.
    • Why have you chosen a career in... (counselling, HR, marketing, finance, library science, etc.)?
    • What are you looking for in a job?
    • What are the biggest skills you bring to this position?
    • What would your previous supervisor say about you?
    • Tell us about a time you worked in a group and it did not go as planned.
    • Please talk about the most significant goal you have accomplished.
    • What stresses you out?
    • Why did you leave your previous employment?
    • Talk about a time you received critical feedback on your performance. How did you handle feedback on your areas of improvement? Did you disagree with it?
    • Where do you see this industry/field going in five years’ time?
    • What are your salary expectations?
    • What did you like most/least about your last position?
    • Tell us about a time you went above and beyond the call of duty.
    • What are you hoping to gain from this work experience?
    • What would your last boss say you needed to improve on?
