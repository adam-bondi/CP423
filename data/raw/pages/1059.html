    Department of Residence

    Living on campus is more than just a place to stay while you study, it's an integral part of your university experience. Whether you're an incoming student thinking about living in residence, or you're already living in residence, we're here to guide you every step of the way so that you settle in comfortably and thrive in your new environment.

    In this section, you'll find everything you need to know about living in residence: how to apply, what to do if you lose your keys, where to pick up your mail, policies and agreements, etc.

    Applying to Residence

    First-year student online applications to live in residence on our Brantford and Waterloo campuses for the 2026/27 academic year (September to April) will launch in early April! Take some time to explore the Laurier residence experience and see what it's like to live in our vibrant campus community.

    Campus Tours

    We can’t wait to welcome you to your home away from home. Whether you want to join us in-person or explore campus virtually, there are lots of ways you can explore our Laurier campuses and engage with our student ambassadors, including:

    In-Person Campus Tours are available at varying times. Explore our tour options and schedules to find a time that works best for you!

    Get Involved, Make Connections

    Make the most out of your first year by getting involved – you'll make a difference at Laurier and in the larger community, build skills, gain valuable experiences, grow personally and academically, and make new friends.

    The application deadline for our Residence Learning Communities (RLC) and Learning Clusters (LC) is June 1.

    Mission

    Our mission is to foster safe, inclusive and engaging communities that allow students to develop meaningful connections, a sense of belonging and pursue academic success. We do this through building community, innovative programming, and a collaborative approach to partnership and student support.

    Vision

    Our vision is to be the leader in post-secondary residence operations.

    Values

    Community: Cultivating a supportive, accepting space where people can grow, take risks, build meaningful connections and feel a sense of belonging.

    Belonging: Providing inclusive spaces where all feel valued, embraced, and connected within a diverse, accessible community.

    Leadership: Equipping students with confidence and skills through residence experiences, preparing them to lead their own lives beyond university.

    Safety: Fostering a secure, inclusive environment where students have access to resources, and can priotitize physical and psychological well-being.

    Student-Focused: Prioritizing student needs, experiences and growth by tailoring services, decisions and support to create meaningful connections and success.

    Laurier Upper Year Housing

    Waterloo

    Ezra-Bricker Student Apartments is a great resource for upper year students who are looking for rental accommodations. This resource includes a number of properties located close to the Waterloo campus, which are owned by Laurier but managed by Campus Living Centres.

    With Ezra-Bricker Student Apartments, you'll find:

    • Rental accommodations that are a one to five minute walk to the Waterloo campus.
    • Large bedrooms and common rooms, full-kitchen and two bathrooms in each suite.
    • Housing that is cost-effective.

    Please check out Ezra-Bricker Student Apartments - Contact Us! if you would like to inquire about these rental accommodations.

    Brantford

    All Laurier Brantford students, regardless of year, are eligible for residence! Our residence spaces come fully furnished and allow for flexible terms (four-, eight-, or twelve-month terms).

    Living in residence offers a welcoming, supportive environment that helps you stay connected to the campus community. You’ll be close to your classes, campus resources, and all the events and opportunities that can shape your Laurier experience. Whether you're looking to make new friends, get involved in student life, or simply enjoy the convenience of living steps away from campus, residence is a great choice. 

    For more information, please visit Upper Year Residence, Brantford Campus.

    Students interested in applying for upper year residence should email the Brantford Residence Office.

    Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy

    The Department of Residence is accountable to provincial legislation, the Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act (FIPPA), which dictates how the university collects, uses and discloses personal information. Under FIPPA, university staff and faculty are prohibited from discussing, changing or divulging a student’s personal information with anyone other than the student. As such, staff will need to speak directly with the student about any concerns unless written permission is received from the student granting us permission to speak to a parent or guardian.

    Please ensure that your parents and other individuals who may contact our office on your behalf are aware that Department of Residence staff will not be able to discuss any specific information about your room assignment or any concerns that may emerge during your time in residence (i.e. involvement in the conduct process, extension requests, restitution costs, etc.).
