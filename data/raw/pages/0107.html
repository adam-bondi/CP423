    Student Records

    This Student Records page provides you with information on how you can request academic documentation at Laurier. Whether you're checking for account holds, ordering transcripts, requesting letters and forms, or updating your personal information, this page provides the tools and guidance you need.

    On this page: 

    • Holds
    • Transcripts
    • Letter and Form Requests (Verification of Enrolment)
    • Degree
    • Name Change

    Holds 

    Service Laurier is unable to verify enrolment or produce documents if a hold is on the account. 

    Holds are restrictions placed on a record that prevent account holders from accessing grades, verifying enrolment, receiving transcripts, etc.

    To view holds: 

    1. Go to LORIS.
    2. Select Student Services on the navigation ribbon.
    3. Select Open Student Dashboard.
    4. Select Account Summary.
    5. Select Holds.

    Once the hold is removed, account holders may proceed with their request.

    Transcripts

    Laurier will not email official documents directly to a student or alumni. We are only permitted to send documents to a verified institution/organization. It is the student's responsibility to ensure the receiving party will accept Laurier's electronic document prior to ordering.

    Grades are typically made official six to eight weeks after the term ends. 

    An official transcript is a complete record of a student’s academic history, including any completed, official undergraduate and graduate courses, as well as any current registrations. Transcripts include all official grades. Partial transcripts cannot be issued. Transcripts can be used as proof of registration, graduation, or as a requirement for an application.

    How to request: Official Transcripts can be requested via our online transcript ordering system

    Processing time: Requests are processed in receipt order: 4 business days, up to 7 business days during peak times. (rush service unavailable)
    Charge: $18
    Delivery options: Pick-up (Waterloo campus), Standard Mail, Courier, or Email to an institution or organization.

    Laurier will not email official documents directly to a student or alumni. It is the student's responsibility to ensure the receiving party will accept Laurier's electronic document prior to ordering. We are only permitted to send one electronic transcript to a verified institution/organization, per request. 

    Note - Laurier does not use third-party external transcript services such as MyCreds or Parchment. 

    Unofficial Transcripts can be found on LORIS or Navigator.

    To obtain an unofficial transcript from LORIS:

    1. Go to LORIS.
    2. Select Student Services on the navigation ribbon. 
    3. Find the Student Records card.
    4. Select Official Grades.
    5. Select the Transcript Level and the Transcript Type.
    6. Click Submit.
    7. Click Print to generate a pdf of your unofficial record of coursework.

    Please note: only official grades will generate on this document. This is not an official transcript.

    To request an unofficial record of course work from Navigator:

    1. Go to Navigator.
    2. Select the Documents tab.
    3. Select View beside Transcript.
    4. Select Download PDF.

    The Navigator unofficial record of coursework will display your chosen name. If a chosen name is not provided, your legal name will be used.

    Processing time: Immediate
    Charge: Free
    Delivery options: Download or print a PDF through your web browser. Note: Laurier will not email unofficial transcripts directly to a student or alumni. 

    If you are an alumni of Laurier and haven't logged into your account in over two years your account may be deactivated due to inactivity. Additionally, if you attended Laurier before Laurier emails were issued, you likely don't have an account. To gain access to your account to view unofficial transcripts, please email support@mylaurier.ca and the ICT Service Desk will assist you in re-enabling your account or creating a MyLaurier account for you.

    If you are unsure of your ID number, contact Service Laurier or book an appointment and we can assist you with retrieving your ID number.

    If you have issues with your Network Login Username and/or Password, please contact Tech Support and Services for assistance.

    Course Number
    A first digit of 1, 2, 3 or 4 normally corresponds to the year level in an undergraduate program. A first digit of 5 or above represents a course at the graduate level.

    Course Weight
    The weighting factor is used to count course credits and calculate averages. For undergraduate courses, a weight of 0.5 approximates a 12-week (one-term) course of three contact hours per week. For graduate courses, a weight of 0.5 approximates a 13-week (one-term) course. Other weights are accordingly proportioned by duration and/or lecture hours.

    Transcript Notations

    The following designate a non-graded standing in a course.

    Symbol definition:

    • AUD = Audit
    • CPL = Complete
    • CR = Credit
    • DEF = Deferred Examination approved
    • IP = In Progress
    • P = Passed
    • S = Satisfactory
    • U = Unsatisfactory
    • WD = Voluntary Withdrawal without failure after two-thirds of the course completed
    • DR = Fail (course was dropped after the academic deadline) 
    • XF = Fail (exam was not written) 
    • F = Fail 

    Not Accountable: Granted only through a faculty petition committee decision due to extenuating circumstances. Appears on an official transcript adjacent to grades that are not used in the GPA calculation.

    Failed Courses: A grade of DR, XF or F is a failed course with a ‘zero’ GPA value. You must repeat a failed course, if it is required for your program. If it is not required, you may repeat the course, or take an additional course. No course may be attempted more than twice. 

    Repeated Later: If a course is repeated then the first attempt will show as 'Repeated Later' and the grade from the second attempt will be included in the GPA calculation, regardless of whether it is higher or lower than the first attempt.

    I don't remember my Student ID - how can I obtain this information?
    We cannot release ID numbers over email. If you are unsure of your ID number, please contact Service Laurier via live chat or telephone and we can assist you with retrieving your ID number. If you were a student prior to 1970 please advise Service Laurier when you call and we can assist you with placing your order manually.

    My grades aren't official, but I need them on my record - what can I do? Releasing official grades early is not a common practice as it is not equitable to complete for one student and not another.  That being said, there are certain circumstances where an exception is made (i.e. Deadlines for graduate school, Visa purposes, etc.).  If you believe you may be eligible, please complete the Early Grade Release Request Form. 

    Once approved, you will be required to provide proof of payment. Fee payment instructions will be included in your approval email.  Full instructions, including a processing timeline, can be found on the Early Grade Release Request Form website directly.

    I never got a Confirmation Email - what can I do?
    If you do not receive a confirmation email within a few minutes of submitting your order, contact Service Laurier as soon as possible with your student ID number and a subject line of "Transcript Technical Difficulty."

    Why am I getting a AVS error when submitting payment?

    An AVS error occurs when the billing address provided during checkout does not match the address on file with the card-issuing bank or credit card company. Please verify the address on file with your card-issuing bank or credit card company before submitting payment again.

    Letter and Form Requests

    Laurier will not email official documents directly to a student or alumni. We are only permitted to send documents to a verified institution/organization. It is the student's responsibility to ensure the receiving party will accept Laurier's electronic document prior to ordering. 

    Any registration changes may take up to 24 hours to appear on LORIS.

    Letter and Forms are only as accurate as the information we have available to us at that time. Make sure LORIS reflects your most up-to-date registration before submitting a request.

    VOE letters (for current or past terms) can be obtained through LORIS. Registration changes may take up to 24 hours to appear on the VOE. A VOE will not be available until a student has registered for the term. 

    Charge: Free
    Delivery options: Online self-serve

    To request an official verification of enrolment letter: 

    1. Go to LORIS.
    2. Select Student Services on the navigation ribbon.
    3. Locate the Transcripts and Official Documents card.
    4. Select Verification of Enrolment (VOE).
    5. Select a term. 
    6. Select Submit.
    7. Print or save the generated PDF document.

    The VOE is generated in high resolution on Laurier letterhead, using data directly from the student record, and is considered official by:

    • RESP Providers (Paying Tuition, etc.)
    • Government Forms - CPP Forms, etc.
    • Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC; formerly Citizenship and Immigration Canada)
    • local transport authorities (GRT, GO, Toronto Transit)
    • employers
    • banks or other financial institutions
    • etc.
    The information contained within the VOE includes:
    • personal identifiers including name, student number, and birthdate
    • the date and time the document was generated
    • registration status
    • degree and program of study
    • current year, and duration of the program (if applicable)
    • full-time or part-time
    • campus
    • term dates
    • course registration summary (for the term)

    Service Laurier is able to provide undergraduate students with the following letters (graduate students can request a letter from the Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies):

    • Verification of enrolment for current or past terms;
    • Confirm degree requirements are completed and degree has been confirmed (proof of graduation);
    • Confirm degree requirements are met, but the degree has not been conferred at convocation;
    • Anticipate meeting all degree requirements following a given term; and
    • Custom letters pertaining to a student record.

    How to request: Letters can be requested via the Letter Request Portal.

    Processing time: Requests are processed in receipt order, 4 - 7 business days. Specialized letters (I.e., degree requirements have been met or QECO letters) cannot be expedited and can take up to three weeks to produce.
    Charge: $16.25
    Delivery:

    • Standard Mail (Canada Post): No tracking number provided. Delivery times vary.
      • Laurier will only send one printed letter to one mailing address. 
    • Courier: Tracking number provided. Additional charges apply.
      • Laurier will only send one printed letter to one mailing address. 
    • Email: Laurier will not email official documents directly to a student or alumni. We are only permitted to send documents to a verified institution/organization.
    • Pick-up Waterloo campus ONLY:
      • Pick-up will only be available at Service Laurier Waterloo during hours of operation.
      • After an order is submitted a confirmation email will be sent with the details of your document request, including a link to check the status of your order. Once ready, document status will change from pending to awaiting pick-up. For a full list of statuses, please refer to the Order Request Status Legend section of this page.
      • Please note that you will need to show a piece of photo ID to pick up your document (ie. OneCard or driver's license).

    I don't remember my Student ID - how can I obtain this information?

    We cannot release ID numbers over email. If you are unsure of your ID number, contact Service Laurier via live chat or telephone and we can assist you with retrieving your ID number. 

    Where can I pick up my order?

    Why am I getting a AVS error when submitting payment?

    An AVS error occurs when the billing address provided during checkout does not match the address on file with the card-issuing bank or credit card company. Please verify the address on file with your card-issuing bank or credit card company before submitting payment again.

    Why is my letter request taking so long to be completed or showing as 'Pending'?

    Specialized letters requiring more detailed verification (e.g. degree requirements have been met, QECO) need multiple levels of confirmation and can take up to three weeks to process. Your patience is greatly appreciated. 

    Why has my order been canceled?

    If information is missing or a hold is on the student account, we may cancel your request and issue a refund. An email identifying missed information will be sent. Students may be responsible for submitting a new request. 

    How can I cancel my Letter or Form Request?

    *Students can email documents@wlu.ca to request the cancelation of their Letter or Form Request if the order has not been processed. Email Subject: CANCEL ORDER #
