    StartUp Lab Milton

    StartUp Lab Milton offers a gateway to the world of entrepreneurship, providing access to a wide range of resources and support for students from any field of study at the Milton campus.

    How We Can Support You

    If you’re a student in Milton, we’re pleased to offer flexible virtual support from staff based at the Waterloo and Brantford campuses, ensuring you always have guidance for your venture.

    Events and Programming

    We’d love to see you at our in-person “Lunch & Learns” to connect with a vibrant community and gain valuable insights. Our StartUp Lab programming in Milton is still growing as the campus and programming continues to grow too.

    Join us at our StartUp Lab Milton events to connect with students, entrepreneurs, and mentors to learn how to build and grow your startup.
