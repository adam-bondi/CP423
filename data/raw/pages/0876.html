    Career Development Workshops

    Attend Career Centre workshops to learn how to begin exploring careers and gain insight into job search and employment preparation. As you participate, you'll be on track to receive the Career Development Certificate and the Engagement and Employability Certificate, a valuable addition to your resumé and Laurier Experience Record.

    Log in at navigator.wlu.ca to register. Sessions can be attended in any order based on your schedule.
