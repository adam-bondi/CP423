    Programs and Workshops

    Student Success offers a suite of academic success programs designed to build academic skills that help you succeed at university and beyond. In addition, our programs and workshops are designed to build essential skills, enhance your knowledge, and equip you for your university career and beyond.

    Math and Course Support

    Struggling with solving equations, using formulas, or applying key concepts covered in your course? Our Math and Course Support team of successful senior students and professional staff will work with you to provide guidance, study techniques and strategies to enhance your understanding. Let us help you succeed in your math, statistics, and problem-solving based courses.

    Programs:

    Our Math and Statistics programs help you build numeracy skills to achieve success in university and beyond. 

    Drop-In Help

    Have a math question? Need help with that stats formula? We can help you understand concepts from a variety of quantitative-based subject areas – fundamentals, algebra, calculus, proofs, linear algebra, financial math, probability and statistics, economics, computer coding, etc. and we support chemistry courses as well!

    Our drop-in sessions provide an informal peer-to-peer learning environment. No appointment needed!

    Audience: Undergraduate

    Math Mentoring Program

    If you're at risk of not meeting progression requirements for your program, register for the Math Mentoring Program to get help getting back on track. 

    Audience: Undergraduate students at risk of academic probation

    Pre-Calculus and Pre-Statistics Modules

    The Pre-Calculus and Pre-Statistics Modules for introductory courses provide you with the opportunity to review and demonstrate mastery of prerequisite material. You will progress through each module’s series of online assignments as independent study. If you have any questions please attend our Drop-In Help for extra help with any of the material covered by the modules. 

    Tutor Connection Program

    Are you a Laurier student looking for a tutor or interested in providing tutoring services?  Our Tutor Connection Program provides training to prospective tutors as well as to those students looking for tutor support and helps connect the two groups.

     

     

    Workshops:

    Attend our math workshops to build essential skills, improve problem-solving techniques, and achieve your academic goals.

    Directed Homework Sessions

    Get help from senior students to get yourself in the habit of doing your math homework and set some learning strategies.

    Audience: Undergraduate

    Mock Midterms and Review Sessions

    One of the ways you can prepare yourself for your math midterms and exams is to participate in a mock test. You'll receive feedback on your answers so you can see your areas of strength and the concepts you need to review. 

    Audience: Undergraduate

    Monday Morning Math Clinics

    Join us in the Fall 2026 term to review and refresh your understanding of these foundational mathematical topics during our Monday Morning Math Clinics! You will be able to deepen your conceptual knowledge, develop essential skills, and prepare for success in your mathematics courses.

    Audience: Undergraduate

    Supplemental Instruction (SI)

    Supplemental Instruction (SI) sessions provide an opportunity for students to learn and understand challenging course content with their peers, during regularly scheduled one-hour SI sessions. SI sessions are led by SI leaders, senior students who have successfully completed the course, regularly attend lecture and are well trained to plan and facilitate SI sessions. 

    Audience: Undergraduate

    TriAGe

    Review and deepen your understanding of core concepts, build a strong foundation and increase your chances of success in calculus with TriAGe!

    TriAGe is a non-credit course designed to help prepare students who plan to enrol in an entry-level university calculus course by reinforcing fundamental skills in Trigonometry, Algebra, and Geometry. 

    Study Skills

    Take your learning to the next level with our Study Skills team! We offer workshops, one-on-one coaching, and resources to help you succeed in university, and beyond. Learn how to manage your time, take effective notes, stay organized, and prepare for exams with confidence. Whether you're aiming for better grades, sharpening your focus, or wanting to boost productivity, our tools and techniques are tailored for you and your learning.

    Programs:

    Whether you need guidance, motivation, or specialized support, explore our study skills programs to find the tools and resources that will empower you to achieve your academic and personal goals.

    Athlete Academic Success Program

    The Athlete Academic Success Program (AASP) supports Laurier’s varsity athletes to achieve their full academic potential while accommodating training and competition schedules. Fellow varsity athletes mentor their peers to achieve success both in the classroom and on the field.

    Audience: Undergraduate

    Black Student Academic Success Programs

    The Black Student Academic Success Programs supports Black students at Laurier. Our Black Student Academic Support services are dedicated to empowering Black students at Laurier throughout their academic journey. We offer tailored resources, peer connection, and supportive programming to foster academic excellence and personal growth. Through community-building initiatives and strategic support, we help students navigate challenges and thrive in their university experience.

    Audience: Undergraduate and Graduate  

    Keep Calm and Study On

    Feeling the pressure in the final weeks of the term? Keep Calm and Study On!

    Running in the fall and winter terms, Keep Calm and Study On offers great workshops, de-stressing fun and online resources to help you succeed in studying for finals and focusing on your personal wellness.

    Audience: Undergraduate and Graduate

    Progressing Past Probation (P3)

    Progressing Past Probation (P3) connects students placed on academic probation with resources and supports to help them improve their academic standing.

    Audience: Undergraduate

    Student Opportunity for Academic Resilience Program (SOAR)

    SOAR is an academic recovery program that supports students after their first semester who are at risk of being on academic probation. SOAR will allow you to set goals, connect with academic support teams on campus, and attend workshops devoted to bettering your academic habits.

    Audience: Undergraduate

    Workshops and Appointments:

    Our diverse range of study skills workshops is designed to help you build essential skills, manage stress, and succeed both academically and personally.

    Academic Skills (ASk) Workshops and Certificate

    Available to all students, Academic Skills (ASk) is a collection of workshops about study skills that are geared toward your academic success. Come to one, or all, of the workshops and learn about new academic skills and strategies!

    To earn the ASk Certificate, you must complete five ASk activities within one term. This includes at least four workshops, with the fifth activity being either another workshop or a student success appointment. Once completed, the ASk Certificate can be added to your Laurier Experience Record.

    Audience: Undergraduate and Graduate

    Book with a Learning Consultant

    Connect with one of our professional learning consultants for strategies to boost your focus, time management, and academic success. We will help you to develop learning skills to excel in your academic studies, enhance your school-life balance and provide you with study strategies to improve your understanding of course content.

    No matter where you are in your university journey, we’ll help you achieve your academic goals.

    Audience: Undergraduate and Graduate

    Graduate Student Academic Support

    The Graduate Student Academic Support from our Study Skills team supports you throughout your graduate journey with resources tailored to your needs. From time‑management tools and academic skill‑building to effective note‑taking, we offer workshops, events, and personalized services to help you stay organized, strengthen your skills, and feel confident in your graduate studies.

    Audience: Graduate

    Growing Your Strengths

    Running in the fall and winter terms, Growing Your Strengths: Resiliency Toolkit program is a five-module program that aims to help you move from surviving to thriving at university by helping you build resiliency. Resiliency can help you live with the stressors of university life by allowing you to develop coping skills to overcome common challenges you may face, while becoming stronger from them.

    Audience: Undergraduate and Graduate

    Peer Academic Coaching

    Peer Academic Coaches offer peer-to-peer support for all undergraduate students to help you navigate university. Our coaching team is made up of successful senior students that want to help you get off to a good start and throughout the term. Connect with a coach to help you build a study schedule, discuss note-taking methods, learn effective study strategies and more.

    Audience: Undergraduate

    Writing Support

    Need help crafting your essays, refining your writing skills, or organizing your thoughts? Our Writing Support team can support you through any step of the writing process. From understanding the assignment to polishing final drafts, we’ll help you hone your writing skills and build a sustainable writing practice for university and beyond.

    Programs:

    Discover how our writing support programs can assist you in developing your skills, staying motivated, and making meaningful progress on your projects. If you have any questions, you can contact Writing Services at studentsuccess@wlu.ca. Available in Brantford and Waterloo.

    Book with our Writing Tutors!

    Struggling to organize your thoughts, craft compelling essays, or refine your writing style? A writing tutor can help! Whether you're working on academic papers, scholarship proposals, or professional documents, we provide personalized support to:

    • Brainstorm Ideas: Turn scattered thoughts into essay topics and outlines.
    • Clarify Organization: Craft clear, structured arguments and points.
    • Improve Grammar & Style: Polish your writing for accuracy, fluency, and impact.
    • Boost Confidence: Gain the tools to express yourself effectively and persuasively.

    No matter your skill level or place in the writing process, we’ll help you achieve your writing goals. Let’s make your words work for you!

    Audience: Undergraduate and Graduate

    Ready, Set, Go: Writing at Canadian Universities

    This three-part writing series takes multilingual students (and all students who want to learn!) through the foundations of writing well in Canadian universities. Ready, Set, Go: Writing at Canadian Universities will begin with sentence structure, move to essential grammar, and end with strategies for paraphrasing and summarizing, this series builds confidence for writing in an academic setting. By the end of Ready, Set, Go, you'll understand the foundations of writing clearly, writing accurately, and writing for a Canadian academic context.

    Audience: Undergraduate and Graduate

    Writing Certificate

    The Writing Certificate, run by Writing Services, has students participate in individual sessions to systematically develop effective skills in academic writing. You are eligible to receive the Writing Certificate if you have attended and completed at least two writing workshops and two writing appointments during your studies at Laurier.

    If you would like to register for a writing workshop and/or book a writing appointment you can do so through the Student Success Portal.

    To receive The Writing Certificate, attend two Writing Centre workshops and two Writing Centre appointments during your studies at Laurier. You can track your participation on Navigator. Select Certificate Programs from the dropdown menu > Enroll in The Writing Certificate > Click on the certificate elements to track your workshop and your appointments by completing the reflection questionnaires. Once you have completed the 4 certificate elements, The Writing Certificate will automatically appear on your Laurier Experience Record.

    AcWriMo (Academic Writing Month)

    Want to crush your writing goals this November? Whether you're working on essays, research papers, or your thesis, our weekly AcWriMo writing group is here to help! During the AcWriMo group you will set writing goals, build your writing habits, connect with other motivated writers, and get more words on the page! Open to all undergraduate and graduate students in any program. Let's write together! Registration coming in Oct. 2026. 

    Audience: Undergraduate and Graduate 

    Workshops:

    Explore our writing workshops and retreats to elevate your academic writing and advance your academic goals.

     

    Gen AI and Writing Workshops

    The Gen AI and Writing workshops will help you to understand the opportunities and pinpoint some risks of using AI in your writing process. You'll explore how generative AI (ex. ChatGPT) can support different stages of the writing process from brainstorming to revision, while flagging potential ethical and academic risks involved. 

    Audience: Undergraduate and Graduate

    Graduate & ASPIRE Writing Supports

    Our Graduate Writing Supports are here to help you on your graduate writing journey. We offer comprehensive writing guidance tailored specifically for graduate-level students. Whether you're writing research papers, theses, grant proposals, or journal articles, our PhD qualified writing consultants are here to help you refine your ideas, strengthen your arguments, and polish your writing. 

    Audience: Graduate

    Scholarship Proposal Workshops

    Get help writing a research proposal for Ontario Graduate Scholarships (OGS), the Social Science and Humanities Research Council of Canada (SSHRC), the Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada (NSERC), or similar grants and scholarships. In these workshops, you will learn tools to approach scholarship proposal writing with confidence by examining common features of successful proposals and learning how to apply these writing strategies in your own proposal. 

    Audience: Undergraduate and Graduate

    Self-Paced Workshops

    Our self-paced writing workshops teach foundational writing skills on your time, in your way. You can find these workshops under "Self Registration" on MyLearningSpace. Look for these titles, click the blue hyperlink to register, and let the learning commence:

    • Academic Integrity Modules – Graduate
    • Online Academic Integrity Modules – Undergraduate
    • Writing with Inclusive Language
    • Writing Services – How to Cite Using APA
    • Writing Services – How to Cite Using the Chicago Manual of Style
    • Writing Services – How to Cite using MLA

    Audience: Undergraduate and Graduate
