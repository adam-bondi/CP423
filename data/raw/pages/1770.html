    Laurier Orientation:
    Indigenous Students Schedule

    SEEDs Orientation is a welcoming program for incoming Laurier students who self-identify as Indigenous, including first-year undergraduate and graduate students, as well as transfer students. Designed to help you start your academic journey with confidence, SEEDs Orientation is a great opportunity to meet new people, build connections, and feel at home before classes begin.

    Through fun and engaging events, you'll explore campus, connect with peers, and take part in interactive workshops focused on skills that support your success. Together, these experiences will help you build a strong foundation and feel supported as you transition to life as a Laurier student.
