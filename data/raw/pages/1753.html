    Laurier Conducting Symposium Institute, November 25, 2023

    Laurier Conducting Symposium Institute, November 25, 2023

    May 31, 2023

    Print | PDF

    Overview

    CSI-Laurier is a one-day event for music educators and students who want to improve their conducting and rehearsal skills. It will be held at the Savvas Chamberlain Music Building on Laurier’s Waterloo campus. Participants will learn from presentations, Q&A, and hands-on sessions with the Laurier Wind Orchestra and Chamber ensembles. You will also network and grow as musicians and educators.
