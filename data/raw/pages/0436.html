    Your Summer Checklist: Laurier 101

    Our Laurier 101 checklists are lists of recommended tasks that you can complete throughout the summer so that you're ready for day one at Laurier. 

    Whether you're checking to make sure you haven't missed anything or want to look ahead, these lists are here for you to prepare for September.

    Current Checklist: August

     Register for Academic Prep Week sessions. 

     Complete your University Preparation Modules (and earn badges!).

     Fill out the Scholarships and Bursaries Student Profile Form. 

     Review your textbook list and purchase course materials.

     Access your invoice on LORIS. 

    Missed a Checklist? Catch up Here!

    Here's a look at past checklists to ensure that you have everything you need.

    June Checklist

     Register for your courses starting on June 29 at 10 a.m. ET!

     Activate your mylaurier student email.

     Activate your Laurier Zoom account.

     Upload your OneCard photo.

     Explore Financial Aid opportunities.

    July Checklist

     Regularly check your MyLaurier student email.

     Attend Summer Info Sessions and Connection Events.

     Register for a Student Experience Tour!

     If you have a disability or suspected disability, register with Accessible Learning.

     Fill out the Student Self-Identification Form.
