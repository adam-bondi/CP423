    Urgent and After-Hours Care

    The following urgent and after-hours resources are available if you need care when we are closed, if you are having a medical emergency, or if you need sexual violence support.

    If you are in crisis or having a medical emergency right now, call 911 immediately. If you are on campus, call Special Constables.

    Call the Ontario Poison Centre at 1.844.764.7669 for free and confidential consultation on poisonings and drug overdoses.

    Are you a student on the: 

    • Waterloo campus? 
    • Brantford campus?
    • Milton campus?

    Waterloo Resources

    Emergency Medical Care

    If you are having a medical emergency:

    • Call 911;
    •  call Special Constables;
    • Go to the Emergency Department at Grand River Hospital or St. Mary's General Hospital

    Nearby Community Walk-In Clinics

    The Waterloo Walk-In Clinic
    Address: 13-170 University Avenue West, Waterloo
    Phone: 519.725.1514
    Hours: Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 8 p.m.; Saturday and Sunday, 10 a.m. to 3 p.m.

    Spruce Medical Walk-In Clinic
    Address: 318 Spruce Street, Unit 106, Waterloo
    Phone: 519.725.1111

    The Urgent Care Clinic
    Address: 751 Victoria Street South, Kitchener
    Phone: 519.745.2273

    Call Telehealth Ontario to check your symptoms or get advice from a registered nurse: 1.866.797.0000 Telehealth Ontario is a free, 24-hour service. It does not replace calling 911 in an emergency.

    Emergency Mental Health Support

    If you are suicidal or do not think you can keep yourself safe right now:

    • Call 988;
    • call Here 247 at 1.844.437.3247

    Sexual Violence Support

    • Immediate medical assistance is available from the Waterloo Region Sexual Assault and Domestic Violence Treatment Centre at St. Mary's General Hospital. Visit the Emergency Department or call weekdays between 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.: 519.749.6994
    • Call the Sexual Assault Support Centre of Waterloo Region: 519.741.8633
    • Contact Laurier’s Sexual Violence Support Advocate

     

    Brantford Resources

    Emergency Medical Care

    If you are having a medical emergency:

    • Call 911;
    • call Special Constables;
    • Go to the Emergency Department of Brantford General Hospital at 200 Terrace Hill Street.

    Nearby Community Walk-In Clinics

    • Willett Urgent Care Clinic: 238 Grand River Street North, Paris, 519.442.2251
    • Call Telehealth Ontario to check your symptoms or get advice from a registered nurse: 1.866.797.0000 Telehealth Ontario is a free, 24-hour service. It does not replace calling 911 in an emergency.

    Emergency Mental Health Support

    If you are suicidal or do not think you can keep yourself safe right now:

    • Call 988;
    • Call SOAR Community Services: 1.866.811.7188

    Off-Campus Counselling Resources

    • Crisis Line (24/7): 519.752.CARE (2273)
    • St. Leonard's Community Services: 519.759.7188
    • Family Counselling Centre of Brant: 519.753.4173

    Sexual Violence Support

    • Immediate medical assistance is available from the Sexual Assault and Domestic Violence Care Team.  Visit the Emergency Department at Brantford General Hospital or call 519.751.5544 x4449
    • Call the Sexual Assault Centre of Brant for 24-hour crisis phone support: 519.751.3471
    • Contact Laurier’s Sexual Violence Support Advocate
