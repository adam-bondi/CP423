    Course Registration Guide

    Course registration doesn't just start on the day you register. It's all about the preparation. 

    The course registration guide walks you through things to consider before course registration opens so that the process of actually registering goes as smooth as possible. 

    Prepare for Course Registration

    1.

    Check Your Program Requirements. Make a list of courses you need to take.

    2.

    Explore Electives. Select courses that interest you.

    3.

    Build Your Timetable. Plan a schedule for your courses.

    4.

    Register for Your Courses. Now that you've prepared, use LORIS to register.

    Determine Your Registration Date

    Below is a snapshot of when course registration begins. Your course registration date is determined based on the number of credits you have completed (including transfer credits and any courses in progress during the current term).

    Learn how to determine your personal time ticket. 

    Incoming First-Year Students

    Registration opens June 29, 2026 at 10 a.m. 

    Make sure you review your first-year program requirements before registration opens.

    Returning and Transfer Students

    • May 15, 2026 at 10 a.m. for graduate students.
    • July 6 to July 9, 2026 all returning students and incoming transfer students.
    • July 13, 2026 at 10 a.m. for Faculty of Education year two students.
    • July 16, 2026 at 10 a.m. for Faculty of Education year one students.

    LORIS

    LORIS (Laurier Online Registration and Information System) is Laurier's administration tool, which you'll use during the course registration process. 

    LORIS is also used to update your personal information, view student accounts and funding opportunities, check your grades, get safety notifications and much more.

    Learn more about LORIS, including how to use it, common terms and errors you may see during course registration.
