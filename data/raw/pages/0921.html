    Workshops and Certificates

    The Career Centre offers a range of workshops such as career planning, job search preparation, further education and more. 

    Attend workshops to earn the Career Development Certificate and the Engagement and Employability Certificate, a valuable addition to your resumé and Laurier Experience Record.

    Explore Workshop Topics

    Career Planning

    Bring your career puzzle together, one piece at a time!

    Whether you already have key pieces in place or are still gathering them, get ready to unlock new insights into the factors shaping your career options. Discover valuable sources of information, tools, and strategies as you assemble your personalized career research action plan. See how the powerful combination of self-reflection and strategic research can help you complete the picture and reveal rewarding opportunities!

    Career Exploration Using the Challenge Mindset

    Did you know that Laurier Arts and Science graduates are tackling everyday business challenges, while business students are addressing social issues? Break away from the idea that your degree determines your career path. Instead, learn to see the world of work as a landscape of challenges and opportunities, beyond just job titles. This approach is known as "The Challenge Mindset."

    Before attending this workshop, you'll complete the SparkPath Challenge Card exercise to help you explore career options by identifying real-world challenges that resonate with you. You'll also learn some straightforward yet effective strategies to help you navigate the often daunting question: "What do I want to do after graduation?" 

    Craft a Professional Development Plan

    In this session, we'll delve into professional growth strategies to enhance your employability skills. Attend this workshop to develop strategies for setting goals, brainstorm professional development options, and feel confident in making decisions to create a personalized action plan.

    Building Confidence: Overcoming Self-Doubt in Your Career Journey

    Do you hesitate to apply for new roles or doubt your abilities in your current position? It's common to compare ourselves to others and question our qualifications, but this mindset can be a significant barrier to professional growth and satisfaction.

    In this workshop, we'll embark on a transformative journey to boost your self-confidence and minimize the habit of unfavourable comparisons. This interactive session offers practical tools and strategies to recognize and amplify personal achievements and capabilities. By fostering a positive self-image and understanding the value of your unique contributions, you will learn to navigate your career paths with assurance and determination.

    Preparing for Salary Negotiation

    Are you confident in negotiating your salary? Join this workshop to become aware of resources to do research, recognize how to communicate in a way that allows you to maximize the value of your job offers, and discover new resources that can help equip you for salary negotiations. 

    Thrive in the Workplace

    You've nailed the interview and landed the job. Congratulations! Now, it's time to shift gears and discover how to flourish in your new work environment.

    In this workshop, we'll explore strategies to help you thrive: navigating policies, procedures, and unwritten rules, managing expectations, and building meaningful professional connections. Our checklists will help you prepare. By asking the right questions and fully understanding your responsibilities, you'll hit the ground running and start adding value to your new team immediately! 

    ASPIRE for Graduate Students

    ASPIRE is a professional skills development training program for graduate students and postdoctoral fellows at Laurier. ASPIRE provides a framework to help you craft an individualized, extracurricular learning plan tailored to your professional journey and entry to the workplace.

    Specialized Sessions

    We also offer tailored workshops and events for specific academic disciplines, career fields, first-year students and distinct student groups. 

    Resumé Writing

    Elevate your resumé: connect your skills to success!

    In today's competitive job market, your resumé needs to do more than just list your experiences, it needs to stand out instantly. Join us for a workshop where you'll discover how to tailor your resumé for every opportunity, whether it's a part-time role, a summer job, or your dream career after graduation. From designing an eye-catching layout to showcasing your most relevant skills, we'll equip you with the tools to create a resumé that stands out and gets noticed.

    Bring your resumé along and start enhancing it during the workshop.

    Cover Letter Writing

    Unlock your potential: craft cover letters that highlight your unique fit and leave a lasting impression.

    A well-crafted cover letter can be your key to unlocking opportunities in a competitive job market. It's not just an add-on; it's your moment to shine and demonstrate why you're the ideal candidate. In this workshop, we'll guide you through the process of creating cover letters that showcase your unique qualifications and make a powerful connection between your experience and the employer's needs. From choosing the right layout to optimizing the content, you'll gain the confidence to include a cover letter with your resumé that enhances every job application - unlocking the potential for success.

    Job Search Strategies

    Harness the power of the hidden job market!

    In this workshop, you'll discover strategic techniques and savvy networking approaches to tap into job opportunities before they're widely known. Leave equipped with valuable resources and tips to supercharge your job search! 

    Interview Success

    Be interview-ready before you even land the interview! 

    This workshop is your ultimate guide to mastering the job interview, packed with tips and tools to ensure you're ready and confident. We'll show you how to highlight your strongest qualifications, craft effective responses to common interview questions, and share stress-management techniques so you can perform at your best. Don't leave your interview to chance - be prepared, take an active role, and demonstrate your readiness and genuine interest in the role.

    Unlock Your Competencies

    Discover your knowledge, skills and attributes.

    Do you know what competencies you have and what employers are seeking? Align your competencies with your career development goals in this hands-on workshop. You will be able to identify and assess your personal competencies, identify areas for development and learn how to showcase the value of your experiences to an employer.

    AI as Your Career Coach: Enhancing Job Search and Professional Skills

    This workshop equips you with the knowledge to determine your first actionable step in the job search process, utilizing AI technologies. This approach ensures you can confidently embark on your job search journey, equipped with a clear starting point and a roadmap for success.

    LinkedIn

    Transform your LinkedIn profile into a powerful career-boosting tool. 

    Join us to discover tips on optimizing your privacy settings, making impactful connections, and leveraging LinkedIn's features to supercharge your job search. Make the most of LinkedIn by expanding and nurturing your professional network, turning connections into valuable relationships.

    Ready to take action? Learn how to build your profile or revamp your profile in real-time during the session, leaving with a LinkedIn presence that sets you apart, broadens your network, and propels your career forward!

    Network With Confidence

    Networking is your gateway to new opportunities, and it can be both exciting and rewarding!

    In this workshop, you'll learn how to confidently navigate networking events and recruiting sessions, transforming each encounter into a valuable connection. We'll review the essentials of a high-quality elevator pitch to ensure you make a powerful first impression. Plus, you'll learn how to leverage informational interviews to expand your network effectively. With these game-changing tips, you'll be ready to seize any opportunity and make every connection count!

    Job Fair Boot Camp 

    The Career Centre hosts the P4E Job Fair every September and February. To help you prepare for the event, attend the Job Fair Boot Camp and walk away equipped with your personalized Job Fair Action Plan. We will cover topics such as goal setting, employer research, resumé/networking card, attire, and elevator pitch (a brief introduction about yourself). You will also have a chance to practice various networking techniques and how to frame questions to impress employers. 

    Further Education Planning Workshops

    These workshops are for students considering furthering their education beyond their degree at Laurier. Learn about admission requirements and application procedures for several types of post-degree programs, and how the Career Centre can help you with the application process. For example, applying to graduate school, teacher education programs, or law school.

    Obtain your Certificates

    Register for workshops in Navigator. As you attend sessions, Navigator will track the certificate requirements. Certificate components can be completed at any time during your studies at Laurier. 

    To view your certificate status: 

    1. Log in to Navigator.
    2. Select Certificate Programs > Enroll or select the certificate to view requirements. 
    3. Once you have attended the required workshops and completed relevent reflection questions, open the purple action circle and print your certificate.

    Completed certificates will automatically appear on your Laurier Experience Record.
