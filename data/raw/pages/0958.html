    Zoom

    Zoom allows you to host online meetings and collaborate on group projects virtually, using your phone or computer.

    Log in to Laurier Zoom Account

    1. Go to zoom.wlu.ca.
    2. Select Sign In.
    3. Enter your Laurier username and password.

    Installation Instructions

    To install and log in to the Zoom Desktop Application:

    1. Go to the Zoom Download Centre.
    2. Select Download under "Zoom Desktop Client."
    3. After the download completes, launch the Zoom application.
    4. Select Sign In.
    5. Select SSO.
    6. Enter wilfrid-laurier and select Continue.
    7. Enter your Laurier username and password.
    8. Select Launch Zoom.

    To install and log in to the Zoom mobile application:

    1. Go to your device's app store (e.g. AppStore for iOS or Google Playstore for Android). 
    2. Search for Zoom and download.
    3. After the download completes, launch the Zoom application. 
    4. Select Sign In. 
    5. Select Sign In with SSO. 
    6. Enter wilfrid-laurier as the company domain and select Continue.
    7. Enter your Laurier username and password.
    8. Select Launch Zoom.

    Resources and Guides

    For more instructions on using Zoom including how to be a host or participant in a meeting, consult Zoom's Getting started guide for new users.
