    Laurier Fall Orientation:
    Milton Undergraduate Schedule

    Get ready to kick off your journey as a Golden Hawk at Laurier’s Milton campus!

    Milton Orientation takes place over two days, Wednesday, Sept. 9, and Thursday, Sept. 10. It is designed to help you feel confident, connected, and prepared for your first year at Laurier. Throughout Orientation, you’ll have the opportunity to meet your peers, build friendships, and socialize in a welcoming, supportive environment as you begin to build your community.

    On this page, you’ll find detailed information about Orientation activities focused on setting you up for success, both academically and personally. You’ll participate in sessions that introduce effective strategies for academic success, connect you with faculty and staff, and highlight the many supports and resources available to you at Laurier Milton.

    We can’t wait to welcome you to campus!

    Event Details

    Kick off your Laurier Milton experience by checking in, grabbing your Orientation kit and OneCard, and enjoying a light continental breakfast. It’s the perfect opportunity to meet new people, settle in, and start building connections.

     Where: Main Entrance and Milton Academic Centre (MAC) Lobby
     When: 9 to 10 a.m.
     Host: The Student Experience Team

    Led by Orientation Volunteers and Off-Campus Advisors (OCAs), these interactive activities are designed to help you connect with other students in your program and across campus. Expect a fun, welcoming environment where starting conversations feels easy.

     Where: Computer Science & Core Science - MAC 137; Business Technology Management - MAC128; Psychology and General Bachelor of Arts - MAC121
     When: 10 to 10:30 a.m.
     Host: The Student Experience Team

    Join university leaders and Your Students’ Union for an inspiring welcome to Laurier Milton and the broader Laurier community. Get a sense of what’s ahead and what it means to be part of this vibrant campus.

     Where: MAC137 | Milton Academic Centre
     When: 10:30 to 11 a.m.
     Host: The Student Experience Team

    We know that the first day of classes is an exciting milestone in your Golden Hawk journey, and being prepared will help you make the most of your first day!

    This session will walk you through everything you need to know about your first day of classes, including what to expect from your first lecture(s), how to navigate MyLearningSpace effectively, and why your syllabus is the most important resource for each course!

     Where: MAC137 | Milton Academic Centre
     When: 11 a.m. to Noon
     Host: The Laurier 101 Team

    Get to know your future classmates, professors, and program community in a relaxed and welcoming environment. Make new connections, hear from the people who will be teaching and supporting you, and start building your Laurier network before classes begin.

     Where: Computer Science & Core Science - MAC 137; Business Technology Management - MAC128; Psychology and General Bachelor of Arts - MAC121
     When: Noon to 12:30 p.m.

    Enjoy a complimentary lunch from a food truck while continuing conversations with your peers. Whether you’re relaxing or jumping into more conversations, this is your time to recharge and keep building your network.

     Where: Outside the Main Entrance
     When: 12:30 p.m. to 1:30 p.m.
     Host: The Student Experience Team

    Studying and learning at university is different. Whether you are coming from high school or college or are coming back to academics after some time away, you’ll likely find that you need to upgrade or adapt your academic skills.

    Join us for a review of the academic differences between high school and university and an overview of the study strategies that can help you make the most of your study hours.

     Where: MAC137 | Milton Academic Centre
     When: 1:30 to 2:30 p.m.
     Host: The Laurier 101 Team

    Break the ice, spark some conversations, and start building your Laurier community!

    Through a fun and interactive game of People Bingo, you'll meet fellow students, discover shared interests and experiences, and make connections with the people you'll be learning alongside throughout your first year at Laurier.

     Where: MAC137 and MAC Lobby | Milton Academic Centre
     When: 2:30 p.m. to 3 p.m.
     Host: The Student Experience Team

    Join a panel of upper-year students as they share their experiences getting involved on campus and making the most of their time at Laurier. Learn about clubs, leadership opportunities, events, and campus life, and get answers to your questions from students who have been in your shoes. Discover how getting involved can help you build connections, develop new skills, and create a meaningful university experience.

     Where: MAC137 | Milton Academic Centre
     When: 3 to 4 p.m.
     Host: The Student Experience Team

    Wrap up the day with a social planned by your LOCUS OCAs. Through fun and engaging activities, you’ll have even more chances to connect, unwind, and start forming lasting friendships.

     Where: TBD
     When: 4 to 6:30 p.m.
     Host: LOCUS Team
